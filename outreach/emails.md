# 外联邮件模板(2026-09-06)

写给真人看的信,不是群发模板。**每封至少改两处**:开头点名你读过他们哪篇文章、
以及你的站为什么适合放进那一篇。站长每天收一堆群发,只有看得出你真读过的才会回。

发信地址建议用 readmandarin.com 的域名邮箱或 Fei 本人的常用邮箱,不要用一次性邮箱。

## 模板 A · 给资源评测站(Hacking Chinese / All Language Resources / HSKStory 等)

> **Subject:** Free HSK graded reading site — 415 original texts, might fit your reading resources list

Hi <名字>,

I've been reading <他们的具体某篇文章标题> — <一句真实的感想或你从中学到的东西>.

I run a small free site that might belong on that list: **readmandarin.com**. It's 415
original graded readings across HSK 1–6, and everything is free with no signup and no ads.

What makes it different from most of the reading sites on those roundups:

- Every text is **written from scratch** for its HSK level, not adapted from news or textbooks
- Pinyin over every character, tap any word for a definition, audio for every sentence
- Each reading has a 3-question comprehension check and a short grammar note
- Roughly 100 of the readings are built around the phrases learners actually search for —
  "how to say thank you in Chinese", "where is the bathroom in Chinese", and so on

I'm a Mandarin teacher (I teach one-on-one on Preply) and I wrote all of it myself, so
I'm happy to answer anything about the content or the level grading.

No worries at all if it's not a fit — I'll keep reading either way.
