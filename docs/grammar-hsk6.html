A……，B 则……

Literary 则 contrasts two parallel clauses ('whereas; then'): 火太猛会烧干，火太小则煮不熟。

南方多雨，北方则干燥。— The south is rainy, whereas the north is dry.
Seen in 中庸之道
A…，B 则…

Formal 则 marks a contrast between two subjects: 'A does X, while B does Y'. 例：有的孩子马上吃了糖，有的则熬过了等待。

南方多雨，北方则干燥。— The south gets plenty of rain, while the north is dry.
Seen in 延迟满足
Literary 者

Classical 者 turns a verb or phrase into 'one who…', common in proverbs and formal writing. 例：知足者富 = 知足的人富有。

仁者爱人。— The benevolent one loves others.
Seen in 知足常乐
V + 得 + complement

A degree complement paints how far the action goes — here the pressure is so heavy one cannot breathe.

高昂的房价压得年轻人喘不过气来。
Seen in 大城市病
……不在于……，而在于……

'It does not lie in A, but rather in B' — used to redirect the reader's attention from a surface-level factor to the real, deeper factor that actually matters. 例：学习的效果不在于时间的长短，而在于是否用心。

两者的根本区别不在于失败的次数多寡，而在于是否建立起系统的复盘机制。 — The fundamental difference between the two lies not in how many times one fails, but in whether one has built a systematic habit of review.
Seen in 失败的价值
…不在于…，而在于…

Rejects one explanation in favor of another, both introduced by 在于 ('lies in'): 'it doesn't lie in A, but in B'. Common for making a distinction between surface and essence. 例：成功的关键不在于起点，而在于坚持。

仪式感的本质，不在于形式本身有多讲究，而在于它能否唤醒我们对当下的感知。 — The essence of ritual does not lie in how elaborate the form is, but in whether it can awaken our awareness of the present.
Seen in 仪式感的意义
…之所以…，是因为…

States the result first, then explains the cause: 'the reason A is precious/true is that B'. Common in reflective, analytical writing. 例：这段友情之所以珍贵，是因为它经受住了时间的考验。

个人记录之所以珍贵，是因为它保留了宏大叙事所无法容纳的犹豫、恐惧与盼望。 — Personal records are precious precisely because they preserve what grand narratives have no room to hold.
Seen in 历史的温度
…之所以…，是因为… — 'the reason X is the case is because Y'

A formal cause-explanation structure: 之所以 marks the effect first, and 是因为 supplies the underlying cause.

这种设定之所以引发广泛讨论，是因为它把国际关系、博弈论与人性的幽暗面编织进了一个引人入胜的故事。 — This premise sparked such widespread discussion because it weaves international relations, game theory, and the dark side of human nature into a captivating story.
Seen in 《三体》现象
…之所以…，除了…之外，更在于…

A layered explanation pattern: states a result, dismisses an obvious cause as merely partial ('besides... '), then points to the deeper reason ('lies even more in...'). 例：这部作品之所以成功，除了制作精良之外，更在于它触动了观众的共鸣。

这类小说之所以能吸引外国读者，除了情节紧凑之外，更在于它提供了一套完全陌生的世界观。 — What draws foreign readers is not just the compact plots, but even more the entirely unfamiliar worldview it offers.
Seen in 网络文学出海
…从来不只是…，而是…

Rejects a narrow, single-cause explanation in favor of a broader one: 'has never been merely A, but rather B'. Common in essays making a deeper, reflective point. 例：真正的成功从来不只是运气，而是长期积累的结果。

决定人类能否延续下去的，从来不只是技术本身，而是我们运用技术的智慧。 — What determines whether humanity can carry on has never been technology itself alone, but the wisdom with which we wield it.
Seen in 人类的未来
…而…，却…

Sets up a contrast between two clauses ('whereas... yet...'), often pairing a formal register with an unexpected turn. 例：白天他沉默寡言，而深夜，却常常提笔写信。

正史记录的往往是帝王将相的功过，而普通人的日常生活常常被一笔带过。 — Official history tends to record the achievements and faults of emperors and ministers, while ordinary people's daily lives are often mentioned only in passing.
Seen in 历史的温度
…，以 + verb

In formal writing, 以 before a verb means 'in order to; so as to', linking an action to its purpose. 例：公司降低价格，以吸引更多顾客。

盟军想给轰炸机加装装甲，以减少损失。 — The Allies wanted to add armor to their bombers in order to reduce losses.
Seen in 幸存者偏差
…，即…

In formal writing, 即 means 'that is; namely', introducing a definition or restatement of the term just mentioned, much like 'i.e.' in English.

心理学家把答案归结为“内驱力”，即发自内心、无须外部奖励的动机。 — Psychologists boil the answer down to "intrinsic drive," that is, motivation that comes from within and needs no external reward.
Seen in 内驱力
一旦……便……

'once... then...' — a formal conditional often signaling serious or irreversible consequences.

数据一旦泄露便覆水难收。
Seen in 科技伦理
一旦……，便/就……

'Once/the moment A happens, then B' — 便 is the literary equivalent of 就. 一旦回报来得太慢，我们便半途而废。

一旦下定决心，便不要轻易放弃。— Once you have made up your mind, do not give up lightly.
Seen in 愚公移山与现代人
一旦…就…

Expresses 'once X happens, then Y follows' — a conditional trigger with a resulting, often serious, consequence. E.g. 一旦下定决心，就不要轻易放弃 (once you've made up your mind, don't give up easily).

一旦过分依赖目击者信誓旦旦的证词，就可能酿成冤假错案。
Seen in 记忆的重构性
一旦…，就…

'Once/as soon as A happens, B follows' — presents B as the immediate, almost automatic consequence of A. 例：一旦养成习惯，就很难改掉。

一旦你尝试把母语里最喜欢的一句诗译成汉语，就会立刻体会到译者的甘苦。 — The moment you try translating your favorite line of poetry into Chinese, you will immediately taste the translator's joys and hardships.
Seen in 翻译的艺术深度工作基因编辑之争怀旧为何流行
一旦…，意味着… — 'once X happens, it means Y'

A formal conditional pattern: 一旦 introduces a hypothetical trigger, and the clause that follows spells out its significant consequence.

这个结果一旦成立，意味着人类在该地区的活动历史，需要被重新书写。 — If this result holds up, it would mean that the history of human activity in the region needs to be rewritten.
Seen in 考古新发现
不仅……，也…… — not only …, but also …

Adds a second, often more surprising, consequence to the first. In written style 也 may be replaced by 还 or 甚至 for a stronger climb.

这项措施不仅降低了成本，也改善了工作环境。 — The measure not only lowered costs but also improved the working environment.
Seen in 生物多样性
不仅…，更… — 'not only X, but even more so Y'

An escalating pattern that builds from an already-significant point to an even more significant one, often used in concluding remarks.

《三体》的成功，不仅是一部作品的胜利，更标志着中国科幻真正走入了世界文学的对话。 — The success of The Three-Body Problem is not just the triumph of a single work, but a sign that Chinese science fiction has truly entered the conversation of world literature.
Seen in 《三体》现象
不仅…，还…

'Not only A, but also B' — stacks a second, often weightier consequence on top of the first; the written cousin of 不但…而且…. 例：熬夜不仅伤身体，还影响第二天的效率。

锋芒毕露不仅显得缺乏修养，还容易招来嫉妒。 — Flaunting your brilliance not only looks uncultivated but also easily attracts jealousy.
Seen in 谦虚的文化根源
不在……,而在……

'lies not in..., but in...' — rejects a false cause and points to the true one.

问题不在工具,而在使用工具的人。
Seen in 科技伦理
不在于…，而在于…

'It lies not in A, but in B' — a formal way to redirect the reader from a false focus to the real one, common in argumentative essays. 例：成功不在于速度，而在于方向。

争论的核心并不在于技术本身，而在于人类是否有资格改写生命的说明书。 — The core of the debate lies not in the technology itself but in whether humans have the standing to rewrite the manual of life.
Seen in 基因编辑之争
不在于…，而在于… — 'does not lie in A, but in B'

A formal pattern for correcting where the real significance of something lies, often used to conclude an essay's argument.

数字货币真正的价值，不在于它能不能一夜暴富，而在于它提供了一种可行的技术方案。 — The true value of digital currency lies not in getting rich overnight, but in offering a workable technical solution.
Seen in 数字货币
不在于…，而在于… — 'lies not in A, but in B'

A formal contrastive pattern used to redirect attention from an obvious feature to the deeper, more essential one.

这部小说最令人震撼之处，不在于宏大的星际战争场面，而在于它提出的“黑暗森林”这一冷峻假说。 — What is most striking about the novel is not its grand scenes of interstellar warfare, but the grim hypothesis it proposes.
Seen in 《三体》现象
不失为…… — may still be counted as

A concessive judgment: the thing is not perfect, yet it still qualifies as something worthwhile. Almost always followed by 一 + measure word + noun.

在没有更好办法的情况下，这不失为一个折中的选择。 — With no better option available, this may still count as a reasonable compromise.
Seen in 博物馆热
不妨

'might as well; there is no harm in' — politely suggests trying something. 不妨学学那位老人。

你不妨换个角度想想。— You might as well look at it from another angle.
Seen in 塞翁失马新解
不妨 + verb

'Might as well; there is no harm in doing…' — a gentle suggestion that the action costs little and may help. 例：如果拿不定主意，不妨先问问专业人士。

我们不妨偶尔停下来，看看事情本来想往哪个方向生长。 — We might as well stop now and then and see which way things wanted to grow.
Seen in 道法自然快与慢的辩证
不是 A,而是 B — not A, but rather B

Corrects an assumption by replacing it: 靠的不是热情,而是积累. 而是 must introduce the true statement.

学好中文靠的不是天赋,而是坚持。 — Mastering Chinese relies not on talent, but on persistence.
Seen in 坚持的力量
不是……，而是……

'not A, but rather B' — corrects a misconception; the literary twin is 并非……而是……。中庸不是不敢走极端，而是选择克制。

他沉默不是因为无话可说，而是因为不屑争辩。— His silence was not for lack of words, but from disdain for arguing.
Seen in 中庸之道
不是…，而是…

Corrects an assumption by negating one option and affirming another: 'it is not A, but rather B'. Common for reframing who or what is really responsible. 例：改变现状的，不是运气，而是坚持。

把这些故事带到英语世界的，不是出版社，而是一群业余译者自发组成的翻译社群。 — It was not publishing houses that carried these stories into the English-speaking world, but translation communities formed by amateur translators.
Seen in 网络文学出海
不能因为…就… — 'one must not, just because of X, hastily do Y'

A cautionary pattern warning against jumping to conclusions based on insufficient or single-source evidence.

不能因为一次测年就急于下定论。 — One dating result alone should not lead to a hasty conclusion.
Seen in 考古新发现
与其A，不如B

'Rather than A, it is better to B' — weighs two options and prefers the second. 与其为年龄焦虑，不如默默深耕。

与其抱怨，不如行动。— Rather than complain, it is better to act.
Seen in 大器晚成
与其……不如……

Expresses a preference between two options, meaning 'rather than X, it would be better to Y', e.g. 与其抱怨，不如行动 (Rather than complain, it's better to act).

因此，与其把算法视为洪水猛兽，不如在设计之初就有意识地为偶然留出余地，让人重新拥有迷路的自由。
Seen in 算法与偶然性孤独与独处之辨
与其……，不如……

'Rather than A, it would be better to B' — used to weigh two options and argue that the second is the wiser or more worthwhile course of action. 例：与其抱怨环境，不如努力改变自己。

与其空谈失败的意义，不如养成对失败进行系统反思的习惯。 — Rather than talking emptily about the meaning of failure, it is better to cultivate a habit of systematically reflecting on it.
Seen in 失败的价值
与其……，不如…… — rather than A, better B

Weighs two options and rejects the first: 'rather than doing A, it is better to do B'. Common in written argument when the author wants to recommend a more active alternative.

与其抱怨环境，不如先把手头的事做好。 — Rather than complain about your circumstances, it is better to first do well the task in front of you.
Seen in 敦煌的守护者
与其说……，不如说…… — rather than saying A, say B

Reframes a claim instead of simply denying it: the speaker accepts that something happened but proposes a more accurate description of it.

这与其说是失败，不如说是一次代价高昂的实验。 — Rather than call it a failure, call it an expensive experiment.
Seen in 科幻的黄金时代
与其说…，不如说…

'It is not so much A as B' — weighs two descriptions and declares the second more accurate. A staple of essay-style reflection. 例：与其说是运气，不如说是坚持。

与其说我们在学习一门外语，不如说我们在学习另一种打量世界的目光。 — Learning a foreign language is not so much acquiring words as acquiring another way of looking at the world.
Seen in 语言与思维
与此同时

'At the same time; meanwhile' — a formal connector that adds a second, parallel statement to the previous sentence.

经济在增长，与此同时，环境问题也日益严重。— The economy is growing; at the same time, environmental problems grow more serious by the day.
Seen in 匠人精神
之所以……，在于/是因为…… — the reason why … is that

Puts the result first and the cause second, giving the explanation extra weight; typical of analytical and argumentative writing.

他之所以坚持下来，是因为看到了别人看不到的意义。 — The reason he kept going is that he saw a meaning others could not see.
Seen in 气候变化与责任
也不是没有…（双重否定）

A double negative that concedes a point mildly: 'it is not that there is no…', i.e. there is some. Softer and more balanced than a flat affirmative. 例：这个计划也不是没有风险，只是风险可以控制。

怀疑者的担忧也不是没有道理。 — The skeptics' worries are not without foundation either.
Seen in 太空探索值得吗
令人 + verb/adj

'makes one (feel)...' — a written pattern forming expressions like 令人振奋 (inspiring) and 令人担忧 (worrying).

前景令人振奋。
Seen in 科技伦理
以……为…… — take … as …

A compact literary frame meaning 'take A as B', used for standards, goals and principles: 以质量为标准, 以人为本.

这家公司一向以质量为生命。 — This company has always treated quality as its lifeblood.
Seen in 生物多样性
以……的面目出现

'to appear in the guise of…' — literary 以 here means '用/带着'. 真正的丰盛，往往以极简的面目出现。

机会常常以困难的面目出现。— Opportunity often shows up wearing the face of difficulty.
Seen in 大道至简
以…为例 — 'taking X as an example'

A formal way to introduce a concrete example supporting the preceding argument, common in essays and reports.

以某国产运动品牌为例，它把敦煌壁画的色彩与现代运动鞋的剪裁结合起来。 — Take a domestic sportswear brand as an example: it combines the colors of Dunhuang murals with the cut of modern sneakers.
Seen in 国潮兴起
使 + sb + verb

'to cause/make somebody do something' — a formal causative common in written argumentation.

使人们不知不觉陷入“信息茧房”。
Seen in 媒介素养
依我看

'in my view' — introduces the writer's own opinion, often before a counter-argument.

依我看,这种说法未免言过其实。
Seen in 全球化的退潮
倘若……，…… — supposing that; if

A formal written equivalent of 如果, used to introduce a hypothetical the author is about to reject or warn against.

倘若没有及时提醒，后果不堪设想。 — Had no timely warning been given, the consequences would be unthinkable.
Seen in 气候变化与责任
值得注意的是，…

A formal opener meaning 'it's worth noting that...', used to introduce a nuance or complication after making a point. 例：值得注意的是，压力并非总是坏事。

值得注意的是，适度的怀旧并非坏事：研究显示，它能增强归属感，缓解孤独情绪。 — Notably, moderate nostalgia is not actually a bad thing: research shows it can strengthen belonging and relieve loneliness.
Seen in 怀旧为何流行
值得警惕的是… — 'what deserves caution is...'

A formal essay opener used to flag a caveat or risk the reader should not overlook, often before criticizing a common misconception.

值得警惕的是，媒体常常把量子计算描绘成“无所不能”的黑科技。 — What deserves caution is that the media often portrays quantum computing as an 'omnipotent' high technology.
Seen in 量子计算浅谈
关键不在于…，而在于… — 'the key lies not in A, but in B'

Used to redirect the reader's attention from a superficial factor to the real determining one.

国潮能否走远，关键不在于图案是否传统，而在于设计本身能不能经得起审美的考验。 — Whether Guochao can last depends not on whether the pattern is traditional, but on whether the design itself can stand up to aesthetic scrutiny.
Seen in 国潮兴起
反之

'Conversely; on the other hand' — a formal connector that introduces the opposite case of what was just said. 例：每天读书使人进步；反之，每天熬夜刷手机透支健康。

多运动有益健康；反之，久坐不动则伤害身体。— Exercise benefits health; conversely, sitting still all day harms the body.
Seen in 习惯的复利
反观……

Literary 'turning to look at… (by contrast)' — introduces a contrasting subject right after an example. 反观我们的生活，却在不断做加法。

他天天锻炼；反观我，连楼都懒得下。— He works out every day; I, by contrast, can't even be bothered to go downstairs.
Seen in 大道至简
只有…，才…

'Only if A, then B' — states a necessary condition. The clause after 才 cannot happen unless the 只有 condition is met.

只有把沉默的证据也纳入视野，我们的判断才可能接近真相。 — Only when the silent evidence is also brought into view can our judgments come close to the truth.
Seen in 幸存者偏差
可见…

A formal connector meaning 'it is evident that...' or 'this shows that...', used to draw a conclusion from preceding evidence. 例：她连续三次拒绝邀请，可见她并不感兴趣。

可见，类型文学的跨文化接受，靠的往往不是官方推广，而是读者之间最朴素的口耳相传。 — It is evident that the cross-cultural reception of genre literature usually depends on plain word of mouth rather than official promotion.
Seen in 网络文学出海
哪怕…，也…

'Even if A (seems minor), B still holds' — used to emphasize that even a small cause has a real, disproportionate effect. 例：哪怕只有一分钟，也值得认真对待。

哪怕是点燃一支蜡烛这样微小的动作，也能显著提升人们对接下来这顿饭的满足感。 — Even a tiny action like lighting a single candle can significantly boost people's satisfaction with the meal that follows.
Seen in 仪式感的意义
唯有……才……

'only by... can...' — a formal conditional stating the sole path to a result.

唯有促进区域协调发展,人们才能真正拥有选择的自由。
Seen in 大城市病
固然…，但(也)…

A formal concessive pattern: 'admittedly / it is true that A, but B'. It concedes a point before pivoting to the speaker's real emphasis. 例：这个办法固然省钱，但风险不小。

快固然带来了效率，但也让许多人失去了从容思考的空间。 — Speed has admittedly brought efficiency, but it has also cost many people the room to think calmly.
Seen in 快与慢的辩证
固然…，但…

A concessive pivot: 'admittedly A, but B'. It grants the first point genuine validity before asserting that the second matters more. 例：钱固然重要，但健康更重要。

听懂委婉语固然重要，但更重要的是学会使用它。 — Understanding euphemisms is admittedly important, but more important still is learning to use them.
Seen in 委婉语碎片化阅读之弊
固然…，却/但…

'Admittedly A, but B' — 固然 concedes a point that is true before the writer pivots to the more important qualification. Common in argumentative writing. 例：这个办法固然省钱，却太费时间。

这种做法固然有利于维持表面的和谐，却可能让真正的问题被一再拖延。 — Admittedly this practice helps maintain surface harmony, but it may leave the real problems postponed again and again.
Seen in 儒家与现代职场
在……的同时

Indicates that two actions or states occur simultaneously, often to set up a contrast between them, e.g. 他在学习的同时也在打工 (While studying, he also works part-time).

然而，算法在迎合既有偏好的同时，也悄然压缩了偶然存在的空间，使人与陌生领域擦肩而过的机会越来越少。
Seen in 算法与偶然性
姑且不论…… — leaving aside the question of…

A formal way to set one issue aside so the argument can move on, while hinting that the issue is debatable. Usually followed by a clause introduced by 总/也/仍.

姑且不论成本，这个方案在时间上也来不及。 — Leaving cost aside, this plan is also impossible in terms of time.
Seen in 博物馆热
宁可…，也不…

'Would rather A than B' — 宁可 marks the option the speaker accepts (often an unpleasant one), and 也不 marks the option rejected even more strongly. 例：他宁可自己吃亏，也不愿意跟人争。

不少员工在会议上宁可保持沉默，也不愿当众反驳领导。 — Plenty of employees would rather keep silent in a meeting than contradict the boss in public.
Seen in 儒家与现代职场
对…而言

A formal equivalent of 对…来说: 'as far as X is concerned; for X'. It frames the following judgment from a particular person's perspective.

这件事对你而言，究竟为什么值得做？ — Why, for you, is this thing actually worth doing?
Seen in 内驱力
并不在于…，而在于…

A formal way to correct an assumption about where the essence of something lies: 'lies not in A, but in B'. 并不 makes the denial emphatic.

这场运动的意义并不在于反对速度本身，而在于提醒我们：生活的质量不能完全由速度来衡量。 — The point of the movement was not to oppose speed itself, but to remind us that the quality of life cannot be measured by speed alone.
Seen in 快与慢的辩证
并不是…，而是…

An emphatic correction: 'it is by no means A; rather, it is B'. 并 strengthens the negation, often countering what the reader might assume.

这并不是车变多了，而是你的注意力被“确认偏差”牵引。 — It is not that there are more of those cars; rather, your attention is being pulled by confirmation bias.
Seen in 认知偏差道法自然
并未…… — has not at all

The literary counterpart of 没有, used to reject an assumption emphatically; 并 adds the force of 'contrary to what you may think'. Often paired with 只是 to state what did happen.

问题并未解决，只是暂时被掩盖了。 — The problem has not been solved; it has merely been covered up for now.
Seen in 科幻的黄金时代
并非

Formal, emphatic negation: 'by no means is / is not at all', typically correcting a likely misreading; 并 reinforces 非 (the literary 不是). 例：成功并非偶然。

这并非意味着语言是一座监狱。 — This by no means implies that language is a prison.
Seen in 语言与思维
并非……,而是……

'is by no means..., but rather...' — a formal written pattern that rejects one description and asserts another.

集体与个人并非水火不容,而是相辅相成的关系。
Seen in 集体与个人
并非……，而是……

'is by no means A, but rather B' — 并非 is the formal, literary version of 不是, often correcting an assumption.

他并非天赋异禀，而是几十年如一日地磨炼。— He was by no means born gifted; rather, he trained relentlessly for decades.
Seen in 大器晚成
并非……，而是…… — not A, but rather B

A formal, emphatic correction of a wrong assumption. 并非 is stronger and more literary than 不是, and 而是 introduces the accurate statement.

他并非不愿意帮忙，而是实在抽不出时间。 — It is not that he is unwilling to help; he genuinely cannot find the time.
Seen in 敦煌的守护者
并非…，而是…

An emphatic corrective pair: 'is by no means A; rather, it is B'. The literary 非 plus reinforcing 并 makes the correction feel authoritative. 例：沉默并非软弱，而是一种修养。

这种模糊并非缺陷，而是一种独特的美。 — This vagueness is by no means a defect but a beauty all its own.
Seen in 汉语的模糊之美匠人精神怀旧为何流行
并非…，而是… — 'it is by no means A; rather, it is B'

A formal, emphatic correction pattern common in essays: 并非 negates the assumption before 而是 supplies the real explanation.

量子计算并非要取代传统电脑，而是为特定领域提供一种全新的计算思路。 — Quantum computing is not meant to replace traditional computers, but rather to offer a brand-new computational approach for specific fields.
Seen in 量子计算浅谈
应当是…，而不是… — 'it should be A, rather than B'

A formal prescriptive pattern often used at the end of an essay to state what the ideal form of something ought to be.

真正的国潮，应当是传统美学与当代生活方式的自然融合，而不是一场短暂的营销秀。 — True Guochao should be a natural fusion of traditional aesthetics and contemporary lifestyle, not a short-lived marketing show.
Seen in 国潮兴起
建立在…之上 — 'to be built upon...'

A formal pattern describing the foundation something rests on, often used for abstract things like trust, systems, or theories.

传统货币的信任建立在国家信用之上。 — Trust in traditional currency is built on national credit.
Seen in 数字货币
归根结底……

'in the final analysis; at bottom' — introduces the essential underlying point after weighing complicating factors, often used to deliver a concluding judgment.

归根结底，独处与孤独之别，不在形式而在心境。
Seen in 孤独与独处之辨
恰恰在于

'lies precisely in' — pinpoints the essence of something, often after words like 可贵、问题、区别. 愚公的可贵，恰恰在于他不问结果。

两者的区别恰恰在于态度。— The difference between the two lies precisely in attitude.
Seen in 愚公移山与现代人
愈发

'increasingly; all the more' — a written synonym of 越来越.

社会共识愈发难以形成。
Seen in 媒介素养
所谓…，是指…

A formal defining pattern: 'what is called A refers to B'. Essays use it to introduce and then define a technical term. 例：所谓内卷，是指无意义的过度竞争。

所谓认知偏差，是指思维在处理信息时出现的系统性偏离。 — A cognitive bias is a systematic deviation that occurs when the mind processes information.
Seen in 认知偏差
把 A 当成 B

'To treat A as B' — 把 fronts the object, 当成 (also 当作) states the identity assigned to it, often a mistaken one. 例：别把客气话当成承诺。

把专注当成又一项必须完成的任务，本身就违背了禅的本意。 — Treating concentration as yet another task to be completed already goes against the point of Zen.
Seen in 禅与生活
换言之 — 'in other words'

A formal connective used to restate the previous point more plainly or from a different angle; more literary than 也就是说.

换言之，信任的对象从“某个权威机构”变成了“一套公开透明、人人可查的算法规则”。 — In other words, the object of trust shifts from 'some authoritative institution' to 'a set of open, transparent algorithmic rules.'
Seen in 数字货币
无异于

'To be no different from; tantamount to' — a formal comparison used to condemn or dramatize: doing A is effectively doing B. 例：以碎片化阅读取代读书，无异于用零食代替正餐。

明知有错却不改，无异于自欺欺人。— Knowing you are wrong yet refusing to change is tantamount to deceiving yourself.
Seen in 碎片化阅读之弊
无论…，都…

'No matter / regardless of A, B holds.' 无论 is followed by a question word or paired alternatives like 好坏、多少. 例：无论好坏，都要多年以后才会显现。

无论遇到什么困难，他都不放弃。— No matter what difficulties he runs into, he never gives up.
Seen in 习惯的复利谦虚的文化根源
无论…，都… — 'no matter what..., always...'

Introduces a concessive clause: whatever the condition turns out to be, the outcome or lesson in the main clause still holds.

无论最终结论如何，这次发现都提醒我们：历史并非一成不变的定论。 — Whatever the final conclusion turns out to be, this discovery reminds us that history is not a fixed, unchanging verdict.
Seen in 考古新发现
无论是…，还是…

'No matter whether it is A or B' — lists alternative cases and asserts that the statement that follows holds for all of them.

无论是学习一门语言，还是坚持一项运动，凡由兴趣驱动的，反而细水长流。 — Whether it is learning a language or keeping up a sport, whatever is driven by interest tends to flow on steadily.
Seen in 内驱力
无非是…… — nothing more than

Downplays something as being merely one simple thing; often used to set up a rebuttal, as when the text says people assume guarding is 无非是 locking things away.

他们争论的无非是先后顺序。 — What they are arguing about is nothing more than the order of things.
Seen in 敦煌的守护者
既……也……

'both... and...' — links two parallel requirements or qualities in one sentence.

既要保障个人的自由与尊严,也要培养公民的责任感。
Seen in 集体与个人
既……，也…… — both … and

Joins two qualities or requirements that hold at the same time. Unlike 又……又……, it is more formal and often summarizes an argument.

这份工作既需要耐心，也需要判断力。 — This job calls for both patience and judgment.
Seen in 气候变化与责任
既…，也…

Presents two things that hold true at once, often two opposing sides of one thing: 'both... and also...'. 例：这份工作既能锻炼能力，也能积累人脉。

核能既能点亮万家灯火，也曾带来毁灭性的灾难。 — Nuclear energy can both light up the homes of ten thousand families and has once brought devastating disaster.
Seen in 人类的未来
既…，又…

'Both A and B' — coordinates two merits (or faults) of the same thing, giving the sentence a balanced, essayistic rhythm. 例：这个办法既省钱，又省时间。

一个“差不多”，既给了对方大致的预期，又为自己留了余地。 — A single "more or less" both gives the other person a rough expectation and leaves oneself some leeway.
Seen in 汉语的模糊之美
既可以…，也可以…

Presents two equally valid options: 'you can either A or B'. 既…也… coordinates the alternatives without ranking them. 例：周末既可以在家休息，也可以出去走走。

你既可以谦虚地回答“还差得远呢”，也可以微笑着说声“谢谢”。 — You can either reply modestly, "I still have a long way to go," or smile and say "thank you."
Seen in 谦虚的文化根源
既没有…，也没有…

'Neither A nor B' — the negative use of the 既…也… pair, listing two things that equally failed to happen. 例：那次会议既没有解决问题，也没有安排下一步。

他们既没有真正吃饭，也没有真正睡觉。 — They neither truly ate nor truly slept.
Seen in 禅与生活
是……,还是……?

'is it A, or is it B?' — an alternative question, here used rhetorically to close the essay.

是我们在使用媒介,还是媒介在塑造我们?
Seen in 媒介素养
更何况

'What is more; let alone' — adds a further, stronger reason after an argument has already been made, pushing the conclusion harder. 例：他连简单的句子都听不懂，更何况这样的长篇报告。

更何况，许多今天离不开的东西，都来自航天技术的意外收获。 — What is more, many things we cannot do without today came from the unexpected spin-offs of aerospace technology.
Seen in 太空探索值得吗
未免

'rather; a bit too' — politely signals that a statement or judgment goes too far.

这种说法未免言过其实。
Seen in 全球化的退潮
未免 + 太/过于 + adj.

'That is rather too…' — 未免 softens a criticism, marking a judgment as somewhat excessive. It comments on a situation, never on a request. 例：这样批评一个孩子，未免太严厉了。

把“道法自然”理解成什么都不做，未免过于消极。 — Reading 道法自然 as doing nothing at all is rather too passive.
Seen in 道法自然
未必

'Not necessarily' — a softer, more literary way to negate certainty than 不一定. 例：物质条件好了，幸福感却未必增长。

贵的东西未必适合你。— Expensive things do not necessarily suit you.
Seen in 知足常乐塞翁失马新解
殊不知

A literary connector meaning 'little do they know; what they fail to realize is'. It introduces a truth that contradicts the belief just stated.

有人得出“读书无用”的结论，殊不知这恰恰是被幸存者蒙蔽了双眼。 — Some conclude that education is useless, little knowing that this is precisely a case of being blinded by the survivors.
Seen in 幸存者偏差
每当…，总…

'Whenever A, B always happens' — 每当 marks a recurring occasion (more formal than 每次), and 总(会/是) states the regular outcome. 例：每当想起那段日子，他总是笑着摇头。

每当有火箭升空，社交媒体上总会出现同一个质疑。 — Whenever a rocket lifts off, the same challenge always appears on social media.
Seen in 太空探索值得吗
用……来 + verb

'to (verb) by means of...' — names the standard or tool used for an action.

城市的价值,究竟应该用高度来衡量,还是用居民的幸福感来衡量?
Seen in 大城市病
稍有…，就… — 'the slightest X will immediately Y'

Conveys extreme sensitivity or fragility: even a minimal trigger (稍有) is enough to bring about the consequence (就).

量子比特极易受到外界干扰，稍有风吹草动就会发生“退相干”。 — Qubits are extremely susceptible to outside interference; the slightest disturbance can cause 'decoherence.'
Seen in 量子计算浅谈
若…，难免…

A formal conditional with an unavoidable outcome: 'if A, then B can hardly be avoided'. 若 is the written equivalent of 如果; 难免 marks the result as natural and predictable. 例：若准备不足，难免出错。

初学者若把这些话全都当真，难免闹出误会。 — If beginners take all these phrases at face value, misunderstandings are inevitable.
Seen in 汉语的模糊之美
莫过于

A formal superlative: 'nothing surpasses…; the greatest/most typical is none other than…'. Often follows 最…的 to crown one example above all others. 例：人生最大的幸福莫过于家人平安。

最典型的莫过于年龄与死亡。 — The most typical cases are none other than age and death.
Seen in 委婉语
莫过于…… — nothing is more … than

Marks a superlative in written style: of all cases, this one is the most extreme. Typically follows a topic such as 最值得警惕的、最大的遗憾.

人生最大的遗憾，莫过于明知该做却迟迟不做。 — Life's greatest regret is nothing other than knowing what you ought to do and endlessly putting it off.
Seen in 博物馆热
被视为

'to be regarded as' — a formal passive structure very common in written Chinese.

全球化曾被视为不可逆转的历史潮流。
Seen in 全球化的退潮
被视为…… — to be regarded as

A written-style passive used for judgments and reputations; 视为 is more formal than 看作 and is common in commentary and academic prose.

他一直被视为这个领域最有希望的年轻人。 — He has long been regarded as the most promising young person in the field.
Seen in 科幻的黄金时代
越 A，就越 B

'The more A, the more B' — links two variables that rise together. Each 越 attaches directly to a verb or adjective. 例：他越解释，大家就越糊涂。

技术跑得越快，社会就越需要停下来。 — The faster technology runs, the more society needs to stop.
Seen in 基因编辑之争
越是…，(反而)越…

'The more A, the more B' — 越是 fronts the condition for emphasis, and 反而 adds a note of paradox when the result is the opposite of what one would expect.

越是无知的人，反而越自信。 — Paradoxically, the more ignorant a person is, the more confident they tend to be.
Seen in 认知偏差
越是…，越是…

An emphatic version of 越…越… : 'the more (it is) A, the more (it is) B', often used for general truths. 例：越是容易得到的快乐，越是转瞬即逝。

越是紧张，越是容易出错。— The more nervous you are, the more likely you are to make mistakes.
Seen in 延迟满足
难道……吗？ — surely you don't mean…?

A rhetorical question that expects the answer 'no'; the writer uses it to make an objection feel obvious rather than to ask for information.

难道我们要等到损失无法挽回时才行动吗？ — Are we really going to wait until the loss is beyond repair before acting?
Seen in 生物多样性
