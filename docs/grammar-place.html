Grammar by topic

Saying where something is and what there is

在 marks location and comes before the verb, not after it — the opposite of English word order.

在 + place + verb — where an action happensHSK 1

Where does the place go in a Chinese sentence?

In Chinese the place phrase comes before the verb: 在商店工作 (at-store work), the opposite order from English 'work at the store'.

我爸爸在商店工作。 — My dad works at a store.
Seen in 爸爸工作再见，老师下雨了我的房间 +2

的 — possessive/attributive particleHSK 1

How do you show possession in Chinese?

的 links a noun to what comes after it, like 's in English.

大商店的东西很多。 — The big store's things are a lot (the big store has a lot of things).
Seen in 大商店小商店妈妈的生日我的猫我的中国名字 +1

有 — there is / there areHSK 1

How do you say "have" in Chinese?

有 introduces what exists in a place: place + 有 + thing.

中国有很多人。 — China has many people.
Seen in 中国很大开车去北京我的钱

是 — A is BHSK 1

How do you say "is" in Chinese?

是 links two nouns: A 是 B means 'A is B'. Use it for identity, not for adjectives.

今天是妈妈的生日。 — Today is Mom's birthday.
Seen in 妈妈的生日自我介绍星期天

住在 + place — to live somewhereHSK 1

How do you say where you live in Chinese?

住 (to live) takes 在 plus a place right after it to say where you live.

我住在北京。 — I live in Beijing.
Seen in 中国很大我住在哪儿

在 + place (+ 上)HSK 1

How do you say where someone is in Chinese?

在 puts someone or something at a place, and 上 after the place means 'on/aboard': 在飞机上 = on the plane.

我现在在飞机上。 — I'm on the plane right now.
Seen in 在飞机上教室里

在 + verb（doing something right now）HSK 2

在 right before a verb shows the action is happening at this moment.

你在忙吗？— Are you busy right now?
Seen in 请进别玩手机了

How to use this page

These 7 patterns all do the same job in Chinese, so they are worth seeing side by side — the differences between them are easier to feel when they are next to each other than when they are spread across six HSK levels. They range from HSK 1–2.

Grammar sticks through reading, not through lists. Each entry links to the reading it was taken from. Skim the page, then go read one of them and come back when a sentence stops making sense.
