Chinese question words 疑问词

Chinese questions keep the word order of the answer. You put the question word where the answer will go and change nothing else: 你去哪儿？ — 我去北京。 Where are you going? — I'm going to Beijing. Here are the question words and particles, with questions from the readings.

Three ways to ask a question

  • Add 吗 to a statement for a yes/no question: 你忙。 → 你忙吗？
  • Say the verb twice with 不: 你是不是老师？ 你去不去？ Same meaning as 吗, a little more direct. Do not add 吗 as well.
  • Use a question word (below) in the place of the answer. Do not add 吗 to these either: 你叫什么？ not 你叫什么吗？

Question words and particles

WordMeaning and how to use itQuestions from the readings
什么shénmewhat
Put it where the answer goes: 你叫什么？ — 我叫小美。 Before a noun it means "what kind of": 什么书？
  • 你看，这是什么？Look, what is this?HSK 1 · 这是什么
  • 你呢？你有什么东西？How about you? What things do you have?HSK 1 · 这是什么
谁shéiwho
Also said shuí. Takes the place of the person: 他是谁？ 谁是老师？
  • 你看，这个人是谁？Look, who is this person?HSK 1 · 他是谁
  • 我问：“谁？”I ask: "Who is it?"HSK 2 · 请进
哪nǎwhich
Needs a measure word or noun after it: 哪个？ 哪本书？ 你是哪国人？
  • 香蕉皮、塑料袋、喝了一半的奶茶，应该扔到哪个垃圾桶呢？A banana peel, a plastic bag, a half-finished milk tea — which bin should each of them go into?HSK 4 · 垃圾分类
哪儿nǎrwhere
Northern speech says 哪儿, southern and Taiwan speech says 哪里 (nǎlǐ). Usually after 在 or 去: 你去哪儿？
  • 你住在哪儿？Where do you live?HSK 1 · 我住在哪儿
  • 服务员问：“你们去哪儿？”The clerk asks, "Where are you going?"HSK 2 · 买火车票
哪里nǎlǐwhere
Same as 哪儿. Also a modest reply to praise: 哪里哪里 "not at all".
  • 挂号的时候，阿姨问我：“你哪里不舒服？”While registering, the lady asked me: "Where do you feel unwell?"HSK 3 · 医院挂号
几jǐhow many (a small number)
When you expect an answer under about ten, and always with a measure word: 你家有几口人？ Also for dates and clock time: 几点？ 几月几号？
  • 我的火车是几点？What time is my train?HSK 1 · 坐火车
  • 我说：“现在几点了？”I say, "What time is it now?"HSK 1 · 几点了
多少duōshaohow many, how much
For any number, often without a measure word: 多少钱？ 你们学校有多少学生？
  • 这个杯子很漂亮，多少钱？This cup is very pretty — how much is it?HSK 1 · 买东西
  • 我问司机：“多少钱？”I ask the driver: "How much is it?"HSK 1 · 坐出租车
怎么zěnmehow; how come
Before a verb it asks how to do it: 这个字怎么读？ With a surprised tone it means "how come": 你怎么没来？
  • 医生问我：“你怎么了？”The doctor asked me, "What's wrong?"HSK 2 · 生病了
  • 我问朋友：“这个用中文怎么说？”I ask my friend: "How do you say this in Chinese?"HSK 2 · 怎么说
怎么样zěnmeyànghow is it; what do you think
At the end, asking for an opinion or a suggestion: 这个菜怎么样？ 我们明天去，怎么样？
  • 她说：“明天怎么样？”She says: "How about tomorrow?"HSK 2 · 什么时候
  • 电影看完以后，朋友问我：“电影怎么样？”After the movie finishes, my friend asks me: "How was the movie?"HSK 2 · 还可以
为什么wèishénmewhy
Goes before the verb or at the start: 你为什么学汉语？ The answer usually starts with 因为.
  • 很多朋友问我：“你为什么学中文？”A lot of friends ask me: "Why do you study Chinese?"HSK 2 · 为什么学中文
  • 朋友问我：“为什么不说三个人？”My friend asked me: "Why not say 'three people'?"HSK 2 · 几位
什么时候shénme shíhouwhen
Time goes before the verb in Chinese, so the question word does too: 你什么时候回家？
  • 朋友问我：“你什么时候搬的？为什么突然搬家？”My friend asked me: "When did you move? Why so suddenly?"HSK 3 · 我搬家了
多duōhow (big, long, old…)
Before an adjective: 多大？ how old / how big; 多长时间？ how long; 多远？ how far.
  • 我问她：“你今年多大？”I ask her, "How old are you this year?"HSK 1 · 新同学
  • 我去火车站，问服务员：“坐火车去，要多长时间？”I went to the train station and asked the clerk: "How long does it take to get there by train?"HSK 3 · 要多长时间
吗ma(yes/no question)
Add it to the end of a statement to make a yes/no question: 你是学生。 → 你是学生吗？
  • 你想来北京吗？Would you like to come to Beijing?HSK 1 · 我住在哪儿
  • 你会写汉语的数字吗？Can you write numbers in Chinese?HSK 1 · 数字真有趣
呢neand…? what about…?
After a noun it asks the same question back: 我很好，你呢？ It also softens a question word question.
  • 你呢？你有什么东西？How about you? What things do you have?HSK 1 · 这是什么
  • 我要去学校，我的钥匙呢？I want to go to school, where are my keys?HSK 1 · 找钥匙
吧ba…right? / let's
Checks something you think is true: 你是老师吧？ Or makes a suggestion: 我们走吧。
  • 朋友也说：“就点羊肉吧！”My friend also said: "Let's just order the lamb!"HSK 2 · 菜单
  • 姐姐说：“我们打车回家吧，快一点儿。”My sister says: "Let's take a taxi home, a little faster."HSK 2 · 有点儿
