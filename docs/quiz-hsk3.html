HSK 3 Reading Comprehension Questions 阅读理解

30 sample questions with answers, each taken from a free graded reading.

Every HSK 3 reading on this site ends with three multiple-choice questions in English about what the text actually said. There are 282 of them across 94 readings; below is one from each of 30 readings, spread across topics. Try to answer before opening the answer, then read the text it came from.

These are comprehension checks written for learners, not official HSK exam items. The HSK 3 reading practice page has every reading.

  1. What did the coworker ask on the narrator's first day at the new job?

    1. Where do you live
    2. Are you married
    3. How old are you
    Answer

    B. Are you married

    From 你结婚了吗 — Are You Married in Chinese

  2. Where was the narrator on his birthday?

    1. At home with his family
    2. Alone in a hotel in another city
    3. At a restaurant with friends
    Answer

    B. Alone in a hotel in another city

    From 难忘的生日 — An Unforgettable Birthday

  3. What does 干杯 literally mean?

    1. Cheers! (finish your drink together)
    2. Goodbye
    3. Thank you very much
    Answer

    A. Cheers! (finish your drink together)

    From 干杯 — Cheers in Chinese

  4. What was the score like for most of the match?

    1. Very close
    2. One team was far ahead
    3. Tied at zero the whole game
    Answer

    A. Very close

    From 有趣的比赛 — An Interesting Match

  5. What does "菜差不多了" mean here?

    1. The food is almost ready
    2. The food tastes bad
    3. The food is completely cold
    Answer

    A. The food is almost ready

    From 差不多 — About the Same in Chinese

  6. Why does the narrator often borrow books from the library?

    1. Books are too expensive to buy
    2. The library is next to his home
    3. His teacher asked him to
    Answer

    A. Books are too expensive to buy

    From 借书 — Borrowing Books in Chinese

  7. What did the narrator need to buy after arriving in China?

    1. A phone
    2. A SIM card
    3. A bus ticket
    Answer

    B. A SIM card

    From 买手机卡 — Getting a SIM Card in Chinese

  8. Where did the narrator meet the new friend?

    1. At the office
    2. While hiking
    3. At a restaurant
    Answer

    B. While hiking

    From 加个微信 — Add Me on WeChat in Chinese

  9. Which season does the writer say is the best in Beijing?

    1. Spring
    2. Autumn
    3. Winter
    Answer

    B. Autumn

    From 北京的四季 — Beijing's Four Seasons

  10. What does he teach the writer to use?

    1. A wok
    2. Chopsticks
    3. A brush
    Answer

    B. Chopsticks

    From 我的中国朋友 — My Chinese Friend

  11. What did the groom do while speaking at the wedding?

    1. He sang a song
    2. He cried while talking
    3. He left the stage
    Answer

    B. He cried while talking

    From 参加婚礼 — Attending a Wedding in Chinese

  12. What does Xiaomei's mom do when the narrator says he's full?

    1. She insists he eat just a little more
    2. She gets angry
    3. She stops offering food immediately
    Answer

    A. She insists he eat just a little more

    From 我饱了 — I Am Full in Chinese

  13. How often should the medicine be taken?

    1. Three times a day, two pills each time
    2. Once a day, one pill
    3. Only before bed

    A. Three times a day, two pills each time

    From 在药店 — At the Pharmacy in Chinese
