Questions

How many words do you need for each HSK level?

Under HSK 2.0: 150 words for HSK 1, 300 for HSK 2, 600 for HSK 3, 1,200 for HSK 4, 2,500 for HSK 5 and 5,000 for HSK 6. Each level roughly doubles the one before.

How much harder do the texts get from level to level?

On this site the typical HSK 1 reading is 6 sentences and 57 characters; HSK 3 is 7 sentences and 133 characters; HSK 6 is 8 sentences and 268 characters. The number of sentences barely changes. What grows is how much each sentence carries: about 10 characters per sentence at HSK 1, 24 at HSK 4 and 33 at HSK 6.

Which HSK level is the biggest jump?

Most learners find HSK 3 and HSK 5 the hardest steps. HSK 3 is where sentences start carrying complements and linking words; HSK 5 is where the register switches from spoken Chinese written down to written Chinese.

Does this use HSK 2.0 or HSK 3.0?

The word counts and levels here are HSK 2.0, the six-level scheme these readings are graded against. The newer HSK 3.0 standard (2021) regroups the levels into nine bands with larger word lists. If you are preparing for a specific exam sitting, check which version it uses.
