栋 vs 座: measure words for buildings

How to say your building and unit, and which measure word goes with a building.

What is the difference between 栋 and 座?

Both 栋 and 座 are measure words for buildings. 栋 is used for houses and buildings, and in many cities it is also the number of a residential building in a compound: 3栋 is Building 3. 座 is a broader measure word for large, fixed things: buildings, mountains, bridges, towers, even cities (一座山, 一座桥, 一座城市); office complexes often label their towers A座, B座. In an address, 单元 (dānyuán) is the entrance or stairwell of a building, so 3栋2单元501 is Building 3, Entrance 2, Flat 501.

栋houses and buildings; building numbers in residential compounds: 一栋楼, 5栋
座large fixed things: 一座山, 一座桥, 一座城市; office towers: A座

栋 dòng

Example sentences

  • 我住在8栋3单元。I live in Building 8, Entrance 3.
  • 这栋楼有二十层。This building has twenty floors.

座 zuò
