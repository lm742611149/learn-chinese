Grammar by topic

Describing things in Chinese: 很, 太 and degree

Chinese adjectives work as verbs — you don't need 是. But a bare adjective sounds like a comparison, so a degree word comes along.

太 + adjective + 了HSK 1

How do you say something is too expensive in Chinese?

太…了 wraps an adjective to say 'so…!' or 'too…', adding strong feeling: 太好吃了 = so delicious!

太好吃了！ — It's so delicious!
Seen in 妈妈做的菜太热了我累了羊肉还是鱼 +3

很 + adjective — adjective predicateHSK 1

Why do you need 很 before an adjective in Chinese?

很 plus an adjective forms the predicate; no separate word for "is" is needed.

那个商店很小。 — That store is small.
Seen in 大商店小商店小狗和小猫再见，老师我的猫 +1

不太 + adjective — not veryHSK 1

How do you say "not very" in Chinese?

不太 softens a negative judgement. Because 太 is fourth tone, 不 is read bú here.

我写的字不太好。 — The characters I write are not very good.
Seen in 在学校学习写汉字

有点儿 + adj（a little too... — usually a complaint）HSK 2

有点儿 goes before an adjective and adds a negative, complaining tone — something is a bit more than you'd like.

可是我觉得有点儿贵。— But I think it's a bit too expensive.
Seen in 有点儿送礼物好吃吗红色的裙子 +2

Adj + 极了 — extremelyHSK 2

极了 after an adjective intensifies it to mean "extremely."

我高兴极了。 — I was extremely happy.
Seen in 快乐的一天真好吃难忘的生日爬山

adj + 一点儿（asking for a small change — neutral）HSK 2

adj + 一点儿 asks for a small change and doesn't carry a complaint — the opposite feel from 有点儿.

可以便宜一点儿吗？— Can it be a little cheaper?
Seen in 有点儿便宜还是贵

Adj + 多了 — much more (comparative change)HSK 2

多了 after an adjective shows a big improvement compared to before.

我觉得好多了。 — I felt much better.
Seen in 累了就休息医院挂号

越来越 + adjective — more and more…HSK 3

越来越 shows a quality increasing over time.

气氛越来越热闹。 — The atmosphere got livelier and livelier.
Seen in 参加婚礼锻炼身体搬家还钱 +5

越 A 越 B — the more A, the more BHSK 3

Shows one thing growing along with another: 我越喝越觉得好喝。

雨越下越大。 — The rain is getting heavier and heavier.
Seen in 茶文化汉字的美网络交友地方口音 +5

越来越 (more and more)HSK 3

越来越 + adjective describes something increasing over time: 越来越好 (better and better), 越来越冷 (colder and colder).

我的身体越来越好。— My health is getting better and better.
Seen in 减肥雾霾与蓝天网络暴力宠物经济

adj + 地 + verbHSK 3

地 turns an adjective into an adverb describing how an action is done.

他热情地说。
Seen in 迷路南方和北方

How to use this page

These 11 patterns all do the same job in Chinese, so they are worth seeing side by side — the differences between them are easier to feel when they are next to each other than when they are spread across six HSK levels. They range from HSK 1–3.

Grammar sticks through reading, not through lists. Each entry links to the reading it was taken from. Skim the page, then go read one of them and come back when a sentence stops making sense.
