的 vs 得 vs 地: three particles, one sound

Before a noun, after a verb, before a verb.

What is the difference between 的 and 得 and 地?

All three are pronounced de. 的 links a description to a noun: 我的书, 漂亮的衣服. 地 turns a description into a way of doing, before a verb: 慢慢地走, 高兴地说. 得 comes after a verb or adjective and introduces how well or how much: 跑得很快, 说得不错, 累得不想动. A quick test: noun after it → 的; verb after it → 地; verb before it → 得.

的description + noun: 红色的车, 妈妈做的菜
地manner + verb: 认真地学习, 慢慢地说
得verb + degree or result: 写得很好, 忙得没时间吃饭
