HSK 4 Grammar 提高语法

77 patterns, each with the reading it came from.

A 是 A，但是……

Repeating the adjective concedes a point before turning to what matters more: 麻烦是麻烦，但是它让我们重新认识了自己的生活。

这个菜辣是辣，但是很好吃。— This dish is spicy, sure, but it's delicious.
Seen in 垃圾分类

V + 不 + 动

The potential complement 不动 means you lack the strength to do the action: 疼得连楼梯都爬不动。

行李太重了，我一个人拿不动。— The luggage is too heavy; I can't carry it by myself.
Seen in 健身房

V + 得 + complement

A degree complement paints how far the action goes — here the pressure is so heavy one cannot breathe.

他跑得很快。— He runs very fast.
Seen in 学车记大城市病

V + 起（来）

起来 after a verb can mean "to start to"; an object goes between 起 and 来 or replaces 来: 刮起了大风，下起了大雨 / 龙从墙上飞了起来。

孩子们一看见雪，就在外面玩了起来。 — As soon as the children saw the snow, they started playing outside.
Seen in 成语故事：画龙点睛

V来V去

A verb repeated with 来 and 去 means doing it back and forth or all over the place: 它每天在井里跳来跳去。

他在房间里走来走去，不知道该怎么办。 — He paced up and down the room, not knowing what to do.
Seen in 成语故事：井底之蛙

adjective + 得 + complement

得 after an adjective introduces a complement describing the degree, often with a vivid comparison, as in 热闹得像过节一样 or 白得发亮.

到处是灯光和音乐，热闹得像过节一样。 — Lights and music everywhere, as lively as a festival.
Seen in 迷人的丽江二维码生活

verb/adjective + 得 + complement

得 introduces a complement showing the degree or result of an action or state, as in 累得只好坐下来休息 or 激动得说不出话来.

我激动得说不出话来。 — I was so moved that I couldn't speak.
Seen in 长城游记

……的时候……的时候……(对比)

Repeating "……的时候" lets you contrast two different times or situations: 好的时候和不好的时候差别很大。

忙的时候和不忙的时候，他的心情很不一样。— When he's busy versus not busy, his mood is very different.
Seen in 网约车司机

……的话

的话 attaches to a clause to mark it as a condition, similar to "if": 东西质量好的话，用起来跟新的差不多。

有时间的话，我们一起去逛逛。— If you have time, let's go browsing together.
Seen in 二手市场

…，却… (yet; however)

却 means "yet / however" and sits after the subject, inside the second clause, to mark a contrast: 城市给了我新的生活，故乡却留着我最老的记忆。 Unlike 但是, it cannot start the clause before the subject.

人们连机器都没有，却能在这么高的山上留下这样伟大的建筑。 — People didn't even have machines, yet they managed to leave behind such a great structure on mountains this high.
Seen in 长城游记成语故事：画蛇添足移民与故乡传统手艺的消失 +2

一 + 量词 + 也/都 + 不/没…… — not even one

This pattern emphasises a complete zero: 重要的事一件也没做；一次也不看。

那天他一句话也没说。— That day he didn't say a single word.
Seen in 时间管理

一直…… — Continuously / always

Describes a state or action that continues without change over time.

人们想通过歌曲表达感情的心，一直没有变。— People's desire to express their feelings through songs has never changed.
Seen in 流行音乐

一直……，直到…… — kept …ing, until…

一直 marks a state that continued without change, and 直到 gives the moment it ended.

他一直站在门口，直到朋友出来。— He kept standing at the door until his friend came out.
Seen in 太极拳

不仅……而且……

"Not only A, but also B" — the 而且 part adds something further and often more important, as in 不仅是美丽的风景，而且是那种让人慢下来的生活.

丽江迷人的地方，不仅是美丽的风景，而且是那种让人慢下来的生活。 — What makes Lijiang charming is not only the beautiful scenery, but also the kind of life that makes people slow down.
Seen in 迷人的丽江中国的高铁火锅与朋友面试技巧 +1

不但……也……

"Not only... but also..." links two related results: 学车不但让我学会了开车，也让我明白了耐心的重要。

这本书不但有意思，也很有用。— This book is not only interesting, but also very useful.
Seen in 学车记

不但……而且…… — not only… but also…

不但 introduces the first fact and 而且 adds a second, stronger one about the same subject: 他不但记得每个人的名字，而且知道谁家白天没有人。

这个办法不但简单，而且很有效。— This method is not only simple, but also very effective.
Seen in 广场舞快递小哥独自旅行

不再……而是……

'no longer A, but instead B' — an old way replaced by a new one.

服务员不再给你菜单,而是请你扫桌子上的二维码点菜。
Seen in 二维码生活

不只是……更是……

'not only... but even more...' — the second part is the deeper or more important point.

送礼送的不只是东西,更是人和人的感情。
Seen in 送礼的学问

不是……只是…… — It's not X, it's just Y

Softens a claim by rejecting one interpretation and offering a smaller, simpler one instead.

相亲不是坏事，它只是多给了你一次认识别人的机会。— Blind dates aren't a bad thing, they just give you one more chance to meet someone.
Seen in 相亲

不是……就是……

'if not A, then B' — the situation is always one of two (often undesirable) options.

不是太贵,就是太旧。
Seen in 租房记

不是……的

Used at the end of a sentence to soften or emphasize a statement about how things are: 效果不是马上就能看到的。

学好一门语言不是一天两天的。— Mastering a language isn't something that happens in a day or two.
Seen in 中医初体验

不是…而是… — it's not X, but rather Y

Corrects an assumption by negating one option and affirming another: 'it is not A, but rather B'. Common for reframing who or what is really responsible. 例：改变现状的，不是运气，而是坚持。

把这些故事带到英语世界的，不是出版社，而是一群业余译者自发组成的翻译社群。 — It was not publishing houses that carried these stories into the English-speaking world, but translation communities formed by amateur translators.
Seen in 汉字的美火锅与朋友时间管理广告的秘密 +10

不用 + V

'no need to...' — a soft way to say something is unnecessary. Note the tone change: bú yòng.

礼物不用太贵。
Seen in 送礼的学问

不管…都… (no matter…, …)

"No matter A, B still holds." 不管 introduces a condition that makes no difference, and 都 stresses that the result never changes, as in 不管刮风下雨，我都得带它出去散步.

不管刮风下雨，我都得带它出去散步。 — No matter whether it's windy or raining, I have to take it out for a walk.
Seen in 养宠物的责任京剧脸谱

也算是……

"Can also be considered as..." — used to reframe something in a positive light: 也算是一种收获。

虽然没拿到奖，但学到了很多，也算是一种收获。— Although he didn't win an award, he learned a lot, which can also be considered a gain.
Seen in 网约车司机

从来不 + verb

从来不 means "never (as a habit)" and describes something the speaker has never done and does not do, as in 我从来不告诉网上的朋友自己的地址.

我从来不告诉网上的朋友自己的地址。 — I never tell online friends my address.
Seen in 网络交友

再 + adj + 也……

'no matter how (adj), still...' — an extreme degree that still cannot change the result.

再高的工资也不能让你快乐。
Seen in 职业选择

再也没（有）……过

"Never … again" — something stopped happening from a certain point on: 他的羊再也没有丢过。

那次以后，他再也没有迟到过。 — After that time, he was never late again.
Seen in 成语故事：亡羊补牢

到处都是…… — … is everywhere

到处 means "all over the place" and is normally followed by 都, showing there are no exceptions.

下雪以后，路上到处都是水。— After the snow, there was water all over the road.
Seen in 十二生肖

即使……也……

"Even if A, still B." 即使 introduces an extreme or hypothetical situation, and 也 shows the result does not change, as in 即使是半夜，我也会爬起来陪她聊天.

即使是半夜，我也会爬起来陪她聊天。 — Even if it's the middle of the night, I'll get up to chat with her.
Seen in 汉字的美遥远的朋友

原来 (sudden realization)

原来 introduces a sudden realization of something that was true all along: 原来我怀念的不是办公室，而是人和人之间的温度。

他今天没来上课，原来是生病了。 — He didn't come to class today; it turns out he was ill.
Seen in 成语故事：亡羊补牢独自旅行远程办公

反而 — on the contrary; instead

Introduces a result that is the opposite of what one would expect: 'on the contrary, instead', e.g. 吃了药，病反而更重了.

很多人说话时反而更容易失去分寸。 — Many people actually find it easier to lose restraint when speaking (the opposite of what you'd expect).
Seen in 成语故事：画蛇添足消费主义网络暴力独生子女一代 +2

只有…才… (only if / only… can…)

"Only if A can B happen." 只有 states the one necessary condition, and 才 marks the result that follows only from it, as in 只有你能对它负责，才应该把它带回家.

只有把沉默的证据也纳入视野，我们的判断才可能接近真相。 — Only when the silent evidence is also brought into view can our judgments come close to the truth.
Seen in 考研热养宠物的责任故宫的故事幸存者偏差

只要……就…… — As long as... then...

"As long as A, then B." 只要 introduces a sufficient condition, and 就 marks the result: 只要用手机选好菜，饭就送到了门口。

只要双方愿意站在对方的角度考虑问题，幸福就不是一件难事。 — As long as both partners are willing to see things from each other's perspective, happiness is not a difficult thing.
Seen in 外卖时代流行音乐地方口音雾霾与蓝天 +4

只要……都/就…… — as long as…

只要 gives the one condition that is enough, and 都 or 就 states the result: 只要努力，属什么都一样。

只要有时间，我就去图书馆看书。— As long as I have time, I go and read at the library.
Seen in 十二生肖

只要……都…… — As long as..., everyone/anyone...

States a condition that, once met, applies to every possible subject (谁, 什么, etc.).

只要坚持练习，谁都可以变得更强。— As long as you keep practicing, anyone can become stronger.
Seen in 功夫电影

在于 (to lie in; to consist in)

"(The key/problem) lies in..." points to where the real cause is: 问题在于我们是不是清楚自己真正需要什么。

学好外语的关键在于每天练习。 — The key to mastering a foreign language lies in daily practice.
Seen in 直播带货唐诗之美

好像…… — It seems like...

Expresses that something appears or seems to be the case, without stating it as fact.

好像只是为了让人在网上多听几遍。— It seems as if it's only to get people to replay it online more times.
Seen in 流行音乐

好像……一样

"As if; just like." 好像 plus 一样 wraps around a situation to say something seems or feels that way, as in 好像从来没有分开过一样.

我们一见面就笑了，好像从来没有分开过一样。 — The moment we met we burst out laughing, as if we had never been apart.
Seen in 遥远的朋友

对……感兴趣

'to be interested in...' — the object of interest goes after 对, before 感兴趣.

我对电脑技术特别感兴趣。
Seen in 职业选择

对……来说 — as far as … is concerned

对……来说 marks whose point of view you are taking, and is often used twice to contrast two people: 对我来说……；对他来说……。

对孩子来说，暑假是一年中最快乐的时候。— For children, the summer holiday is the happiest time of the year.
Seen in 咖啡文化汉服复兴快递小哥宠物经济

尽管……，还是……

"Although / despite A, still B" admits a fact but keeps the conclusion: 尽管有这些问题，我还是觉得方向不会改变。

尽管下着大雨，他还是准时到了。— Despite the heavy rain, he still arrived on time.
Seen in 无现金社会

并不是……的

An emphatic negation meaning "is definitely not...": 并不是随便穿穿就行的。

这件事并不是他一个人的错。— This matter is definitely not his fault alone.
Seen in 汉服复兴

忍不住 — Can't help but...

Placed before a verb phrase to show someone cannot resist doing something.

让人忍不住想知道后面发生了什么。— It makes people unable to resist wanting to know what happens next.
Seen in 追剧

想什么时候……就什么时候……

The same question word used twice with 就 means "whenever / wherever / whatever you like": 我想什么时候游泳就什么时候游泳。

周末你想几点起床就几点起床。 — At the weekend you can get up whatever time you like.
Seen in 成语故事：井底之蛙

把 + O + V + 了一遍

The 把 structure moves the object before the verb; 一遍 means doing the action once from start to finish.

我把每一条都认真地看了一遍。
Seen in 租房记

把 + O + V + 补语

The 把 construction moves the object before the verb to emphasize what happens to it, often followed by a result or complement: 把空调调暖和一点儿。

请把门关好。— Please close the door properly.
Seen in 网约车司机

把 + object + 放在 + place — the 把 construction

把 moves the object in front of the verb when you say what happens to it, especially where it ends up.

请把这些书放在桌子上。— Please put these books on the desk.
Seen in 时间管理

把 + object + 留在 + place

The 把 structure highlights where something ends up being left or kept: 工作把我留在了这里；把心留在了远方。

我把钥匙留在了家里。— I left my keys at home.
Seen in 移民与故乡

把 A 当成 B — to treat A as B

'To treat A as B' — 把 fronts the object, 当成 (also 当作) states the identity assigned to it, often a mistaken one. 例：别把客气话当成承诺。

把专注当成又一项必须完成的任务，本身就违背了禅的本意。 — Treating concentration as yet another task to be completed already goes against the point of Zen.
Seen in 学车记李白与月亮马拉松热禅与生活

无论……，……都/就……

"No matter..." 无论 introduces a question word or choice, and the following clause states a result that holds regardless: 无论假期怎么过，重要的是让自己真正放松下来。

无论多忙，他每天都锻炼身体。— No matter how busy he is, he exercises every day.
Seen in 假期计划

无论…，都… — 'no matter what..., always...'

'No matter how/what A, B still holds' — 无论 introduces an open-ended condition (often with a question word like 多么/什么), and 都 asserts the unchanging result. 例：无论遇到什么困难，都不要放弃。

无论最终结论如何，这次发现都提醒我们：历史并非一成不变的定论。 — Whatever the final conclusion turns out to be, this discovery reminds us that history is not a fixed, unchanging verdict.
Seen in 夜市文化快递小哥老龄化社会考古新发现 +2

既……又……

'Both A and B' — coordinates two merits (or faults) of the same thing, giving the sentence a balanced, essayistic rhythm. 例：这个办法既省钱，又省时间。

一个“差不多”，既给了对方大致的预期，又为自己留了余地。 — A single "more or less" both gives the other person a rough expectation and leaves oneself some leeway.
Seen in 二手市场汉语的模糊之美

既然……

"Since (a fact already holds)..." draws a conclusion from an accepted premise: 既然本科学历已经不够用了，很多人觉得只有读研才能让自己更有竞争力。

既然你已经来了，就留下来吃饭吧。— Since you're already here, stay for dinner.
Seen in 考研热

既然……也就……

"Since (a fact is already true), then naturally..." draws a conclusion from an accepted premise: 既然白天大家都在上班上学，晚上出来放松一下，也就成了一种生活习惯。

既然你已经决定了，我也就不再劝你了。— Since you've already decided, I won't try to persuade you anymore.
Seen in 夜市文化

既然……就…… — since…, then…

既然 states a fact both speakers already accept, and 就 draws the natural conclusion from it.

既然你不舒服，就早点儿休息吧。— Since you don't feel well, get some rest early.
Seen in 奶茶经济

本来……结果…… — Originally... but it turned out...

Contrasts an original plan or expectation with an unexpected actual outcome.

本来只想看一集，结果看到了半夜。— They originally only wanted to watch one episode, but ended up watching until midnight.
Seen in 追剧

比……还…… — even more … than

比 makes the comparison and 还 says the difference is bigger than expected: 比一份午饭还贵。

今天比昨天还热。— Today is even hotter than yesterday.
Seen in 奶茶经济

甚至

甚至 means "even" and introduces the most extreme or surprising case in a list or statement, as in 我很累，甚至有点儿后悔养了它.

有一段时间我真的很累，甚至有点儿后悔养了它。 — For a while I was really exhausted; I even slightly regretted getting it.
Seen in 养宠物的责任地方口音消费主义文化冲击

直到……才……

"Not until A did B happen." 直到 marks the turning point, 才 stresses that B happened late: 直到生病住院，我才明白健康有多重要。

直到晚上十二点，他才回家。— He didn't come home until twelve at night.
Seen in 熬夜的代价

看起来……，其实…… — it looks…, but actually…

看起来 gives the first impression, and 其实 corrects it with the real situation.

这个问题看起来很难，其实很简单。— This question looks hard, but it's actually very simple.
Seen in 排队文化太极拳

竟然

竟然 marks that something is unexpected or surprising to the speaker, like "amazingly" or "to my surprise", as in 她竟然和我想的一模一样.

他学了三年汉语，竟然没去过中国。 — He has studied Chinese for three years, yet surprisingly has never been to China.
Seen in 普通话与方言网络交友

让人 + V

'makes people...' — 让 introduces the person affected and the resulting action or feeling.

它们的发音让人想到不好的意思。
Seen in 送礼的学问

谁……，谁就……

A question word used twice refers to the same (not yet known) person, like "whoever": 谁先画完，酒就是谁的。

谁先到，谁就先买票。 — Whoever arrives first buys the tickets first.
Seen in 面子成语故事：画蛇添足

谁也……不了……

"No one can..." 谁 here means "whoever/anyone," and 也 emphasizes that not even one person can do it: 谁也说服不了谁。

这道题太难了，谁也做不出来。— This problem is too hard; no one can solve it.
Seen in 假期计划

跟……一样

"The same as; just like"; can follow 得 to describe how something is done: 这些龙画得跟真的一样。

他的中文说得跟中国人一样好。 — He speaks Chinese as well as a Chinese person.
Seen in 成语故事：画龙点睛

跟……没有关系 — to have nothing to do with…

A 跟 B 有/没有关系 says whether two things are connected; 多大 before 关系 softens it to "not much".

这件事跟他没有关系。— This matter has nothing to do with him.
Seen in 十二生肖

还没……就……

"Before (A) has even happened, (B) already…": 它的左脚还没进去，右腿就已经卡在井口了。

我还没说完，他就走了。 — I hadn't even finished speaking when he left.
Seen in 成语故事：井底之蛙

这才……

"Only then" — something happens later than it should have, after a trigger: 发现羊又少了一只，这才后悔没听邻居的话。

考试前一天，他这才开始复习。 — Only the day before the exam did he start reviewing.
Seen in 成语故事：亡羊补牢

连……都/也……

"Even ..." highlights an extreme case to stress the point: 连小偷都快找不到工作了；连一瓶水都买不了。

他忙得连饭都忘了吃。— He was so busy he even forgot to eat.
Seen in 无现金社会

连…也… (even…)

"Even" — stresses an extreme case, usually in a negative sentence: 他连一只兔子也没等到。 (He didn't get even one rabbit.)

他太忙了，连吃饭的时间也没有。 — He's so busy he doesn't even have time to eat.
Seen in 成语故事：守株待兔唐诗之美

连…都… — even…

'Even…' — 连 fronts the most extreme or most basic item, and 都 (or 也) marks it as included, implying everything lesser follows. 例：他连自己的生日都忘了。

几百年前人们连机器都没有。 — Hundreds of years ago, people didn't even have machines.
Seen in 长城游记二维码生活微信改变生活极简生活 +2

除了……以外，再/还……

"Besides A, also B" adds something on top of what is already there: 除了普通话以外，再学几句当地的方言。

除了米饭以外，我们还点了一个汤。 — Besides rice, we also ordered a soup.
Seen in 普通话与方言

随着…… — Along with / as...

Means 'along with, as' — one change happens in step with another change, usually at the start of the sentence, e.g. 随着经济的发展，生活水平提高了.

随着西医在中国的普及，中医的地位经历了从被质疑到重新被重视的过程。 — As Western medicine spread in China, the status of Chinese medicine went through a process of being doubted and then re-valued.
Seen in 相亲咖啡文化奶茶经济老龄化社会 +5

难道……吗？ — surely you don't mean…?

A rhetorical question that expects the answer 'no'; the writer uses it to make an objection feel obvious rather than to ask for information.

难道我们要等到损失无法挽回时才行动吗？ — Are we really going to wait until the loss is beyond repair before acting?
Seen in 熟能生巧生物多样性

靠 (to rely on)

"To rely on; to depend on", followed by what you depend on: 只想靠运气的人 (people who only want to rely on luck).

很多学生靠奖学金完成了大学学习。— Many students finished university by relying on scholarships.
Seen in 成语故事：守株待兔一碗兰州拉面

首先……其次……另外……最后……

A set of connectors for listing points in order: 首先 (first), 其次 (second), 另外 (in addition), 最后 (finally). The text uses them to organize the interview tips.

首先要多听，其次要多说，另外还要多写。— First listen more, second speak more, and in addition write more.
Seen in 面试技巧
