HSK 1 Reading Comprehension Questions 阅读理解

30 sample questions with answers, each taken from a free graded reading.
HSK 1HSK 2HSK 3HSK 4HSK 5HSK 6

Every HSK 1 reading on this site ends with three multiple-choice questions in English about what the text actually said. There are 207 of them across 69 readings; below is one from each of 30 readings, spread across topics. Try to answer before opening the answer, then read the text it came from.

These are comprehension checks written for learners, not official HSK exam items. The HSK 1 reading practice page has every reading.

  1. How big is the dog?

    1. Big
    2. Small
    3. Medium
    Answer

    A. Big

    From 小狗和小猫 — Dog and Cat

  2. Whose birthday is it today?

    1. Dad's
    2. Mom's
    3. The narrator's
    Answer

    B. Mom's

    From 妈妈的生日 — Mom's Birthday

  3. What does the narrator like?

    1. Apples and rice
    2. Tea and coffee
    3. Fish and vegetables
    Answer

    A. Apples and rice

    From 苹果和米饭 — Apples and Rice

  4. What is the narrator looking for?

    1. His keys
    2. His book
    3. His shoes
    Answer

    A. His keys

    From 找钥匙 — Looking for My Keys

  5. What does 请问 mean before asking a question?

    1. Excuse me / may I ask
    2. Thank you
    3. Goodbye
    Answer

    A. Excuse me / may I ask

    From 请问 — Excuse Me in Chinese

  6. What does the narrator study at school?

    1. Chinese
    2. English
    3. Math
    Answer

    A. Chinese

    From 再见，老师 — Goodbye, Teacher

  7. Which store has fewer people?

    1. The big store
    2. The small store
    3. Both stores
    Answer

  8. Where is the computer?

    2. On the chair
    3. On the bed

    3. Three

    From 数字真有趣 — Numbers Are Fun

  10. Where does the narrator live?

    3. Hong Kong

    From 中国很大 — China Is Big

  11. Why couldn't the speaker go to the shop at noon?

    1. It started raining
    2. They were sleeping
    3. The shop was closed
    Answer

    From 下雨了 — Talking About Rain in Chinese

  12. Where does Dad work?

    1. At a store
    2. At a school
    3. At a hospital
    Answer

    A. At a store

  13. How old is the friend this year?

    1. Twenty-one
    2. Twenty
    3. Twenty-two
    Answer

  14. What do they eat besides bread?

    1. Fruit
    2. Eggs
    3. Rice
    Answer

    B. Eggs

    1. His cat
    2. His teacher
    3. His mom
    Answer

    2. A little cat
    3. An apple
    Answer

    Answer

    B. A student
