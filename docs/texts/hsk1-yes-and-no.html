是不是Shì bu shìSaying Yes and No in Chinese

HSK 1 reading practice · 98 words · ~2 min
同学tóngxué说shuō：“你nǐ是shì学生xuésheng吗ma？”我wǒ说shuō：“是shì，我wǒ是shì学生xuésheng。”
My classmate asks, "Are you a student?" I say, "Yes, I am a student."
同学tóngxué说shuō：“你nǐ有yǒu猫māo吗ma？”我wǒ说shuō：“没有méiyǒu，我wǒ有yǒu狗gǒu。”
My classmate asks, "Do you have a cat?" I say, "No, I have a dog."
同学tóngxué说shuō：“你nǐ会huì说shuō汉语Hànyǔ吗ma？”我wǒ说shuō：“我wǒ会huì，我wǒ喜欢xǐhuan汉语Hànyǔ。”
My classmate asks, "Can you speak Chinese?" I say, "I can, and I like it."
同学tóngxué说shuō：“你nǐ喜欢xǐhuan猫māo吗ma？”我wǒ说shuō：“不bù喜欢xǐhuan，我wǒ喜欢xǐhuan狗gǒu。”
My classmate asks, "Do you like cats?" I say, "No, I like dogs."
同学tóngxué说shuō：“你nǐ的de汉语Hànyǔ很hěn好hǎo！”
My classmate says, "Your Chinese is good!"

Grammar note 语法点

是 / 不是 — yes/no with 是

Chinese has no single word for 'yes' or 'no.' To answer a question with 是, repeat 是 for yes, or say 不是 for no.

你是学生吗？是，我是学生。 — Are you a student? Yes, I am a student.
有 / 没有 — yes/no with 有

To answer a question with 有 (to have), say 有 for yes or 没有 for no — 没有 is the special negative form of 有, not 不有.

你有猫吗？没有，我有狗。 — Do you have a cat? No, I have a dog.
会 / 不会, 喜欢 / 不喜欢 — more yes/no pairs

For most other verbs, just repeat the verb for yes, or put 不 before it for no: 会 becomes 不会 (can/cannot), 喜欢 becomes 不喜欢 (like/dislike).

你喜欢猫吗？不喜欢，我喜欢狗。 — Do you like cats? No, I like dogs.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Is the narrator a student?
2. What pet does the narrator have?
3. Can the narrator speak Chinese?
