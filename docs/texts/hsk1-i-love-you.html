HSK 1 · Newbie

我爱你Wǒ ài nǐHow to Say I Love You in Chinese

HSK 1 reading practice · 64 words · ~1 min
明天míngtiān我wǒ去qù北京Běijīng学习xuéxí。
Tomorrow I'm going to Beijing to study.
妈妈māma看kàn我wǒ的de衣服yīfu和hé书shū，她tā说shuō：“你nǐ去qù北京Běijīng，我wǒ在zài家jiā，我wǒ想xiǎng你nǐ。”
Mom looks at my clothes and books and says, "You'll be in Beijing and I'll be at home. I'll miss you."
我wǒ说shuō：“妈妈māma，我wǒ爱ài你nǐ。”
I say, "Mom, I love you."
妈妈māma说shuō：“我wǒ也yě爱ài你nǐ。”
Mom says, "I love you too."
我wǒ喜欢xǐhuan学校xuéxiào，喜欢xǐhuan朋友péngyou，我wǒ爱ài爸爸bàba妈妈māma。
I like school and I like my friends — but it's my mom and dad I love.

Grammar note 语法点

我爱你 — saying I love you

爱 is strong in Chinese. It is used for family and partners, not for things you merely enjoy: 我爱你 = I love you.

妈妈，我爱你。 — Mom, I love you.
喜欢 vs 爱 — like vs love

喜欢 covers liking things, places and friends; 爱 is saved for the people closest to you. Saying 我爱苹果 for a fruit sounds odd — use 喜欢.

我喜欢朋友，我爱爸爸妈妈。 — I like my friends; I love my mom and dad.
也 — also, too

也 goes before the verb, never at the end of the sentence the way English "too" does.

我也爱你。 — I love you too.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Where is the narrator going tomorrow?
2. Where will Mom be while the narrator is in Beijing?
3. According to the last line, who does the narrator love?
