几点开门Jǐ diǎn kāiménWhat Time Does It Open in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 123 words · ~2 min
星期天xīngqītiān早上zǎoshang，我wǒ去qù银行yínháng，可是kěshì银行yínháng的de门mén关guān着zhe，一个yí ge人rén也yě没有méiyǒu。
Sunday morning, I went to the bank, but the bank's door was closed, and there wasn't a single person around.
我wǒ问wèn旁边pángbiān的de人rén：“银行yínháng几点jǐ diǎn开门kāimén？”
I asked the person next to me: "What time does the bank open?"
他tā说shuō：“银行yínháng九点jiǔ diǎn开门kāimén，现在xiànzài才cái八点bā diǎn。”
He said: "The bank opens at nine — it's only eight now."
我wǒ还hái要yào等děng一个yí ge小时xiǎoshí，就jiù先xiān去qù附近fùjìn的de商店shāngdiàn看kàn了le看kàn。
I still had an hour to wait, so I went to look around a nearby shop first.
过guò了le一会儿yíhuìr，我wǒ又yòu回到huídào银行yínháng，看见kànjiàn门mén开kāi着zhe，里面lǐmiàn已经yǐjīng有yǒu很多hěn duō人rén。
After a while, I went back to the bank and saw the door was open — inside, there were already many people.
我wǒ也yě进去jìnqù等děng着zhe，过guò了le半bàn个ge小时xiǎoshí，我wǒ的de事情shìqing就jiù做zuò完wán了le。
I went in and waited too, and after half an hour, my business was done.
我wǒ走zǒu出chū银行yínháng的de时候shíhou，看kàn了le看kàn时间shíjiān，才cái十shí点diǎn，我wǒ决定juédìng先xiān去qù吃饭chīfàn。
When I walked out of the bank, I checked the time — only ten o'clock — and I decided to go eat first.

Key words 生词

几点jǐ diǎnwhat time
开门kāiménto open (for business)
关guānclosed; to close
门méndoor
旁边pángbiānbeside; nearby
附近fùjìnnearby

Grammar note 语法点

几点开门？(What time does it open?)

几点 asks for a specific clock time; paired with 开门 (to open for business) or 关门 (to close), it's the standard way to ask a shop's hours.

银行几点开门？— What time does the bank open?
才 + time（only; not yet — something is earlier than expected）

才 before a time expression shows that a point is still early, or hasn't been reached yet.

银行九点开门，现在才八点。— The bank opens at nine, and it's only eight now.
V + 着（ongoing state）

着 attached to a verb shows a state continuing — here, the door staying shut, and later, staying open.

银行的门关着，一个人也没有。— The bank's door was closed, and there wasn't a single person around.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the narrator find when arriving at the bank?
2. What time does the bank actually open?
3. What does the narrator do right after finishing at the bank?
