着急的一天Zháojí de yì tiānA Stressful Day

HSK 3 reading practice · 109 words · ~2 min
今天jīntiān早上zǎoshang，我的wǒ de闹钟nàozhōng没有méiyǒu响xiǎng，我wǒ起qǐ晚wǎn了le半个小时bàn ge xiǎoshí。
This morning, my alarm clock didn't ring, and I got up half an hour late.
我wǒ马上mǎshàng起床qǐchuáng，可是kěshì找不到zhǎo bu dào钥匙yàoshi，非常fēicháng着急zháojí。
I immediately got out of bed, but I couldn't find my key, and I was extremely anxious.
出门chūmén的时候de shíhou，外面wàimiàn正在zhèngzài下xià大雨dàyǔ，我wǒ没有méiyǒu带dài伞sǎn。
When I went out, it was raining hard outside, and I hadn't brought an umbrella.
我wǒ跑pǎo到dào车站chēzhàn，可是kěshì公共汽车gōnggòng qìchē已经yǐjīng开kāi走zǒu了le。
I ran to the bus stop, but the bus had already driven off.
我wǒ只好zhǐhǎo打车dǎchē去qù公司gōngsī，可是kěshì路上lùshang车chē很多hěn duō，走zǒu得de很hěn慢màn。
I had no choice but to take a taxi to the office, but there were a lot of cars on the road, and we moved very slowly.
到dào了le公司gōngsī，我wǒ发现fāxiàn今天jīntiān开会kāihuì的de时间shíjiān改gǎi了le，其实qíshí我wǒ一点儿yìdiǎnr都dōu不用búyòng着急zháojí。
When I arrived at the office, I discovered that today's meeting time had changed, and actually I didn't need to worry at all.
同事tóngshì看到kàndào我wǒ又yòu累lèi又yòu急jí，都dōu笑xiào了le。
My colleagues saw that I was both tired and anxious, and they all laughed.
虽然suīrán这zhè一天yì tiān很hěn着急zháojí，但是dànshì最后zuìhòu很hěn顺利shùnlì。
Although this day was stressful, in the end everything went smoothly.

Key words 生词

闹钟nàozhōngalarm clock
钥匙yàoshikey
伞sǎnumbrella
只好zhǐhǎohave no choice but to
其实qíshíactually
顺利shùnlìsmoothly; successfully

Grammar note 语法点

只好 (have no choice but to)

只好 shows the speaker is forced to take an action because there is no better option.

我只好打车去公司。
又……又……(both ... and ...)

又 before each of two adjectives or verbs shows both qualities or actions are true at once.

同事看到我又累又急。
虽然……但是……(although ... but ...)

Introduces a fact that contrasts with what follows, showing a concession.

虽然这一天很着急，但是最后很顺利。

Check yourself 小测验

1. 今天早上为什么起晚了？
2. 出门的时候天气怎么样？
3. 到了公司才发现什么？
