唐诗之美

Tángshī zhī měi
The Beauty of Tang Poetry · 178 words · ~3 min
一千三百yìqiān sānbǎi多duō年nián前qián的de唐朝Tángcháo，是shì中国Zhōngguó诗shī的de黄金huángjīn时代shídài。
The Tang dynasty, more than one thousand three hundred years ago, was the golden age of Chinese poetry.
那nà时候shíhou，几乎jīhū人人rénrén都dōu会huì写xiě诗shī：皇帝huángdì写xiě，士兵shìbīng写xiě，连lián卖mài酒jiǔ的de人rén也yě写xiě。
At that time, almost everyone could write poems: emperors wrote them, soldiers wrote them, and even wine sellers wrote them.
《全唐诗Quán Táng Shī》里li保存bǎocún了le将近jiāngjìn五万wǔwàn首shǒu诗shī，作者zuòzhě超过chāoguò两千liǎngqiān人rén。
The Complete Tang Poems preserves nearly fifty thousand poems, by more than two thousand authors.
唐诗Tángshī的de美měi，首先shǒuxiān在于zàiyú它tā的de声音shēngyīn：每měi首shǒu诗shī都dōu像xiàng一yì首shǒu小小xiǎoxiǎo的de歌gē，读dú出来chūlai特别tèbié好听hǎotīng。
The beauty of Tang poetry lies first of all in its sound: every poem is like a tiny song, and reading it aloud is especially pleasant.
其次qícì，诗人shīrén们men善于shànyú用yòng最zuì简单jiǎndān的de字zì，表达biǎodá最zuì深shēn的de感情gǎnqíng。
Secondly, the poets were good at using the simplest characters to express the deepest feelings.
比如bǐrú想xiǎng家jiā的de时候shíhou，他们tāmen不bù说shuō“我wǒ很hěn难过nánguò”，而是érshì写xiě月亮yuèliang、写xiě秋天qiūtiān的de树叶shùyè。
For example, when they missed home, they did not say "I am sad", but instead wrote about the moon, or about autumn leaves.
今天jīntiān，中国Zhōngguó孩子háizi上学shàngxué后hòu背bèi的de第一dì-yī首shǒu诗shī，往往wǎngwǎng还是háishi唐诗Tángshī。
Today, the first poem Chinese children memorize after starting school is still usually a Tang poem.
在zài手机shǒujī时代shídài，很多hěn duō年轻人niánqīngrén把bǎ唐诗Tángshī做成zuòchéng音乐yīnyuè和hé短视频duǎnshìpín，古老gǔlǎo的de诗shī又yòu活huó了le过来guòlai。
In the smartphone era, many young people turn Tang poems into music and short videos, and the ancient poems have come back to life.
也许yěxǔ这zhè就jiù是shì唐诗Tángshī的de美měi：时间shíjiān一直yìzhí在zài变biàn，人rén的de感情gǎnqíng却què没有méiyǒu变biàn。
Perhaps this is the beauty of Tang poetry: time keeps changing, yet human feelings have not.

Grammar note 语法点

在于 (to lie in; to consist in)

Points out where the essence or key of something is found: 唐诗的美，首先在于它的声音。

学好外语的关键在于每天练习。 — The key to mastering a foreign language lies in daily practice.
善于 (to be good at)

A more formal way to say someone does something skillfully, usually followed by a verb phrase: 诗人们善于用最简单的字，表达最深的感情。

她善于跟不同的人交流。 — She is good at communicating with different kinds of people.
连…也… (even…)

连 highlights an extreme example, and 也 (or 都) completes it: 连卖酒的人也写。

他忙得连饭也忘了吃。 — He was so busy that he even forgot to eat.
