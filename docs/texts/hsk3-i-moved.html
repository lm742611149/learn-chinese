我搬家了Wǒ bānjiā leI Moved in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 113 words · ~2 min
我wǒ给gěi朋友péngyou打电话dǎ diànhuà，说shuō：“好久hǎojiǔ不bú见jiàn，我wǒ有yǒu一yí件jiàn事shì要yào告诉gàosu你nǐ：我wǒ搬家bānjiā了le。”
I rang a friend: "Long time no see — I've got something to tell you: I moved."
新家xīnjiā离lí地铁站dìtiězhàn很hěn近jìn，走路zǒulù只要zhǐyào五wǔ分钟fēnzhōng。
The new home is very close to the subway station — it only takes five minutes on foot.
虽然suīrán房租fángzū贵guì了le一点yìdiǎn，但是dànshì附近fùjìn很hěn方便fāngbiàn，楼下lóuxià就jiù有yǒu超市chāoshì。
Although the rent is a bit more expensive, the area is very convenient — there's a supermarket right downstairs.
朋友péngyou问wèn我wǒ：“你nǐ什么时候shénme shíhou搬bān的de？为什么wèishénme突然tūrán搬家bānjiā？”
My friend asked me: "When did you move? Why so suddenly?"
我wǒ说shuō：“上个月shàng ge yuè搬bān的de，因为yīnwèi老lǎo房子fángzi太tài旧jiù了le，我们wǒmen决定juédìng搬bān走zǒu。”
I said: "Last month — because the old place was too run-down, we decided to move out."
我wǒ告诉gàosu她tā新xīn地址dìzhǐ，让ràng她tā周末zhōumò来lái我wǒ家jiā玩wán。
I gave her my new address and told her to come visit me on the weekend.
打完dǎwán电话diànhuà，我wǒ开始kāishǐ收拾shōushi房间fángjiān的de书架shūjià。
After finishing the call, I started tidying up the bookshelf in my room.

Key words 生词

房租fángzūrent
地址dìzhǐaddress
搬家bānjiāto move house
附近fùjìnnearby
决定juédìngto decide
房间fángjiānroom

Grammar note 语法点

离 + place + 很近/远（distance from a place）

离 marks the reference point when saying how close or far something is.

新家离地铁站很近。— The new home is very close to the subway station.
虽然...但是...（although...but...）

虽然 introduces a fact, and 但是 introduces the contrast that follows.

虽然房租贵了一点，但是附近很方便。— Although the rent is a bit more, the area is very convenient.
让 + someone + verb（to have/let someone do something）

让 introduces the person and the action they are caused or allowed to do.

我告诉她新地址，让她周末来我家玩。— I gave her my new address and told her to come visit on the weekend.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Where is the narrator's new home located?
2. Why did the narrator decide to move out of the old place?
3. What does the narrator do after telling the friend the new address?
