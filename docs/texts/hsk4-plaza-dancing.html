广场舞

Guǎngchǎng wǔ
Plaza Dancing · 150 words · ~2 min
每天měi tiān晚上wǎnshang，公园gōngyuán里li都dōu会huì准时zhǔnshí响起xiǎngqǐ熟悉shúxi的de音乐yīnyuè，一yì群qún阿姨āyí跳起tiàoqǐ了le广场舞guǎngchǎng wǔ。
Every evening, familiar music starts playing right on time in the park, and a group of aunties begin dancing plaza dance.
她们tāmen的de动作dòngzuò虽然suīrán简单jiǎndān，但是dànshì跳tiào得de整整齐齐zhěngzhěngqíqí，看起来kànqǐlai十分shífēn开心kāixīn。
Their movements are simple, but they dance in perfect unison, and they look very happy.
广场舞guǎngchǎng wǔ不但búdàn能néng锻炼duànliàn身体shēntǐ，而且érqiě给gěi退休tuìxiū以后yǐhòu的de生活shēnghuó增加zēngjiā了le不少bùshǎo乐趣lèqù。
Plaza dancing not only exercises the body, but also adds a lot of joy to life after retirement.
可是kěshì，音乐yīnyuè的de声音shēngyīn太tài大dà，让ràng住zhù在zài附近fùjìn的de人rén觉得juéde又yòu吵chǎo又yòu烦fán。
But the music is too loud, making people who live nearby feel both noisy and annoyed.
于是yúshì，广场舞guǎngchǎng wǔ慢慢mànmàn变成biànchéng了le一yí个ge引起yǐnqǐ争论zhēnglùn的de话题huàtí：到底dàodǐ是shì健康jiànkāng活动huódòng，还是háishi扰民rǎomín行为xíngwéi？
So, plaza dancing has slowly become a controversial topic: is it a healthy activity, or is it disturbing the residents?
有的yǒude社区shèqū想出xiǎngchū了le办法bànfǎ，比如bǐrú规定guīdìng跳舞tiàowǔ的de时间shíjiān和hé音量yīnliàng，才cái让ràng矛盾máodùn慢慢mànmàn减少jiǎnshǎo。
Some communities have come up with solutions, such as setting rules for dancing hours and volume, which has gradually reduced the conflict.
说到底shuōdàodǐ，广场舞guǎngchǎng wǔ反映fǎnyìng的de是shì老年人lǎoniánrén渴望kěwàng社交shèjiāo、渴望kěwàng被bèi看见kànjiàn的de心情xīnqíng。
When it comes down to it, plaza dancing reflects elderly people's longing to socialize and to be seen.
我wǒ觉得juéde，只要zhǐyào大家dàjiā互相hùxiāng理解lǐjiě，广场舞guǎngchǎng wǔ完全wánquán可以kěyǐ成为chéngwéi城市chéngshì里li一yí道dào温暖wēnnuǎn的de风景fēngjǐng。
I think, as long as everyone understands each other, plaza dancing can completely become a warm scene in the city.

Grammar note 语法点

不但……而且……

"Not only A, but also B" adds a second, often stronger point: 广场舞不但能锻炼身体，而且给退休以后的生活增加了不少乐趣。

这个办法不但简单，而且很有效。— This method is not only simple, but also very effective.
又……又……

"Both A and B" describes two qualities present at the same time: 让住在附近的人觉得又吵又烦。

这家餐厅的菜又好吃又便宜。— This restaurant's food is both delicious and cheap.

Check yourself 小测验

1. According to the text, what benefit does plaza dancing bring to retirees?
2. Why has plaza dancing become a controversial topic?
3. What does the author think plaza dancing reflects?
