开会KāihuìIn a Meeting in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 90 words · ~2 min
今天jīntiān上午shàngwǔ九点jiǔ diǎn，公司gōngsī有yǒu一个yí ge重要zhòngyào的de会议huìyì。
At nine o'clock this morning, the company had an important meeting.
我wǒ早上zǎoshang很hěn早zǎo就jiù到dào了le公司gōngsī，准备zhǔnbèi开会kāihuì用yòng的de东西dōngxi。
I got to the company very early to prepare things for the meeting.
会议huìyì开始kāishǐ的时候de shíhou，小王Xiǎo Wáng还hái没有méiyǒu来lái，经理jīnglǐ有点儿yǒudiǎnr不bù高兴gāoxìng。
When the meeting started, Xiao Wang hadn't arrived yet, and the manager was a little unhappy.
过了guò le十分钟shí fēnzhōng，小王Xiǎo Wáng才cái跑pǎo进来jìnlái，一边yìbiān说shuō对不起duìbuqǐ，一边yìbiān坐下zuòxià。
Ten minutes later, Xiao Wang finally ran in, saying sorry while sitting down.
我们wǒmen在zài会上huì shang讨论tǎolùn了le下个月xià ge yuè的de工作gōngzuò计划jìhuà。
We discussed next month's work plan at the meeting.
经理jīnglǐ说shuō，这个zhè ge计划jìhuà很hěn重要zhòngyào，希望xīwàng大家dàjiā都dōu努力nǔlì做好zuòhǎo。
The manager said this plan was important, and hoped everyone would work hard to do it well.
开完会kāiwán huì以后yǐhòu，我们wǒmen都dōu觉得juéde这次zhè cì会议huìyì很hěn有用yǒuyòng。
After the meeting ended, we all felt this meeting was very useful.

Key words 生词

会议huìyìmeeting

计划jìhuàplan
经理jīnglǐmanager

Grammar note 语法点

一边……一边…… (doing two things at once)

一边 + verb1，一边 + verb2 describes two actions happening at the same time.

小王才跑进来，一边说对不起，一边坐下。 — Xiao Wang finally ran in, saying sorry while sitting down.
还没有 + verb (haven't yet)

还没有 (or 还没) before a verb shows that an action hasn't happened yet, even though it was expected.

会议开始的时候，小王还没有来。 — When the meeting started, Xiao Wang hadn't arrived yet.
希望 + clause (to hope that...)

希望 can be directly followed by a full clause describing what someone hopes will happen.

经理说，这个计划很重要，希望大家都努力做好。 — The manager said this plan was important, and hoped everyone would work hard to do it well.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What happened when the meeting started?
2. What did they discuss at the meeting?
3. How did they feel after the meeting?
