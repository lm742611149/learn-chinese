我的电脑Wǒ de diànnǎoMy Computer

HSK 1 reading practice · 38 words · ~1 min
我wǒ有yǒu一个yí ge电脑diànnǎo，电脑diànnǎo在zài桌子zhuōzi上shàng。
I have a computer; the computer is on the desk.
我wǒ在zài电脑diànnǎo上shàng看kàn电影diànyǐng。
I watch movies on the computer.
我wǒ也yě在zài电脑diànnǎo上shàng看kàn书shū。
I also read books on the computer.
爸爸bàba也yě喜欢xǐhuan我wǒ的de电脑diànnǎo。
Dad also likes my computer.
下午xiàwǔ我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ看kàn电视diànshì。
In the afternoon we watch TV together.

Grammar note 语法点

在 + place + 上 — on top of something

在 marks the location, and 上 means 'on'; together 在电脑上 means 'on the computer'.

我在电脑上看电影。 — I watch movies on the computer.
也 — also

也 goes right before the verb to add another true statement.

爸爸也喜欢我的电脑。 — Dad also likes my computer.
一起 — together

一起 comes before the verb to show that people do something jointly.

我们一起看电视。 — We watch TV together.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Where is the computer?
2. What does the family do in the afternoon?
3. What does dad like?
