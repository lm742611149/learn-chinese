游泳YóuyǒngTalking About Swimming in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 95 words · ~2 min
我wǒ以前yǐqián不bú会huì游泳yóuyǒng，但是dànshì我wǒ丈夫zhàngfu游yóu得de非常fēicháng好hǎo。
I couldn't swim before, but my husband swims very well.
他tā对duì我wǒ说shuō：“游泳yóuyǒng对duì身体shēntǐ很hěn好hǎo，你nǐ和hé我wǒ一起yìqǐ去qù吧ba！”
He said to me, "Swimming is good for your health — come with me!"
上个月shàng ge yuè我们wǒmen开始kāishǐ每měi个ge星期xīngqī去qù游yóu两liǎng次cì。
Last month we started going swimming twice every week.
开始kāishǐ的de时候shíhou，我wǒ游yóu一yì分钟fēnzhōng就jiù累lèi了le。
At the beginning, I got tired after swimming for just one minute.
现在xiànzài我wǒ已经yǐjīng能néng游yóu很hěn长cháng时间shíjiān了le。
Now I can already swim for a long time.
昨天zuótiān丈夫zhàngfu看kàn我wǒ游泳yóuyǒng，他tā笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“你nǐ游yóu得de真zhēn好hǎo！”
Yesterday my husband watched me swim, and he said with a smile, "You swim really well!"
我wǒ觉得juéde游泳yóuyǒng是shì最zuì快乐kuàilè的de运动yùndòng。
I think swimming is the happiest sport of all.

Key words 生词

游泳yóuyǒngto swim; swimming
丈夫zhàngfuhusband
会huìcan (learned skill)
次cìtime (occurrence)
累lèitired
最zuìthe most

Grammar note 语法点

会 — a skill you have learned

会 means 'can' for learned skills like swimming or speaking a language: 我以前不会游泳，现在会了.

我以前不会游泳。— I couldn't swim before.
次 — measure word for times

次 counts how many times something happens; the number goes before it: 两次 (twice), 每个星期去两次.

我们每个星期去游两次。— We go swimming twice every week.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Who swims very well?
2. How often do they go swimming?
3. What does the writer think of swimming now?
