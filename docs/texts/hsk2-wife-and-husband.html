妻子和丈夫Qīzi hé zhàngfuWife and Husband

HSK 2 reading practice · 69 words · ~1 min
我wǒ丈夫zhàngfu每天měitiān六点liù diǎn起床qǐchuáng，去qù公司gōngsī上班shàngbān。
My husband gets up at six every day and goes to work.
我wǒ是shì妻子qīzi，我wǒ在家zài jiā做饭zuòfàn。
I'm the wife, and I cook at home.
晚上wǎnshang，丈夫zhàngfu回家huí jiā的时候de shíhou，我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ吃饭chīfàn。
In the evening, when my husband comes home, we eat together.
他tā喜欢xǐhuan吃chī鱼yú，我wǒ喜欢xǐhuan吃chī菜cài，我们wǒmen做zuò的de菜cài不一样bù yíyàng。
He likes to eat fish and I like to eat vegetables, so the dishes we make are different.
周末zhōumò，我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ去qù公园gōngyuán走走zǒuzou，也yě一起yìqǐ看kàn电影diànyǐng。
On weekends, we go for a walk in the park together, and we also watch movies together.
我们wǒmen工作gōngzuò都dōu很hěn忙máng，但是dànshì我们wǒmen都dōu很hěn高兴gāoxìng。
We're both busy with work, but we're both happy.

Grammar note 语法点

的时候 — 'when ~'

V/clause + 的时候 marks 'when doing X' or 'at the time of X': 丈夫回家的时候 = 'when my husband comes home.'

丈夫回家的时候，我们一起吃饭。— When my husband comes home, we eat together.
都 — 'both/all' with a plural subject

都 follows a plural subject like 我们 or 他们 to mean 'both/all': 我们都很忙 = 'we are both busy.'

我们工作都很忙，但是我们都很高兴。— We're both busy with work, but we're both happy.
一样 / 不一样 — 'the same' / 'different'

一样 means 'the same'; adding 不 gives 不一样, 'different': 我们做的菜不一样。

我们做的菜不一样。— The dishes we make are different.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What time does the husband get up?
2. What does the wife like to eat?
3. What do they do on weekends?
