无现金社会

Wú xiànjīn shèhuì
The Cashless Society · 148 words · ~2 min
在zài中国Zhōngguó的de大dà城市chéngshì，用yòng现金xiànjīn的de人rén越来越yuè lái yuè少shǎo了le。
In China's big cities, fewer and fewer people use cash.
买mǎi菜cài、坐zuò车chē、看病kànbìng，只要zhǐyào拿出náchū手机shǒujī扫一扫sǎo yi sǎo，几jǐ秒钟miǎozhōng就jiù付fù好hǎo了le钱qián。
Buying groceries, taking the bus, seeing a doctor — as long as you take out your phone and scan, the payment is done in a few seconds.
我wǒ上shàng个ge月yuè去qù旅行lǚxíng，钱包qiánbāo一yí次cì也yě没méi打开dǎkāi过guo。
Last month I went traveling, and I never opened my wallet even once.
不bú带dài现金xiànjīn当然dāngrán方便fāngbiàn，也yě更gèng安全ānquán，听说tīngshuō连lián小偷xiǎotōu都dōu快kuài找zhǎo不bu到dào工作gōngzuò了le。
Not carrying cash is of course convenient, and safer too — they say even thieves are about to be out of a job.
但是dànshì，无wú现金xiànjīn社会shèhuì也yě有yǒu问题wèntí：很多hěn duō老人lǎorén不bú会huì用yòng手机shǒujī付fù钱qián，买mǎi东西dōngxi的de时候shíhou特别tèbié着急zháojí。
But a cashless society has problems too: many elderly people don't know how to pay by phone and get really anxious when shopping.
还有háiyǒu，手机shǒujī没méi电diàn的de时候shíhou，你nǐ连lián一yì瓶píng水shuǐ都dōu买mǎi不bu了liǎo。
And when your phone runs out of battery, you can't even buy a bottle of water.
尽管jǐnguǎn有yǒu这些zhèxiē问题wèntí，我wǒ还是háishi觉得juéde无wú现金xiànjīn是shì不bú会huì改变gǎibiàn的de方向fāngxiàng。
Despite these problems, I still think going cashless is a direction that won't change.
技术jìshù应该yīnggāi等一等děng yi děng走zǒu得de慢màn的de人rén，而ér不是bú shì把bǎ他们tāmen留liú在zài后面hòumiàn。
Technology should wait a bit for those who walk more slowly, instead of leaving them behind.

Grammar note 语法点

连……都/也……

"Even ..." highlights an extreme case to stress the point: 连小偷都快找不到工作了；连一瓶水都买不了。

他忙得连饭都忘了吃。— He was so busy he even forgot to eat.
尽管……，还是……

"Although / despite A, still B" admits a fact but keeps the conclusion: 尽管有这些问题，我还是觉得方向不会改变。

尽管下着大雨，他还是准时到了。— Despite the heavy rain, he still arrived on time.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What happened to the author's wallet during last month's trip?
2. Who gets especially anxious when shopping in a cashless society?
3. What does the author think technology should do?
