四大发明

Sì dà fāmíng
The Four Great Inventions · 190 words · ~3 min
你nǐ每天měitiān用yòng的de纸zhǐ、手机shǒujī里li的de地图dìtú，其实qíshí都dōu和hé一千yìqiān多duō年nián前qián的de中国Zhōngguó发明fāmíng有yǒu关系guānxi。
The paper you use every day and the map in your phone are actually connected to Chinese inventions from more than a thousand years ago.
中国Zhōngguó古代gǔdài的de四sì大dà发明fāmíng，是shì由yóu造纸术zàozhǐshù、印刷术yìnshuāshù、火药huǒyào和hé指南针zhǐnánzhēn组成zǔchéng的de。
China's Four Great Inventions of ancient times are made up of papermaking, printing, gunpowder, and the compass.
在zài纸zhǐ发明fāmíng以前yǐqián，人们rénmen把bǎ字zì写xiě在zài竹子zhúzi上shang，一yì本běn书shū重zhòng得de要yào用yòng车chē来lái拉lā。
Before paper was invented, people wrote characters on bamboo, and a single book was so heavy that a cart was needed to pull it.
汉代Hàndài的de蔡伦Cài Lún改进gǎijìn了le造纸术zàozhǐshù，从此cóngcǐ普通pǔtōng人rén也yě读dú得起deqǐ书shū了le。
Cai Lun of the Han dynasty improved papermaking, and from then on ordinary people could also afford to read books.
印刷术yìnshuāshù出现chūxiàn以后yǐhòu，书shū变biàn得de越来越yuèláiyuè便宜piányi，知识zhīshi也yě很hěn快kuài传chuán到dào了le更gèng多duō人rén的de手shǒu里li。
After printing appeared, books became cheaper and cheaper, and knowledge quickly spread into the hands of more people.
火药huǒyào是shì人们rénmen找zhǎo药yào的de时候shíhou偶然ǒurán发现fāxiàn的de，后来hòulái却què变成biànchéng了le节日jiérì里li美丽měilì的de烟花yānhuā。
Gunpowder was discovered by chance while people were searching for medicine, yet later it turned into the beautiful fireworks of festivals.
指南针zhǐnánzhēn帮助bāngzhù船chuán在zài大海dàhǎi上shang找到zhǎodào方向fāngxiàng，凡是fánshì去qù远方yuǎnfāng的de船chuán，都dōu离不开líbukāi它tā。
The compass helped ships find their direction on the sea; every ship that sailed far away could not do without it.
今天jīntiān手机shǒujī里li的de地图dìtú，其实qíshí就jiù是shì指南针zhǐnánzhēn的de“孙子sūnzi”。
The map in today's mobile phone is really the compass's "grandson".
一yì张zhāng纸zhǐ、一yì本běn书shū、一yí次cì旅行lǚxíng，都dōu在zài提醒tíxǐng我们wǒmen：伟大wěidà的de发明fāmíng往往wǎngwǎng来自láizì生活shēnghuó中zhōng的de小xiǎo问题wèntí。
A sheet of paper, a book, a trip — they all remind us that great inventions often come from small problems in daily life.

Grammar note 语法点

由…组成 (to be made up of…)

由 introduces the parts, and 组成 states that they form the whole: 四大发明是由造纸术、印刷术、火药和指南针组成的。

这个班由二十个学生组成。 — This class is made up of twenty students.
凡是…都… (every…, all without exception)

凡是 marks a whole category, and 都 confirms that the statement covers every member: 凡是去远方的船，都离不开它。

凡是来过这里的人，都喜欢这个城市。 — Everyone who has been here loves this city.
V + 得起 (can afford to)

A potential complement showing one has the money or ability for the action: 普通人也读得起书了。

现在大家都买得起手机了。 — Now everyone can afford to buy a mobile phone.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What are the Four Great Inventions made up of?
2. What changed after Cai Lun improved papermaking?
3. How was gunpowder discovered?
