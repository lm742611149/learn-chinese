HSK 4 · Upper Int.

直播带货

Zhíbò dàihuò
Livestream Shopping · 165 words · ~3 min
打开dǎkāi手机shǒujī，随时suíshí都dōu能néng看到kàndào有人yǒurén在zài直播间zhíbōjiān里li介绍jièshào产品chǎnpǐn，一边yìbiān说shuō一边yìbiān卖mài。
Open your phone, and at any time you can see someone in a livestream room introducing products, talking and selling at the same time.
主播zhǔbō的de声音shēngyīn又yòu快kuài又yòu激动jīdòng，常常chángcháng一yì句jù“最后zuìhòu三sān分钟fēnzhōng”就jiù让ràng很多hěn duō人rén马上mǎshàng下单xiàdān。
The host's voice is fast and excited, and often just one line — "last three minutes" — makes many people place an order right away.
直播带货zhíbò dàihuò最大zuì dà的de好处hǎochu是shì便宜piányi，因为yīnwèi省去shěngqù了le商店shāngdiàn和hé中间商zhōngjiānshāng的de费用fèiyòng。
The biggest advantage of livestream shopping is that it's cheap, because it saves on the costs of shops and middlemen.
而且érqiě，你nǐ可以kěyǐ一边yìbiān看kàn主播zhǔbō试用shìyòng产品chǎnpǐn，一边yìbiān提问tíwèn，比bǐ在zài网上wǎngshàng看kàn图片túpiàn更gèng让ràng人rén放心fàngxīn。
Moreover, you can watch the host try out the product while asking questions, which feels more reassuring than just looking at pictures online.
然而rán'ér，有些yǒuxiē人rén一yì到dào深夜shēnyè还hái在zài看kàn直播zhíbō，一不小心yì bù xiǎoxīn就jiù买mǎi了le一yì堆duī根本gēnběn不bù需要xūyào的de东西dōngxi。
However, some people are still watching livestreams deep into the night, and before they know it, they've bought a pile of things they don't need at all.
更gèng让ràng人rén担心dānxīn的是de shì，个别gèbié主播zhǔbō为了wèile卖货màihuò而ér夸大kuādà产品chǎnpǐn效果xiàoguǒ，甚至shènzhì卖mài假货jiǎhuò。
What's even more worrying is that a few hosts exaggerate product effects in order to sell goods, and even sell fake products.
既然jìrán直播带货zhíbò dàihuò已经yǐjīng成chéng了le一yì种zhǒng购物gòuwù习惯xíguàn，消费者xiāofèizhě就jiù更gèng需要xūyào保持bǎochí理性lǐxìng，别bié被bèi气氛qìfēn冲昏chōnghūn了le头tóu。
Since livestream shopping has already become a shopping habit, consumers need to stay even more rational and not get carried away by the atmosphere.
说到底shuōdàodǐ，直播带货zhíbò dàihuò本身běnshēn没有méiyǒu问题wèntí，问题wèntí在于zàiyú我们wǒmen是不是shì bú shì清楚qīngchu自己zìjǐ真正zhēnzhèng需要xūyào什么shénme。
When it comes down to it, livestream shopping itself is not the problem — the problem is whether we are clear about what we truly need.

Grammar note 语法点

一边……一边……

"Doing A while doing B" describes two actions happening at the same time: 你可以一边看主播试用产品，一边提问。

他一边吃饭，一边看手机。— He eats while looking at his phone.
在于

"(The key/problem) lies in..." points to where the real cause is: 问题在于我们是不是清楚自己真正需要什么。

成功的关键在于坚持。— The key to success lies in persistence.
