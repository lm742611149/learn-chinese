HSK 1 · Newbie

请坐，请喝茶Qǐng zuò, qǐng hē cháPlease Sit, Please Have Tea

HSK 1 reading practice · 40 words · ~1 min
朋友péngyou来lái我wǒ家jiā，我wǒ很hěn高兴gāoxìng。
My friend comes to my home, and I am very happy.
我wǒ请qǐng朋友péngyou坐zuò，请qǐng他tā喝hē茶chá。
I ask my friend to sit, and offer him tea.
朋友péngyou坐下zuòxià，喝hē了le茶chá。
My friend sat down, and drank the tea.
朋友péngyou说shuō谢谢xièxie，我wǒ说shuō不客气bú kèqi。
My friend says thank you, and I say you're welcome.
我们wǒmen说话shuōhuà，很hěn高兴gāoxìng。
We talk, and are very happy.

Key words 生词

请qǐngplease
坐zuòto sit
喝hēto drink
茶chátea
谢谢xièxiethank you
不客气bú kèqiyou're welcome

Grammar note 语法点

请 + verb — a polite request

请 before a verb softly asks someone to do something, like "please" in English.

我请朋友坐，请他喝茶。 — I ask my friend to sit, and offer him tea.
了 — completed action

了 right after the verb shows the action already happened.

朋友坐下，喝了茶。 — My friend sat down, and drank the tea.
谢谢 / 不客气 — thank you / you're welcome

This pair of set phrases is the standard exchange for thanks in Chinese.

朋友说谢谢，我说不客气。 — My friend says thank you, and I say you're welcome.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the narrator ask the friend to do first?
2. What does the friend drink?
3. What does the friend say after drinking the tea?
