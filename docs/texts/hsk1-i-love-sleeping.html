HSK 1 · Newbie

我爱睡觉

Wǒ ài shuìjiào
I Love Sleeping · 74 words · ~1 min
我wǒ叫jiào李明Lǐ Míng，我wǒ很hěn爱ài睡觉shuìjiào。
My name is Li Ming, and I really love sleeping.
我wǒ家jiā有yǒu猫māo，猫māo和hé我wǒ都dōu爱ài睡觉shuìjiào。
There is a cat at my home — the cat and I both love sleeping.
今天jīntiān上午shàngwǔ我wǒ睡觉shuìjiào，中午zhōngwǔ我wǒ睡觉shuìjiào，下午xiàwǔ我wǒ想xiǎng睡觉shuìjiào。
This morning I slept, at noon I slept, and in the afternoon I want to sleep.
妈妈māma说shuō：“现在xiànzài不bù能néng睡觉shuìjiào，去qù学习xuéxí！”
Mom says, "You may not sleep now — go study!"
我wǒ看kàn书shū的时候de shíhou，我wǒ的de猫māo在zài我wǒ的de书shū上shàng睡觉shuìjiào。
When I read my book, my cat sleeps on my book.
我wǒ很hěn高兴gāoxìng，我wǒ的de猫māo太tài好hǎo了le！
I am very happy — my cat is so great!

Key words 生词

睡觉shuìjiàoto sleep
爱àito love
猫māocat
想xiǎngto want to
学习xuéxíto study
时候shíhoutime; moment

Grammar note 语法点

想 + verb — want to

想 before a verb means 'want to' or 'would like to' do something.

下午我想睡觉。 — In the afternoon I want to sleep.
太…了 — so / too

Put an adjective between 太 and 了 to say something is 'so …' or 'too …'. It often shows strong feeling.

我的猫太好了！ — My cat is so great!

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does Li Ming love doing?
2. Who also loves sleeping?
3. What does Mom tell him to do?
