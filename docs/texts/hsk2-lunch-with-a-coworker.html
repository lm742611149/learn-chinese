和同事吃午饭Hé tóngshì chī wǔfànLunch with a Coworker

HSK 2 reading practice · 90 words · ~2 min
中午zhōngwǔ十二shí'èr点diǎn，我wǒ和hé同事tóngshì一起yìqǐ去qù吃chī午饭wǔfàn。
At noon, my coworker and I went to have lunch together.
公司gōngsī旁边pángbiān有yǒu一yì家jiā小xiǎo饭馆fànguǎn，那儿nàr的de菜cài很hěn有名yǒumíng。
Next to the company there's a small restaurant, and its dishes are famous.
同事tóngshì问wèn我wǒ：“你nǐ想xiǎng吃chī米饭mǐfàn还是háishi面条miàntiáo？”
My coworker asked me: "Would you like to eat rice or noodles?"
我wǒ说shuō：“都dōu可以kěyǐ，我wǒ不bù挑食tiāoshí。”
I said: "Either is fine, I'm not picky about food."
我们wǒmen点diǎn了le两liǎng个gè菜cài，还hái要yào了le一yì碗wǎn汤tāng。
We ordered two dishes, and also asked for a bowl of soup.
吃饭chīfàn的de时候shíhou，我们wǒmen聊liáo了le聊liáo公司gōngsī的de新xīn项目xiàngmù。
While eating, we chatted a bit about the company's new project.
这zhè顿dùn午饭wǔfàn很hěn简单jiǎndān，但是dànshì我们wǒmen都dōu很hěn开心kāixīn。
This lunch was simple, but we were both very happy.

Grammar note 语法点

还是 — 'or' in a question

还是 links two choices inside a question, e.g. 你想吃米饭还是面条？

你想吃米饭还是面条？ — Would you like rice or noodles?
V + 了 + V — doing something briefly

Repeating a verb with 了 in between (聊了聊, 看了看) shows the action was brief or casual.

我们聊了聊公司的新项目。 — We chatted a bit about the company's new project.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Where did the writer and coworker eat lunch?
2. Did the writer care whether it was rice or noodles?
3. What did they talk about while eating?
