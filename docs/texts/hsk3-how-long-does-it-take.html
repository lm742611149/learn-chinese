要多长时间Yào duō cháng shíjiānHow Long Does It Take in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 111 words · ~2 min
我wǒ打算dǎsuàn周末zhōumò去qù看kàn一个yí ge朋友péngyou，他tā住zhù在zài别的biéde城市chéngshì。
I was planning to visit a friend over the weekend — he lives in another city.
我wǒ去qù火车站huǒchēzhàn，问wèn服务员fúwùyuán：“坐zuò火车huǒchē去qù，要yào多duō长cháng时间shíjiān？”
I went to the train station and asked the clerk: "How long does it take to get there by train?"
服务员fúwùyuán说shuō：“坐zuò快kuài车chē，两liǎng个gè小时xiǎoshí就jiù到dào了le。”
The clerk said, "On the fast train it's two hours."
我wǒ买mǎi了le一yì张zhāng星期六xīngqīliù早上zǎoshang的de火车huǒchē票piào。
I bought a Saturday morning train ticket.
火车huǒchē开kāi得de很hěn快kuài，我wǒ在zài车chē上shàng看kàn了le一会儿yíhuìr书shū，又yòu看kàn了le一会儿yíhuìr手机shǒujī。
The train went really fast — I read for a while on the train, then looked at my phone for a while.
才cái过guò了le两liǎng个ge小时xiǎoshí，火车huǒchē真的zhēnde到dào了le。
Only two hours had passed, and the train had really arrived.
我wǒ出来chūlái的de时候shíhou，朋友péngyou已经yǐjīng在zài门口ménkǒu等děng我wǒ了le，说shuō我wǒ来lái得de正好zhènghǎo。
When I came out, my friend was already waiting at the door, and said I'd arrived just in time.

Grammar note 语法点

要多长时间？(How long does it take?)

多长时间 asks about duration — how long something takes — while 多远 asks about distance instead.

坐火车去，要多长时间？— How long does it take to get there by train?
才 + time period（only; not until — something is less than expected）

才 before a time expression emphasizes that only that much time had passed, showing it felt quick.

才过了两个小时，火车真的到了。— Only two hours had passed, and the train had really arrived.
V + 得 + adjective（complement describing how an action turned out）

来得正好 uses 得 to attach a description of the result — arriving right on time.

朋友已经在门口等我了，说我来得正好。— My friend was already waiting at the door, and said I'd arrived just in time.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the narrator ask the train station clerk?
2. According to the clerk, how long does the trip take with no traffic?
3. What does the friend say when the narrator arrives?
