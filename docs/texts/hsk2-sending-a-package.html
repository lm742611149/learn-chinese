HSK 2 · Elementary

寄快递

Jì kuàidì
Sending a Package · 97 words · ~2 min
我wǒ买mǎi了le一yì本běn书shū给gěi妈妈māma，我wǒ想xiǎng寄jì快递kuàidì给gěi她tā。
I bought a book for my mom, and I want to send it to her by courier.
快递公司kuàidì gōngsī在zài超市chāoshì旁边pángbiān，离lí我wǒ家jiā不bù太tài远yuǎn。
The delivery company is next to the supermarket, not too far from my home.
工作人员gōngzuò rényuán问wèn我wǒ：“您nín要yào寄jì到dào哪儿nǎr？”
The staff member asked me: "Where would you like to send it to?"
我wǒ说shuō：“寄jì到dào北京Běijīng，我wǒ妈妈māma住zhù在zài那儿nàr。”
I said: "Send it to Beijing — my mom lives there."
她tā说shuō因为yīnwèi东西dōngxi不bù重zhòng，所以suǒyǐ只zhǐ要yào二十èrshí块kuài钱qián。
She said because the item isn't heavy, it only costs twenty kuai.
我wǒ付fù了le钱qián，工作人员gōngzuò rényuán给gěi了le我wǒ一yì张zhāng单子dānzi。
I paid, and the staff member gave me a receipt slip.
她tā说shuō三sān天tiān以后yǐhòu，妈妈māma就jiù能néng收到shōudào这zhè个gè包裹bāoguǒ了le。
She said in three days my mom would be able to receive the package.

Key words 生词

快递kuàidìexpress delivery; courier
快递公司kuàidì gōngsīdelivery company
工作人员gōngzuò rényuánstaff member
包裹bāoguǒpackage
重zhòngheavy
单子dānzireceipt slip

Grammar note 语法点

因为…，所以… — cause and result

因为 gives the reason and 所以 gives the result; both can appear together in the same sentence.

因为东西不重，所以只要二十块钱。 — Because the item isn't heavy, it only costs twenty kuai.
V + 到 — reaching a destination

到 right after a verb like 寄, 走 or 送 marks the place the action reaches.

快递寄到北京。 — The package is sent to Beijing.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Who is the writer sending the package to?
2. Why does the package only cost twenty kuai?
3. How many days until the mom receives the package?
