HSK 2 · Elementary

请说慢一点Qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎnPlease Speak Slowly in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 80 words · ~1 min
爷爷yéye打电话dǎ diànhuà给gěi我wǒ，说话shuōhuà很hěn快kuài，我wǒ听不懂tīng bu dǒng。
Grandpa calls me; he talks very fast, and I can't understand him.
我wǒ说shuō：“爷爷yéye，请qǐng说shuō慢màn一点yìdiǎn。”
I say: "Grandpa, please speak a little slower."
爷爷yéye慢慢mànmàn说shuō，可是kěshì我wǒ还hái没méi听tīng懂dǒng他tā说shuō的de名字míngzi。
Grandpa speaks slowly, but I still hadn't understood the name he said.
我wǒ说shuō：“爷爷yéye，请qǐng再zài说shuō一次yí cì。”
I say: "Grandpa, please say it again."
爷爷yéye又yòu说shuō了le一次yí cì，我wǒ听tīng懂dǒng了le，他tā要yào来lái我wǒ家jiā。
Grandpa said it again, and I understood: he's coming to my house.
我wǒ马上mǎshàng告诉gàosu家人jiārén：“爷爷yéye要yào来lái了le！”
I tell my family right away: "Grandpa is coming!"

Key words 生词

请qǐngplease
慢mànslow
一点yìdiǎna little
再zàiagain
一次yí cìone more time
听不懂tīng bu dǒngcan't understand (by listening)

Grammar note 语法点

请 + V + 一点（please do a bit more...）

请 makes a request polite; adding 一点 after 慢 softens it into "a bit slower."

请说慢一点。— Please speak a little slower.
请再 + V + 一次（please do it again）

再...一次 asks someone to repeat an action one more time.

请再说一次。— Please say it again.
听不懂（can't understand by listening）

听不懂 means you heard the words but the meaning didn't get through — different from just not hearing at all.

我听不懂，请说慢一点。— I don't understand; please speak a little slower.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Why can't the narrator understand Grandpa at first?
2. What does the narrator ask for after 请说慢一点?
3. What was Grandpa's news?
