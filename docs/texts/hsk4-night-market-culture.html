HSK 4 · Upper Int.

夜市文化

Yèshì wénhuà
Night Market Culture · 155 words · ~3 min
一yī到dào晚上wǎnshang，很多hěn duō城市chéngshì的de老街lǎojiē就jiù热闹rènao起来qǐlai，因为yīnwèi夜市yèshì开始kāishǐ了le。
As soon as evening falls, the old streets in many cities become lively, because the night market has begun.
无论wúlùn你nǐ想xiǎng吃chī小吃xiǎochī，还是háishi想xiǎng买mǎi便宜piányi的de衣服yīfu，夜市yèshì都dōu能néng满足mǎnzú你nǐ。
Whether you want to eat snacks or buy cheap clothes, the night market can satisfy you.
我wǒ最zuì喜欢xǐhuan的de，是shì空气kōngqì里li那种nà zhǒng炸鸡zhájī和hé烤肉kǎoròu混hùn在一起zài yìqǐ的de香味xiāngwèi。
What I like best is that fragrance in the air where fried chicken and grilled meat mix together.
有意思yǒu yìsi的是de shì，夜市yèshì不只是bùzhǐshì吃饭chīfàn购物gòuwù的de地方dìfang，也yě是shì年轻人niánqīngrén拍照pāizhào、聊天liáotiān、约会yuēhuì的de地方dìfang。
What's interesting is that the night market is not just a place to eat and shop, but also a place for young people to take photos, chat, and go on dates.
既然jìrán白天báitiān大家dàjiā都dōu在zài上班shàngbān上学shàngxué，晚上wǎnshang出来chūlai放松fàngsōng一下yíxià，也yě就jiù成chéng了le一yì种zhǒng生活shēnghuó习惯xíguàn。
Since everyone is at work or school during the day, coming out to relax in the evening has become a lifestyle habit.
不过búguò，随着suízhe城市chéngshì越来越yuè lái yuè现代化xiàndàihuà，一些yìxiē老lǎo夜市yèshì因为yīnwèi要yào拆迁chāiqiān而ér慢慢mànmàn消失xiāoshī了le。
However, as cities become more and more modernized, some old night markets are slowly disappearing due to redevelopment.
于是yúshì，有人yǒurén开始kāishǐ担心dānxīn：如果rúguǒ夜市yèshì都dōu变成biànchéng了le商场shāngchǎng，那种nà zhǒng热闹rènao又yòu亲切qīnqiè的de感觉gǎnjué还hái能néng找到zhǎodào吗ma？
So, some people have begun to worry: if all night markets turn into shopping malls, will that lively yet warm feeling still be found?
我wǒ觉得juéde，夜市yèshì代表dàibiǎo的de不只是bùzhǐshì美食měishí，而是érshì一yí座zuò城市chéngshì晚上wǎnshang的de性格xìnggé。
I think what a night market represents is not just delicious food, but the character of a city at night.

Grammar note 语法点

无论……都……

"No matter (whether) A or B, the result C still holds." It shows that a condition doesn't change the outcome: 无论你想吃小吃，还是想买便宜的衣服，夜市都能满足你。

无论多忙，他都会给妈妈打电话。— No matter how busy he is, he always calls his mom.
既然……也就……

"Since (a fact is already true), then naturally..." draws a conclusion from an accepted premise: 既然白天大家都在上班上学，晚上出来放松一下，也就成了一种生活习惯。

既然你已经决定了，我也就不再劝你了。— Since you've already decided, I won't try to persuade you anymore.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the author like best about the night market?
2. According to the text, why do some old night markets disappear?
3. What does the author think a night market represents in the end?
