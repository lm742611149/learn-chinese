HSK 6 · Fluent

道法自然

Dào fǎ zìrán
The Way Follows Nature · 198 words · ~3 min
《道德经Dàodéjīng》里lǐ“道法自然dào fǎ zìrán”这zhè四sì个ge字zì，常cháng被bèi译yì成chéng“顺其自然shùn qí zìrán”，其实qíshí远yuǎn比bǐ这样zhèyàng的de翻译fānyì复杂fùzá。
The four characters 道法自然 from the Dao De Jing are often rendered as "let nature take its course," but they are in fact far more complicated than that translation suggests.
老子Lǎozǐ所suǒ说shuō的de“自然zìrán”，并不是bìng bú shì山川shānchuān草木cǎomù，而是érshì事物shìwù“本来如此běnlái rúcǐ”的de那nà种zhǒng状态zhuàngtài。
The "nature" Laozi speaks of is by no means mountains, rivers, grass and trees, but rather the state of things being just as they originally are.
换句话说huàn jù huà shuō，它tā强调qiángdiào的de是shì：不要bú yào用yòng蛮力mánlì去qù扭曲niǔqū事物shìwù本来běnlái的de节奏jiézòu。
In other words, what it stresses is this: do not use brute force to twist the original rhythm of things.
这zhè一yì点diǎn在zài城市chéngshì规划guīhuà中zhōng体现tǐxiàn得de很hěn清楚qīngchu：一yì条tiáo被bèi水泥shuǐní封死fēngsǐ的de河hé，遇到yùdào暴雨bàoyǔ反而fǎn'ér更gèng容易róngyì泛滥fànlàn。
This shows up clearly in city planning: a river sealed in concrete is actually more likely to flood when a downpour comes.
近年来jìnnián lái流行liúxíng的de“海绵城市hǎimián chéngshì”让ràng雨水yǔshuǐ重新chóngxīn渗shèn进jìn土壤tǔrǎng，某种程度上mǒu zhǒng chéngdù shang正是zhèng shì古老gǔlǎo智慧zhìhuì的de现代xiàndài版本bǎnběn。
The "sponge cities" fashionable in recent years let rainwater seep back into the soil, which is to some extent a modern version of that ancient wisdom.
当然dāngrán，把bǎ“道法自然dào fǎ zìrán”理解lǐjiě成chéng什么shénme都dōu不bù做zuò，未免wèimiǎn过于guòyú消极xiāojí。
Of course, reading 道法自然 as an excuse to do nothing at all is rather too passive.
老子Lǎozǐ讲jiǎng的de“无为wúwéi”，指zhǐ的de是shì不bú妄为wàngwéi，而ér不是bú shì不bú作为zuòwéi。
The 无为 Laozi talks about means not acting rashly, not refusing to act at all.
一yí位wèi种地zhòngdì的de朋友péngyou告诉gàosu我wǒ，务农wùnóng最zuì要紧yàojǐn的de本事běnshi，是shì知道zhīdào什么时候shénme shíhou该gāi动手dòngshǒu，什么时候shénme shíhou该gāi等děng着zhe。
A friend who farms told me that the most critical skill in working the land is knowing when to get to work and when to simply wait.
在zài节奏jiézòu越来越yuèláiyuè快kuài的de生活shēnghuó里lǐ，我们wǒmen不妨bùfáng偶尔ǒu'ěr停tíng下来xiàlái，看看kànkan事情shìqing本来běnlái想xiǎng往wǎng哪个nǎge方向fāngxiàng生长shēngzhǎng。
In a life that keeps speeding up, we might as well stop now and then and look at which direction things wanted to grow in the first place.

Key words 生词

顺其自然shùn qí zìránto let nature take its course; to go with the flow
蛮力mánlìbrute force
扭曲niǔqūto distort; to twist
泛滥fànlànto flood; to overflow; to spread unchecked
无为wúwéinon-action; refraining from forcing things (Daoist term)
消极xiāojípassive; negative; unenthusiastic

Grammar note 语法点

并不是…，而是…

'It is by no means A, but rather B' — 并 before 不是 strengthens the denial, correcting an assumption the reader may hold; 而是 supplies the accurate version. 例：他并不是不想去，而是没有时间。

老子所说的“自然”，并不是山川草木，而是事物“本来如此”的那种状态。 — The "nature" Laozi speaks of is by no means mountains and rivers, but the state of things being just as they originally are.
未免 + 太/过于 + adj.

'That is rather too…' — 未免 softens a criticism, marking a judgment as somewhat excessive. It comments on a situation, never on a request. 例：这样批评一个孩子，未免太严厉了。

把“道法自然”理解成什么都不做，未免过于消极。 — Reading 道法自然 as doing nothing at all is rather too passive.
不妨 + verb

'Might as well; there is no harm in doing…' — a gentle suggestion that the action costs little and may help. 例：如果拿不定主意，不妨先问问专业人士。

我们不妨偶尔停下来，看看事情本来想往哪个方向生长。 — We might as well stop now and then and see which way things wanted to grow.

Check yourself 小测验

1. According to the text, what does Laozi's 自然 actually refer to?
2. Why does the text mention rivers sealed in concrete?
3. How does the writer explain 无为?
