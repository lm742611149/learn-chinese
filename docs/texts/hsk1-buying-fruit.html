买水果

Mǎi shuǐguǒ
Buying Fruit · 78 words · ~1 min
今天jīntiān下午xiàwǔ，我wǒ去qù商店shāngdiàn买mǎi水果shuǐguǒ。
This afternoon, I go to the store to buy fruit.
商店shāngdiàn里lǐ的de水果shuǐguǒ很hěn多duō，有yǒu大dà苹果píngguǒ，有yǒu小xiǎo苹果píngguǒ。
There is a lot of fruit in the store — there are big apples and small apples.
我wǒ说shuō：“苹果píngguǒ多少duōshao钱qián一yí个gè？”
I say, "How much is one apple?"
商店shāngdiàn的de人rén说shuō：“大dà苹果píngguǒ三sān块kuài钱qián一yí个gè，小xiǎo苹果píngguǒ一yí块kuài钱qián一yí个gè。”
The person at the store says, "Big apples are three kuai each, and small apples are one kuai each."
我wǒ买mǎi了le五wǔ个gè小xiǎo苹果píngguǒ。
I bought five small apples.
我wǒ回huí家jiā和hé妈妈māma吃chī苹果píngguǒ，苹果píngguǒ很hěn好吃hǎochī。
I go home and eat the apples with my mom — the apples are delicious.

Key words 生词

水果shuǐguǒfruit
苹果píngguǒapple
买mǎito buy
商店shāngdiànstore
钱qiánmoney
块kuàikuai (unit of money)

Grammar note 语法点

多少钱一个？ — asking the price

To ask a price, say the thing + 多少钱 + measure: 苹果多少钱一个？ = How much is one apple?

苹果多少钱一个？ — How much is one apple?
verb + 了 — completed action

了 right after a verb shows the action is finished or has happened: 买了 = bought.

我买了五个小苹果。 — I bought five small apples.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Where does the narrator go this afternoon?
2. How much is one small apple?
3. What does the narrator buy?
