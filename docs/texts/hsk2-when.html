什么时候Shénme shíhouWhen in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 102 words · ~2 min
我wǒ给gěi朋友péngyou打电话dǎ diànhuà，问wèn：“我们wǒmen什么shénme时候shíhou见面jiànmiàn？”
I call my friend and ask: "When shall we meet?"
她tā说shuō：“明天míngtiān怎么样zěnmeyàng？”
She says: "How about tomorrow?"
我wǒ说shuō：“好hǎo，明天míngtiān几点jǐ diǎn？”
I say: "Good, what time tomorrow?"
她tā说shuō：“下午xiàwǔ三点sān diǎn，在zài公园gōngyuán。”
She says: "Three o'clock in the afternoon, at the park."
下午xiàwǔ，我wǒ到dào了le公园gōngyuán，可是kěshì没méi看见kànjiàn她tā，打电话dǎ diànhuà问wèn：“你nǐ在zài哪儿nǎr？什么shénme时候shíhou到dào？”
In the afternoon, I arrive at the park, but I don't see her, so I call and ask: "Where are you? When will you get here?"
她tā说shuō：“对不起duìbuqǐ，我wǒ马上mǎshàng到dào，你nǐ等一下děng yíxià。”
She says: "Sorry, I'll be there right away, wait a moment."
后来hòulái，她tā跑pǎo过guò来lái，说shuō：“到dào了le到dào了le，我们wǒmen走zǒu吧ba！”
Afterward, she comes running over and says: "I'm here, I'm here, let's go!"

Key words 生词

时候shíhoutime; when
几点jǐ diǎnwhat time (the hour)
见面jiànmiànto meet
怎么样zěnmeyànghow about
马上mǎshàngright away
等一下děng yíxiàwait a moment

Grammar note 语法点

什么时候？（asking about a day or general time）

什么时候 asks broadly about when something happens — a day, a period, or 'someday'.

我们什么时候见面？— When shall we meet?
几点？（asking the clock time）

几点 asks specifically for the hour, once the day is already settled.

好，明天几点？— Good, what time tomorrow?
马上（right away）

马上 right before a verb says the action will happen very soon.

我马上到，你等一下。— I'll be there right away, wait a moment.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does 什么时候 ask about, compared to 几点?
2. What time do they agree to meet?
3. Where do they agree to meet?
