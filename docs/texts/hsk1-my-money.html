HSK 1 · Newbie

我的钱

Wǒ de qián
My Money · 49 words · ~1 min
我wǒ有yǒu一些yìxiē钱qián。
I have some money.
今天jīntiān我wǒ去qù商店shāngdiàn买mǎi东西dōngxi。
Today I go to the store to buy things.
一本yì běn书shū二十èrshí块kuài钱qián，我wǒ买mǎi了le一本yì běn。
One book costs twenty yuan, and I bought one.
我wǒ也yě买mǎi了le水果shuǐguǒ，十shí块kuài钱qián。
I also bought fruit, ten yuan.
现在xiànzài我wǒ的de钱qián不bù多duō了le。
Now I don't have much money left.
我wǒ不bù买mǎi了le，我wǒ回家huí jiā。
I won't buy anything more; I am going home.

Key words 生词

钱qiánmoney
块kuàiyuan (unit of money)
东西dōngxithings; stuff
一些yìxiēsome; a few
本běn(measure word for books)
现在xiànzàinow

Grammar note 语法点

有 — to have

有 means 'to have' or 'there is'. Its negative is 没有, never 不有.

我有一些钱。 — I have some money.
Measure words 本 and 块

Chinese counts nouns with a measure word: 一本书 (one book), 二十块钱 (twenty yuan). 一 is read yì before a third-tone syllable like běn.

一本书二十块钱。 — One book costs twenty yuan.
了 at the end — a new situation

Sentence-final 了 says something has changed: 不多了 = there isn't much left now, though there was before.

现在我的钱不多了。 — Now I don't have much money left.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Where does the narrator go today?
2. How much does one book cost?
3. What else does the narrator buy?
