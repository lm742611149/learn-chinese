基因编辑之争

Jīyīn biānjí zhī zhēng
The Gene-Editing Debate · 206 words · ~3 min
二〇一八èr líng yī bā年nián，一yí位wèi中国Zhōngguó科学家kēxuéjiā宣布xuānbù世界上shìjiè shang第一dì-yī对duì“基因jīyīn编辑biānjí婴儿yīng'ér”诞生dànshēng，随即suíjí引发yǐnfā了le全球quánqiú范围fànwéi的de强烈qiángliè谴责qiǎnzé。
In 2018 a Chinese scientist announced the birth of the world's first "gene-edited babies," which immediately drew fierce condemnation worldwide.
争论zhēnglùn的de核心héxīn并bìng不在于bú zàiyú技术jìshù本身běnshēn，而在于ér zàiyú人类rénlèi是否shìfǒu有yǒu资格zīgé改写gǎixiě生命shēngmìng的de说明书shuōmíngshū。
The heart of the dispute lies not in the technology itself, but in whether human beings have the standing to rewrite the instruction manual of life.
支持者zhīchízhě指出zhǐchū，如果rúguǒ一yí项xiàng技术jìshù能néng让ràng孩子háizi免于miǎn yú遗传yíchuán疾病jíbìng的de折磨zhémó，放弃fàngqì使用shǐyòng它tā同样tóngyàng是shì一yì种zhǒng残忍cánrěn。
Supporters point out that if a technology can spare a child the torment of a hereditary disease, refusing to use it is a cruelty of its own.
反对者fǎnduìzhě则zé担心dānxīn，一旦yídàn编辑biānjí生殖细胞shēngzhí xìbāo的de口子kǒuzi被bèi打开dǎkāi，“治病zhìbìng”与yǔ“改良gǎiliáng”之间zhījiān的de界限jièxiàn就jiù会huì越来越yuèláiyuè模糊móhu。
Opponents, for their part, worry that once the door to editing reproductive cells is opened, the line between "curing" and "enhancing" will grow ever blurrier.
今天jīntiān允许yǔnxǔ修改xiūgǎi致病zhìbìng基因jīyīn，明天míngtiān是否shìfǒu就要jiù yào挑选tiāoxuǎn身高shēngāo和hé智商zhìshāng？
If modifying disease-causing genes is permitted today, will we be choosing height and IQ tomorrow?
更gèng现实xiànshí的de问题wèntí是shì费用fèiyòng：昂贵ángguì的de技术jìshù往往wǎngwǎng先xiān服务于fúwù yú富人fùrén，从而cóng'ér可能kěnéng把bǎ社会shèhuì差距chājù刻kè进jìn基因jīyīn里lǐ。
An even more practical worry is cost: expensive technology tends to serve the wealthy first, and could thereby carve social inequality into our genes.
值得zhídé注意zhùyì的de是shì，用于yòng yú治疗zhìliáo成年chéngnián病人bìngrén的de基因jīyīn疗法liáofǎ争议zhēngyì要yào小xiǎo得多de duō，因为yīnwèi它tā的de影响yǐngxiǎng不会bú huì传chuán给gěi下一代xià yí dài。
It is worth noting that gene therapies used to treat adult patients are far less controversial, because their effects are not passed on to the next generation.
无论wúlùn支持zhīchí还是háishi反对fǎnduì，双方shuāngfāng至少zhìshǎo在zài一yì点diǎn上shàng是shì一致yízhì的de：这类zhè lèi决定juédìng不该bù gāi由yóu某mǒu个ge实验室shíyànshì单独dāndú作出zuòchū。
Whether they support it or oppose it, both sides agree on at least one point: a decision like this should not be made by a single laboratory alone.
技术jìshù跑pǎo得de越yuè快kuài，社会shèhuì就jiù越yuè需要xūyào停tíng下来xiàlái，先xiān把bǎ规则guīzé讨论tǎolùn清楚qīngchu。
The faster the technology runs, the more society needs to stop and talk the rules through first.

Grammar note 语法点

不在于…，而在于…

'It lies not in A, but in B' — a formal way to redirect the reader from a false focus to the real one, common in argumentative essays. 例：成功不在于速度，而在于方向。

争论的核心并不在于技术本身，而在于人类是否有资格改写生命的说明书。 — The core of the debate lies not in the technology itself but in whether humans have the standing to rewrite the manual of life.
一旦…，就…

'Once A happens, B follows' — 一旦 introduces a hypothetical but consequential turning point; the clause with 就 states the result that then becomes hard to stop. 例：一旦养成习惯，就很难改掉。

一旦编辑生殖细胞的口子被打开，界限就会越来越模糊。 — Once the door to editing reproductive cells is opened, the line will grow ever blurrier.
越 A，就越 B

'The more A, the more B' — links two variables that rise together. Each 越 attaches directly to a verb or adjective. 例：他越解释，大家就越糊涂。

技术跑得越快，社会就越需要停下来。 — The faster technology runs, the more society needs to stop.

Check yourself 小测验

1. According to the text, where does the heart of the gene-editing dispute lie?
2. What argument do supporters of the technology make?
3. Why does the text say gene therapy for adult patients is far less controversial?
