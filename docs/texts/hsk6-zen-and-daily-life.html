禅与生活

Chán yǔ shēnghuó
Zen and Everyday Life · 193 words · ~3 min
有人yǒurén问wèn禅师chánshī什么shénme是shì禅chán，禅师chánshī回答huídá：饿è了le吃饭chīfàn，困kùn了le睡觉shuìjiào。
Someone asked a Zen master what Zen is, and the master answered: when hungry, eat; when sleepy, sleep.
提问tíwèn的de人rén有些yǒuxiē不服气bù fúqì：这zhè谁shéi不bú会huì呢ne？
The questioner was not convinced: who doesn't know how to do that?
禅师chánshī说shuō：多数duōshù人rén吃饭chīfàn时shí想xiǎng着zhe工作gōngzuò，睡觉shuìjiào时shí想xiǎng着zhe明天míngtiān，所以suǒyǐ他们tāmen既jì没有méiyǒu真正zhēnzhèng吃饭chīfàn，也yě没有méiyǒu真正zhēnzhèng睡觉shuìjiào。
The master said: most people think about work while eating and about tomorrow while sleeping, so they neither truly eat nor truly sleep.
这个zhège古老gǔlǎo的de故事gùshi，放fàng到dào今天jīntiān简直jiǎnzhí像是xiàng shì为wèi手机shǒujī时代shídài量身定做liàng shēn dìng zuò的de。
Placed in the present, this ancient story seems practically tailor-made for the smartphone era.
我们wǒmen一边yìbiān刷shuā着zhe短视频duǎn shìpín，一边yìbiān扒bā着zhe碗wǎn里lǐ的de饭fàn，事后shìhòu连lián自己zìjǐ吃chī了le什么shénme都dōu想不起来xiǎng bu qǐlái。
We scroll through short videos while shovelling down the food in our bowl, and afterwards cannot even recall what we ate.
禅chán并不bìng bù要求yāoqiú人rén躲duǒ进jìn深山shēnshān，它tā关心guānxīn的de只是zhǐshì：此刻cǐkè你nǐ的de心xīn在不在zài bu zài这里zhèlǐ。
Zen by no means demands that you hide away in the mountains; all it cares about is whether your mind is here at this moment.
当然dāngrán，把bǎ专注zhuānzhù当成dàngchéng又yòu一yí项xiàng必须bìxū完成wánchéng的de任务rènwu，本身běnshēn就jiù违背wéibèi了le禅chán的de本意běnyì。
Of course, treating concentration as yet another task that must be completed already goes against what Zen was about.
比起bǐqǐ强迫qiǎngpò自己zìjǐ“什么shénme都dōu不bù想xiǎng”，从cóng认真rènzhēn洗xǐ一yí个ge碗wǎn、安静ānjìng走zǒu一yí段duàn路lù开始kāishǐ，也许yěxǔ更gèng容易róngyì让ràng注意力zhùyìlì回到huídào身体shēntǐ上shàng。
Compared with forcing yourself to "think of nothing," starting by washing one bowl carefully or walking one stretch of road quietly may make it easier to bring your attention back into your body.
据说jùshuō有yǒu位wèi老lǎo和尚héshang常cháng说shuō：担水dān shuǐ砍柴kǎn chái，无非wúfēi妙道miàodào，生活shēnghuó本身běnshēn就是jiùshì修行xiūxíng的de地方dìfang。
An old monk, it is said, often remarked that carrying water and chopping firewood are nothing other than the profound Way: life itself is where the practice happens.

Grammar note 语法点

既没有…，也没有…

'Neither A nor B' — the negative use of the 既…也… pair, listing two things that equally failed to happen. 例：那次会议既没有解决问题，也没有安排下一步。

他们既没有真正吃饭，也没有真正睡觉。 — They neither truly ate nor truly slept.
连…都…

'Even…' — 连 fronts the most extreme or most basic item, and 都 (or 也) marks it as included, implying everything lesser follows. 例：他连自己的生日都忘了。

事后连自己吃了什么都想不起来。 — Afterwards you cannot even recall what you ate.
把 A 当成 B

'To treat A as B' — 把 fronts the object, 当成 (also 当作) states the identity assigned to it, often a mistaken one. 例：别把客气话当成承诺。

把专注当成又一项必须完成的任务，本身就违背了禅的本意。 — Treating concentration as yet another task to be completed already goes against the point of Zen.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Why did the Zen master say most people neither truly eat nor truly sleep?
2. What does the text say Zen requires of a person?
3. What problem does the writer see in treating concentration as a task to complete?
