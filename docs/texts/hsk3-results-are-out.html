成绩出来了Chéngjì chūlái leThe Results Are Out

HSK 3 reading practice · 90 words · ~2 min
今天jīntiān早上zǎoshang，老师lǎoshī把bǎ这次zhè cì考试kǎoshì的de成绩chéngjì发给fāgěi了le我们wǒmen。
This morning, the teacher gave us the results of this exam.
我wǒ一边yìbiān看kàn手机shǒujī，一边yìbiān紧张jǐnzhāng地de等着děngzhe。
I looked at my phone while nervously waiting.
看到kàndào分数fēnshù的de那nà一刻yíkè，我wǒ高兴gāoxìng极了jí le。
The moment I saw the score, I was extremely happy.
我的wǒ de成绩chéngjì比bǐ我wǒ想xiǎng的de还要hái yào好hǎo，妈妈māma知道zhīdào以后yǐhòu也yě很hěn高兴gāoxìng。
My grade was even better than I expected, and my mom was very happy too when she found out.
同学tóngxué问wèn我wǒ：“你nǐ是shì怎么zěnme考kǎo得de这么zhème好hǎo的de？”
A classmate asked me: "How did you do so well on the exam?"
我wǒ笑着xiàozhe说shuō：“因为yīnwèi我wǒ每天měitiān都dōu认真rènzhēn复习fùxí。”
I said with a smile: "Because I review seriously every day."
这次zhè cì的de成绩chéngjì让ràng我wǒ很hěn开心kāixīn，我wǒ要yào继续jìxù努力nǔlì。
This grade made me very happy, and I want to keep working hard.

Key words 生词

成绩chéngjìgrade; result
分数fēnshùscore
认真rènzhēnserious; earnest
继续jìxùto continue
努力nǔlìto work hard
开心kāixīnhappy

Grammar note 语法点

把 (bǎ construction)

Subject + 把 + object + verb (+ other elements) moves the object before the verb to emphasize what happens to it.

今天早上，老师把这次考试的成绩发给了我们。 — This morning, the teacher gave us the results of this exam.
比……还要…… (even more than)

比 compares two things; adding 还要 before the adjective strengthens the comparison to 'even more'.

我的成绩比我想的还要好。 — My grade was even better than I expected.
是……的 (emphasizing how/when/where)

是……的 is used for a past action to emphasize a specific detail, such as how, when, or where it happened.

你是怎么考得这么好的？ — How did you do so well on the exam?

Check yourself 小测验

1. What did the teacher do this morning?
2. How did the narrator feel when seeing the score?
3. Why did the narrator say the grade was good?
