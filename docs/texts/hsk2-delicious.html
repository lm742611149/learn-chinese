HSK 2 · Elementary

真好吃Zhēn hǎochīDelicious in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 86 words · ~1 min
星期六xīngqīliù，我wǒ去qù朋友péngyou家jiā做客zuòkè。
On Saturday, I went to visit my friend's home.
朋友péngyou的de妈妈māma做zuò了le很hěn多duō菜cài，有yǒu鱼yú，还hái有yǒu汤tāng。
My friend's mom had made lots of dishes — there was fish, and soup too.
我wǒ吃chī了le一yí块kuài鱼yú，说shuō：“真zhēn好吃hǎochī！”
I ate a piece of the fish and said, "That's really good!"
朋友péngyou说shuō：“你nǐ喜欢xǐhuan，就jiù多duō吃chī一点儿yìdiǎnr。”
My friend said, "If you like it, eat some more."
我wǒ又yòu吃chī了le很hěn多duō，菜cài都dōu好吃hǎochī极jí了le。
I ate a lot more — everything was so delicious.
走zǒu的时候de shíhou，我wǒ对duì朋友péngyou说shuō：“你nǐ妈妈māma做zuò的de菜cài，比bǐ饭馆fànguǎn的de还hái好吃hǎochī。”
When I left, I told my friend, "Your mom's cooking is even better than a restaurant's."

Key words 生词

好吃hǎochīdelicious
做客zuòkèto visit as a guest
极jíextremely
比bǐcompared to
还háieven more
一点儿yìdiǎnra little

Grammar note 语法点

adj + 极了（extremely...）

极了 goes right after an adjective to push it to the extreme — stronger than just 很.

菜都好吃极了。— The food was all incredibly delicious.
A 比 B 还 + adj（even more than）

比 compares A to B; adding 还 before the adjective says A goes even further than B.

你妈妈做的菜，比饭馆的还好吃。— Your mom's cooking is even better than a restaurant's.
多 + verb + 一点儿（do a little more）

多 before a verb, with 一点儿 after it, means to do a bit more of that action.

你喜欢，就多吃一点儿。— If you like it, eat a little more.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Where does the narrator go on Saturday?
2. What does the narrator eat first?
3. How does the narrator describe the friend's mom's cooking at the end?
