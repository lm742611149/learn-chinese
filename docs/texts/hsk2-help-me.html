HSK 2 · Elementary

请帮帮我Qǐng bāngbang wǒHelp Me in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 103 words · ~2 min
我wǒ在zài车站chēzhàn，看kàn不bù懂dǒng那个nàge字zì。
I'm at the station, and I can't read that character.
我wǒ看见kànjiàn一个yí gè老师lǎoshī，说shuō：“老师lǎoshī，请qǐng帮bāng我wǒ看看kànkan这个zhège字zì。”
I saw a teacher and said, "Teacher, please help me look at this character."
老师lǎoshī看了看kànlekàn，说shuō：“没méi问题wèntí，这个zhège是shì去qù北京Běijīng的de车chē。”
The teacher looked at it and said, "No problem — this is the train to Beijing."
我wǒ说shuō：“可是kěshì我wǒ不bù知道zhīdào车chē在zài哪儿nǎr，你nǐ可以kěyǐ帮bāng我wǒ找zhǎo车chē吗ma？”
I said, "But I don't know where the train is — could you help me find it?"
老师lǎoshī笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“没méi问题wèntí，我wǒ带dài你nǐ去qù。”
The teacher smiled and said, "No problem, I'll take you there."
她tā带dài我wǒ找到zhǎodào了le车chē，我wǒ说shuō：“谢谢xièxie你nǐ，你nǐ真zhēn的de帮bāng了le我wǒ。”
She led me to find the train, and I said, "Thank you — you really helped me."

Key words 生词

帮bāngto help
请qǐngplease
车站chēzhànstation
北京BěijīngBeijing
找zhǎoto look for; find
带dàito take (lead)

Grammar note 语法点

请 + 帮 + 我 + V

请帮我 + verb is a simple, polite way to ask someone to do something for you.

请帮我看看这个字。 — Please help me look at this character.
你可以帮我 + V + 吗？

你可以 turns a request into a soft question — 吗 at the end makes it a clear question.

你可以帮我找车吗? — Could you help me find the train?
V + 着

着 after a verb shows one action happening alongside another, like smiling while speaking.

老师笑着说。 — The teacher said, smiling.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Where does the story take place?
2. What is the narrator's first problem?
3. What does the teacher do in the end?
