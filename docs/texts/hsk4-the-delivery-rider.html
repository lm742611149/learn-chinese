HSK 4 · Upper Int.

快递小哥

Kuàidì Xiǎogē
The Delivery Rider · 144 words · ~2 min
我wǒ家jiā楼下lóuxià的de快递kuàidì站zhàn，每天měitiān早上zǎoshang六liù点diǎn就jiù开门kāimén了le。
The delivery station downstairs from my building opens as early as six every morning.
送sòng快递kuàidì的de小哥xiǎogē姓xìng王Wáng，大家dàjiā都dōu叫jiào他tā小王Xiǎo Wáng。
The rider who delivers the parcels is surnamed Wang, and everyone calls him Xiao Wang.
他tā不但búdàn记得jìde每měi个ge人rén的de名字míngzi，而且érqiě知道zhīdào谁shéi家jiā白天báitiān没有méiyǒu人rén。
He not only remembers everyone's name, he also knows whose home is empty during the day.
无论wúlùn刮风guā fēng还是háishi下雨xià yǔ，他tā每měi天tiān都dōu准时zhǔnshí出现chūxiàn在zài我们wǒmen楼下lóuxià。
No matter whether it's windy or raining, he shows up downstairs on time every day.
有yǒu一yí次cì我wǒ在zài开会kāihuì，他tā把bǎ箱子xiāngzi放fàng在zài门口ménkǒu，还hái发fā短信duǎnxìn告诉gàosu我wǒ。
Once, when I was in a meeting, he left the box at my door and even sent me a text to let me know.
我wǒ问wèn他tā一yì天tiān要yào送sòng多少duōshao件jiàn快递kuàidì，他tā笑xiào着zhe说shuō两liǎng百bǎi多duō件jiàn。
I asked him how many parcels he has to deliver in a day, and he said with a smile: more than two hundred.
对duì我wǒ来说láishuō，网上wǎngshàng买mǎi东西dōngxi只是zhǐshì点diǎn一下yíxià手机shǒujī；对duì他tā来说láishuō，那nà是shì从cóng早zǎo跑pǎo到dào晚wǎn的de一yì天tiān。
For me, buying something online is just a tap on my phone; for him, it's a day of running from morning till night.
所以suǒyǐ现在xiànzài拿到nádào快递kuàidì的de时候shíhou，我wǒ总zǒng会huì认真rènzhēn地de说shuō一yì声shēng谢谢xièxie。
So now, whenever I take a parcel, I always make a point of saying a sincere thank you.

Key words 生词

快递kuàidìexpress delivery; parcel
小哥xiǎogēyoung guy (friendly term for a rider)
准时zhǔnshíon time; punctual
出现chūxiànto appear; to show up
短信duǎnxìntext message
白天báitiāndaytime

Grammar note 语法点

不但……而且…… — not only… but also…

不但 introduces the first fact and 而且 adds a second, stronger one about the same subject: 他不但记得每个人的名字，而且知道谁家白天没有人。

他不但会说汉语，而且说得很流利。— He not only speaks Chinese, he speaks it very fluently.
无论……都…… — no matter…, still…

无论 is followed by a choice or a question word, and 都 in the second half says the result never changes.

无论多忙，他都会给家里打电话。— No matter how busy he is, he always calls home.
对……来说 — as far as … is concerned

对……来说 marks whose point of view you are taking, and is often used twice to contrast two people: 对我来说……；对他来说……。

对孩子来说，暑假是一年中最快乐的时候。— For children, the summer holiday is the happiest time of the year.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What time does the delivery station downstairs open each morning?
2. What did Xiao Wang do when the author was in a meeting?
3. How many parcels does Xiao Wang say he delivers in one day?
