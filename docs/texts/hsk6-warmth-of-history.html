HSK 6 · Fluent

历史的温度

Lìshǐ de wēndù
The Warmth of History · 191 words · ~3 min
正史zhèngshǐ记录jìlù的de往往wǎngwǎng是shì帝王dìwáng将相jiàngxiàng的de功过gōngguò，而ér普通pǔtōng人rén的de日常生活rìcháng shēnghuó常常chángcháng被bèi一笔带过yì bǐ dài guò。
Official history tends to record the achievements and faults of emperors, generals, and ministers, while the daily lives of ordinary people are often mentioned only in passing.
然而rán'ér，一封yì fēng泛黄fànhuáng的de家书jiāshū、一yì本běn尘封chénfēng的de日记rìjì，却què能néng让ràng我们wǒmen触摸chùmō到dào那个nàge年代niándài真实zhēnshí的de体温tǐwēn。
Yet a single yellowed letter home, or one long-forgotten diary, can let us touch the real body warmth of that era.
有yǒu位wèi学者xuézhě整理zhěnglǐ了le祖父zǔfù留下liúxià的de账本zhàngběn，从cóng米mǐ、布bù、盐yán的de价格jiàgé变化biànhuà里lǐ，拼pīn出chū了le一yí个ge家庭jiātíng动荡dòngdàng年代niándài里lǐ的de生存shēngcún智慧zhìhuì。
A scholar organized her grandfather's old ledger, and from the shifting prices of rice, cloth, and salt pieced together a family's survival wisdom during a turbulent era.
这zhè些xiē细节xìjié，教科书jiàokēshū里lǐ找不到zhǎobudào，却què恰恰qiàqià是shì历史lìshǐ最zuì鲜活xiānhuó的de部分bùfen。
These details cannot be found in any textbook, yet they are precisely the most vivid part of history.
个人gèrén记录jìlù之所以zhīsuǒyǐ珍贵zhēnguì，是因为shì yīnwèi它tā保留bǎoliú了le宏大hóngdà叙事xùshì所suǒ无法wúfǎ容纳róngnà的de犹豫yóuyù、恐惧kǒngjù与yǔ盼望pànwàng。
Personal records are precious precisely because they preserve the hesitation, fear, and longing that grand narratives have no room to hold.
一yí位wèi母亲mǔqīn在zài信xìn里lǐ反复fǎnfù叮嘱dīngzhǔ儿子érzi“天冷tiān lěng加jiā衣yī”，寥寥liáoliáo数shù语yǔ，却què比bǐ任何rènhé宏大hóngdà的de论述lùnshù都dōu更gèng能néng打动dǎdòng人rén心xīn。
A mother repeatedly urged her son in a letter, "add more clothes when it turns cold" — a handful of plain words, yet more moving than any grand discourse.
正zhèng是shì无数wúshù这样zhèyàng朴素pǔsù的de记录jìlù，为wèi冷冰冰lěngbīngbīng的de年份niánfèn与yǔ事件shìjiàn，注入zhùrù了le血肉xuèròu与yǔ温度wēndù。
It is precisely countless such plain records that inject flesh and blood, and warmth, into cold, impersonal years and events.
与其yǔqí把bǎ历史lìshǐ仅仅jǐnjǐn当作dàngzuò需要xūyào背诵bèisòng的de年代niándài，不如bùrú把bǎ它tā当作dàngzuò无数wúshù普通人pǔtōngrén真实zhēnshí活huó过guò的de证据zhèngjù。
Rather than treating history merely as dates to be memorized by rote, it is better to treat it as evidence that countless ordinary people truly lived.

Grammar note 语法点

…而…，却…

Sets up a contrast between two clauses ('whereas... yet...'), often pairing a formal register with an unexpected turn. 例：白天他沉默寡言，而深夜，却常常提笔写信。

正史记录的往往是帝王将相的功过，而普通人的日常生活常常被一笔带过。 — Official history tends to record the achievements and faults of emperors and ministers, while ordinary people's daily lives are often mentioned only in passing.
…之所以…，是因为…

States the result first, then explains the cause: 'the reason A is precious/true is that B'. Common in reflective, analytical writing. 例：这段友情之所以珍贵，是因为它经受住了时间的考验。

个人记录之所以珍贵，是因为它保留了宏大叙事所无法容纳的犹豫、恐惧与盼望。 — Personal records are precious precisely because they preserve what grand narratives have no room to hold.
与其…，不如…

Weighs two options and recommends the second: 'rather than A, it is better to B'. Frequently used to conclude an argument with a piece of advice. 例：与其抱怨环境，不如改变自己。

与其把历史仅仅当作需要背诵的年代，不如把它当作无数普通人真实活过的证据。 — Rather than treating history merely as dates to memorize, it is better to treat it as evidence that countless ordinary people truly lived.

Check yourself 小测验

2. What does the mother repeatedly urge in her letter to her son?
3. What does the text suggest we should treat history as, rather than merely dates to memorize?
