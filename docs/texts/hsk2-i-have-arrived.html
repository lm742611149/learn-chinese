我到了Wǒ dào leI Have Arrived in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 116 words · ~2 min
星期六xīngqīliù下午xiàwǔ，我wǒ和hé朋友péngyou要yào在zài车站chēzhàn见面jiànmiàn，一起yìqǐ去qù看kàn电影diànyǐng。
On Saturday afternoon, my friend and I were meeting at the bus stop to go watch a movie together.
三sān点diǎn的时候de shíhou，我wǒ先xiān到dào了le车站chēzhàn，可是kěshì没méi看见kànjiàn朋友péngyou。
At three o'clock, I arrived at the bus stop first, but I didn't see my friend.
我wǒ给gěi她tā打电话dǎ diànhuà，说shuō：“我wǒ到dào了le，你nǐ在zài哪儿nǎr？”
I called her and said: "I've arrived, where are you?"
她tā说shuō：“对不起duìbuqǐ，我wǒ快kuài到dào了le，你nǐ再zài等一下děng yíxià。”
She said: "Sorry, I'll be there soon, wait a little longer."
过guò了le十shí分钟fēnzhōng，我wǒ又yòu给gěi她tā打电话dǎ diànhuà，问wèn：“你nǐ到dào了le吗ma？”
Ten minutes later, I called her again and asked: "Have you arrived yet?"
她tā笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“我wǒ也yě到dào了le，我wǒ在zài车站chēzhàn门口ménkǒu！”
She laughed and said: "I've arrived too, I'm at the bus stop entrance!"
我wǒ看见kànjiàn了le她tā，我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ走zǒu进jìn电影院diànyǐngyuàn，坐下zuòxià看kàn电影diànyǐng。
I saw her, and we went into the cinema together and sat down to watch the movie.

Grammar note 语法点

我到了（I have arrived）

到了 after 我 tells someone you have just arrived — the most common line to say or text when meeting up.

我到了，你在哪儿？— I've arrived, where are you?
先（to do something first）

先 before a verb shows that action happens before anything else, such as arriving before someone else.

我先到了车站。— I arrived at the bus stop first.
又 for something that already happened again

又 marks a repeated action that has already taken place, unlike 再 which marks one that hasn't happened yet.

我又给她打电话。— I called her again.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the writer say when she calls her friend the first time?
2. Why does the writer call her friend a second time?
3. What do the two friends do after they meet?
