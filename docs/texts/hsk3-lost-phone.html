手机丢了又找回Shǒujī Diū Le Yòu ZhǎohuíLosing and Finding My Phone

HSK 3 reading practice · 140 words · ~2 min
昨天zuótiān晚上wǎnshang，我wǒ从cóng餐厅cāntīng出来chūlái以后yǐhòu，发现fāxiàn手机shǒujī不见bú jiàn了le。
Last night, after I came out of the restaurant, I discovered my phone was gone.
其实qíshí我wǒ也yě不bù知道zhīdào手机shǒujī是shì什么shénme时候shíhou掉diào的de，因为yīnwèi我wǒ一直yìzhí没méi用yòng它tā。
Actually I didn't even know when the phone fell out, because I hadn't used it the whole time.
我wǒ把bǎ包bāo里lǐ的de东西dōngxi都dōu找zhǎo了le一遍yí biàn，可是kěshì还是háishi没méi找到zhǎodào。
I searched through everything in my bag once, but I still couldn't find it.
虽然suīrán我wǒ很hěn着急zháojí，但是dànshì朋友péngyou安慰ānwèi我wǒ说shuō：“别bié担心dānxīn，我们wǒmen可以kěyǐ用yòng地图dìtú功能gōngnéng找zhǎo手机shǒujī的de位置wèizhi。”
Although I was very anxious, my friend comforted me, saying: "Don't worry, we can use the map function to find the phone's location."
我们wǒmen按照ànzhào地图dìtú上shang的de位置wèizhi，回huí到dào餐厅cāntīng门口ménkǒu，发现fāxiàn手机shǒujī就jiù在zài椅子yǐzi下面xiàmiàn。
According to the location on the map, we went back to the restaurant entrance and found the phone was right under a chair.
服务员fúwùyuán说shuō，她tā刚才gāngcái打扫dǎsǎo的de时候shíhou也yě没méi看见kànjiàn它tā。
The waitress said that when she was cleaning up just now, she hadn't noticed it either.
找到zhǎodào手机shǒujī以后yǐhòu，我wǒ高兴gāoxìng得de差点儿chàdiǎnr哭kū出来chūlái。
After finding the phone, I was so happy that I almost cried.
从cóng那nà以后yǐhòu，我wǒ养成yǎngchéng了le一个yí ge好hǎo习惯xíguàn：出门chūmén前qián一定yídìng要yào检查jiǎnchá东西dōngxi带dài齐qí了le没有méiyǒu。
From then on, I developed a good habit: before going out, I always make sure to check whether I've brought everything.

Key words 生词

Grammar note 语法点

把 + object + verb — disposal construction

把 moves the object before the verb to emphasize what happens to it.

我把包里的东西都找了一遍。 — I searched through everything in my bag.
adj/verb + 得 + degree — describing the extent of a result

得 links an action or feeling to how extreme it was.

我高兴得差点儿哭出来。 — I was so happy I almost cried.

Check yourself 小测验

1. When did the narrator discover the phone was missing?
2. How did the friend help find the phone?
3. Where was the phone finally found?
