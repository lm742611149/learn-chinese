在学校学习

Zài xuéxiào xuéxí
Studying at School · 46 words · ~1 min
我wǒ在zài学校xuéxiào学习xuéxí汉语Hànyǔ。
I study Chinese at school.
我们wǒmen的de老师lǎoshī是shì中国Zhōngguó人rén，她tā很hěn好hǎo。
Our teacher is Chinese, and she is very nice.
上午shàngwǔ我们wǒmen学xué写xiě汉字Hànzì。
In the morning we learn to write Chinese characters.
我wǒ写xiě的de字zì不太bú tài好hǎo，老师lǎoshī说shuō没关系méi guānxi。
The characters I write are not very good, but the teacher says it doesn't matter.
下午xiàwǔ四点sì diǎn，我们wǒmen回家huí jiā。
At four in the afternoon, we go home.
我wǒ很hěn喜欢xǐhuan学习xuéxí汉语Hànyǔ。
I really like studying Chinese.

Grammar note 语法点

在 + place + verb — where you do something

Say where first, then what you do: 在学校学习 = study at school.

我在学校学习汉语。 — I study Chinese at school.
不太 + adjective — not very

不太 softens a negative judgement. Because 太 is fourth tone, 不 is read bú here.

我写的字不太好。 — The characters I write are not very good.
verb + 的 + noun — a modifier before a noun

A whole phrase can describe a noun if you add 的: 我写的字 = the characters that I write.

我写的字不太好。 — The characters I write are not very good.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the narrator study at school?
2. Where is the teacher from?
3. What time do they go home in the afternoon?
