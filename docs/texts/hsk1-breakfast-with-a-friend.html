一起吃早饭Yìqǐ chī zǎofànBreakfast with a Friend

HSK 1 reading practice · 46 words · ~1 min
早上zǎoshang，我wǒ的de朋友péngyou来lái我wǒ家jiā。
In the morning, my friend comes to my home.
我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ吃chī早饭zǎofàn。
We eat breakfast together.
我wǒ喝hē牛奶niúnǎi，他tā喝hē豆浆dòujiāng。
I drink milk, he drinks soy milk.
我们wǒmen吃chī面包miànbāo，也yě吃chī鸡蛋jīdàn。
We eat bread, and also eat eggs.
早饭zǎofàn很hěn好吃hǎochī，我们wǒmen很hěn高兴gāoxìng。
Breakfast is delicious, we are very happy.
我们wǒmen吃chī了le早饭zǎofàn，去qù学校xuéxiào。
We ate breakfast, then go to school.

Grammar note 语法点

一起 — doing something together

一起 means 'together' and goes right before the verb: 我们一起吃早饭 = We eat breakfast together.

我们一起吃早饭。 — We eat breakfast together.
也 — also, too

也 means 'also' and goes before the verb, right after the subject: 我们也吃鸡蛋 = We also eat eggs.

我们吃面包，也吃鸡蛋。 — We eat bread, and also eat eggs.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the narrator drink?
2. What do they eat besides bread?
3. Where do they go after breakfast?
