HSK 2 · Elementary

帮助别人Bāngzhù biérénHelping Others

HSK 2 reading practice · 75 words · ~1 min
昨天zuótiān早上zǎoshang，我wǒ的de朋友péngyou生病shēngbìng了le，不bù能néng起床qǐchuáng。
Yesterday morning my friend got sick and couldn't get out of bed.
她tā给gěi我wǒ打电话dǎ diànhuà，让ràng我wǒ帮助bāngzhù她tā。
She called me and asked me to help her.
我wǒ去qù商店shāngdiàn给gěi她tā买mǎi了le药yào和hé水果shuǐguǒ。
I went to the shop and bought her medicine and fruit.
我wǒ还hái给gěi她tā做zuò了le面条miàntiáo和hé鸡蛋jīdàn。
I also made her noodles and eggs.
朋友péngyou吃chī了le药yào，休息xiūxi了le一天yì tiān，现在xiànzài好多了hǎo duō le。
My friend took the medicine, rested for a day, and is now much better.
她tā笑xiào着zhe对duì我wǒ说shuō：“谢谢xièxie你nǐ！”我wǒ觉得juéde帮助bāngzhù别人biérén真zhēn快乐kuàilè。
She smiled and said to me, "Thank you!" I feel that helping others is a real joy.

Key words 生词

帮助bāngzhùto help
生病shēngbìngto get sick
药yàomedicine
休息xiūxito rest
别人biérénother people
快乐kuàilèhappy

Grammar note 语法点

让 — to ask / let someone do something

让 + person + verb means to have or ask someone to do something: 她让我帮助她.

她让我帮助她。— She asked me to help her.
给 + person + verb — do something for someone

给 puts the person who benefits before the verb: 给她买药 (buy medicine for her), 给他做饭 (cook for him).

我给她买了药和水果。— I bought medicine and fruit for her.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Why did the friend call the writer?
2. What did the writer cook for the friend?
3. How does the writer feel about helping others?
