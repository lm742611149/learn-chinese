快乐的一天Kuàilè de yì tiānA Happy Day

HSK 2 reading practice · 81 words · ~1 min
今天jīntiān是shì我wǒ的de生日shēngrì，我wǒ很hěn快乐kuàilè。
Today is my birthday, and I'm very happy.
早上zǎoshang，家人jiārén都dōu对duì我wǒ说shuō：“生日shēngrì快乐kuàilè！”
In the morning, my family all said to me, "Happy birthday!"
中午zhōngwǔ，朋友们péngyoumen请qǐng我wǒ吃饭chīfàn，我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ唱歌chànggē。
At noon, my friends treated me to a meal, and we sang songs together.
下午xiàwǔ，我们wǒmen去qù公园gōngyuán玩wán，天气tiānqì也yě很hěn好hǎo。
In the afternoon, we went to the park to hang out, and the weather was nice too.
晚上wǎnshang，妈妈māma做zuò了le一yí个gè大dà蛋糕dàngāo，全家quánjiā一起yìqǐ吃chī蛋糕dàngāo。
In the evening, mom made a big cake, and the whole family ate cake together.
大家dàjiā送sòng给gěi我wǒ很多hěnduō礼物lǐwù，我wǒ高兴gāoxìng极jí了le。
Everyone gave me lots of gifts, and I was extremely happy.
这zhè真zhēn是shì快乐kuàilè的de一yì天tiān！
This was really a happy day!

Grammar note 语法点

对 + someone + 说 — to say to someone

对 introduces the person being spoken to before 说.

家人都对我说：“生日快乐！” — My family all said to me, "Happy birthday!"
一起 + V — to do something together

一起 before a verb shows two or more people doing the action together.

我们一起唱歌。 — We sang together.
Adj + 极了 — extremely

极了 after an adjective intensifies it to mean "extremely."

我高兴极了。 — I was extremely happy.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What is special about today for the writer?
2. What did the writer do at noon?
3. What did mom make in the evening?
