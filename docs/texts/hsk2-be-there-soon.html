我马上到Wǒ mǎshàng dàoI Will Be There Soon in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 115 words · ~2 min
今天jīntiān晚上wǎnshang六liù点diǎn半bàn，我wǒ要yào去qù饭馆fànguǎn和hé朋友péngyou吃饭chīfàn。
At 6:30 tonight, I was going to eat at a restaurant with my friend.
我wǒ出门chūmén晚wǎn了le，坐zuò公共汽车gōnggòng qìchē去qù，车chē开kāi得de很hěn慢màn。
I left home late, so I took the bus, and it drove very slowly.
我wǒ给gěi朋友péngyou打电话dǎ diànhuà，说shuō：“路上lùshang车chē很hěn多duō，我wǒ可能kěnéng会huì晚wǎn一点yìdiǎn。”
I called my friend and said: "There's a lot of traffic on the way, I might be a little late."
朋友péngyou说shuō：“没关系méi guānxi，你nǐ慢慢mànmàn来lái，我们wǒmen等děng你nǐ。”
My friend said: "It's OK, take your time, we'll wait for you."
车chē快kuài到dào站zhàn的时候de shíhou，我wǒ又yòu打电话dǎ diànhuà说shuō：“我wǒ快kuài到dào了le，马上mǎshàng就jiù到dào！”
When the bus was almost at the stop, I called again and said: "I'm almost there, I'll be right there!"
车chē到dào了le车站chēzhàn，我wǒ下车xiàchē就jiù往wǎng饭馆fànguǎn跑pǎo。
The bus reached the stop, and as soon as I got off I ran toward the restaurant.
我wǒ跑pǎo进jìn饭馆fànguǎn，看见kànjiàn桌子zhuōzi上shàng已经yǐjīng放fàng着zhe我wǒ最zuì喜欢xǐhuan的de菜cài。
I ran into the restaurant and saw my favorite dish already sitting on the table.

Grammar note 语法点

我快到了，马上就到（I'm almost there, I'll be right there）

快到了 says you're nearly there, and 马上 emphasizes 'very soon' — the classic reassurance to send when someone is waiting for you.

我快到了，马上就到！— I'm almost there, I'll be right there!
可能 + 会（might / probably will）

可能 right before 会 softens a prediction, useful for warning someone you might be late.

路上车很多，我可能会晚一点。— There's a lot of traffic on the way, I might be a little late.
慢慢来（take your time）

慢慢 doubled before a verb tells someone not to rush — a common reply when a friend says they'll be late.

没关系，你慢慢来，我们等你。— It's OK, take your time, we'll wait for you.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Why is the narrator running late?
2. What does the narrator say when the bus is almost at the stop?
3. What does the narrator see on the table at the restaurant?
