怎么说Zěnme shuōHow Do You Say It in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 84 words · ~1 min
我wǒ在zài中国Zhōngguó学xué中文Zhōngwén，可是kěshì很多hěn duō东西dōngxi的de名字míngzi我wǒ还hái不bù知道zhīdào。
I'm studying Chinese in China, but I still don't know the names of a lot of things.
今天jīntiān，我wǒ和hé朋友péngyou在zài教室jiàoshì里li，桌子zhuōzi上shàng有yǒu一个yí ge苹果píngguǒ。
Today, my friend and I are in the classroom, and there's an apple on the table.
我wǒ问wèn朋友péngyou：“这个zhège用yòng中文Zhōngwén怎么zěnme说shuō？”
I ask my friend: "How do you say this in Chinese?"
朋友péngyou说shuō：“这个zhège叫jiào苹果píngguǒ。”
My friend says: "This is called píngguǒ (apple)."
我wǒ又yòu看见kànjiàn杯子bēizi，也yě问wèn她tā：“这个zhège呢ne？”
I see a cup this time, and ask her too: "What about this one?"
她tā说shuō：“这个zhège是shì杯子bēizi，你nǐ今天jīntiān认识rènshi了le两liǎng个gè新xīn东西dōngxi！”
She said, "That's a cup — you've learned two new things today!"

Grammar note 语法点

这个用中文怎么说？（How do you say this in Chinese?）

This is the question to ask when you don't know a word — point at something and say this.

这个用中文怎么说？— How do you say this in Chinese?
叫（to be called）

叫 introduces the name of a thing right after 这个是 or on its own.

这个叫苹果。— This is called 'píngguǒ'.
又 + verb（to do something again）

又 goes before a verb to show an action happening again, already in the past or present.

我又看见杯子。— I see a cup again.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does "这个用中文怎么说？" mean?
2. What was the first new word the narrator learned?
3. How does the friend react at the end?
