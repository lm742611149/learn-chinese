HSK 2 · Elementary

别玩手机了Bié wán shǒujī lePut Down Your Phone

HSK 2 reading practice · 100 words · ~2 min
晚上wǎnshang吃饭chī fàn的de时候shíhou，我wǒ儿子érzi在zài看kàn手机shǒujī。
At dinner in the evening, my son was looking at his phone.
我wǒ说shuō：“别bié玩wán手机shǒujī了le，快kuài吃chī吧ba，菜cài都dōu冷lěng了le。”
I said: "Put the phone down and eat — the food is getting cold."
他tā说shuō：“等děng一下yíxià，我wǒ的de朋友péngyou在zài等děng我wǒ。”
He said: "Just a moment, my friend is waiting for me."
我wǒ告诉gàosu他tā，每天měi tiān看kàn手机shǒujī太tài长cháng时间shíjiān，眼睛yǎnjing会huì很hěn累lèi。
I told him that looking at a phone too long every day makes your eyes tired.
他tā听tīng了le以后yǐhòu，就jiù不bú看kàn手机shǒujī了le。
After he heard that, he stopped looking at his phone.
那天nà tiān晚上wǎnshang我们wǒmen说shuō了le很多hěn duō话huà，我wǒ觉得juéde非常fēicháng高兴gāoxìng。
That evening we talked a lot, and I felt really happy.
我wǒ想xiǎng，以后yǐhòu吃饭chī fàn的de时候shíhou，大家dàjiā都dōu别bié看kàn手机shǒujī了le。
I think that from now on, none of us should look at a phone during meals.

Key words 生词

别biédon't
玩wánto play with
告诉gàosuto tell
眼睛yǎnjingeye
等děngto wait
就jiùthen; right away

Grammar note 语法点

别…了 — stop doing that

别 + verb + 了 asks somebody to STOP something they are already doing: 别玩手机了, 别说了. Without 了 it is a plain 'don't do it'.

别玩手机了，快吃吧。 — Put the phone down and eat.
在 + verb — happening right now

Put 在 before the verb for an action in progress: 他在看手机, 朋友在等我. 正在 means the same and is a little stronger.

我的朋友在等我。 — My friend is waiting for me.
…以后，就… — after …, right away

以后 follows the first action (听了以后 = after hearing), and 就 before the second verb shows it happened immediately.

他听了以后，就不看手机了。 — After hearing that, he stopped looking at his phone.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What is the son doing at dinner?
2. What does the father say happens if you look at a phone too long?
3. What does the son do after listening to his father?
