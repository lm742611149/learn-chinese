买咖啡Mǎi kāfēiBuying Coffee in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 53 words · ~1 min
早上zǎoshang我wǒ去qù咖啡店kāfēidiàn买mǎi咖啡kāfēi。
In the morning I go to the coffee shop to buy coffee.
服务员fúwùyuán问wèn我wǒ：“您nín要yào热rè的de还是háishi冰bīng的de？”
The server asks me: "Would you like it hot or iced?"
我wǒ说shuō：“我wǒ要yào一杯yì bēi热rè咖啡kāfēi，不要bú yào糖táng。”
I say: "I'd like a hot coffee, no sugar."
咖啡kāfēi三十sānshí块kuài钱qián，有点儿yǒudiǎnr贵guì。
The coffee is thirty kuai — a little expensive.
但是dànshì很hěn好喝hǎohē，我wǒ每天měi tiān都dōu想xiǎng喝hē。
But it's delicious — I want to drink it every day.

Key words 生词

咖啡店kāfēidiàncoffee shop
服务员fúwùyuánwaiter; server
还是háishior (in questions)
有点儿yǒudiǎnra little (+ negative feeling)
贵guìexpensive
好喝hǎohētasty (for drinks)

Grammar note 语法点

还是 vs 或者 — two kinds of 'or'

In QUESTIONS use 还是: 热的还是冰的? In statements use 或者: 我喝茶或者咖啡都可以.

你要茶还是咖啡? — Would you like tea or coffee?

Check yourself 小测验

1. What kind of coffee does the writer order?
2. How much does the coffee cost?
3. Why does the writer want to drink it every day?
