小狗和小猫

Xiǎogǒu hé xiǎomāo
Dog and Cat · 46 words · ~1 min
我wǒ家jiā有yǒu一个yí ge小狗xiǎogǒu，也yě有yǒu一个yí ge小猫xiǎomāo。
My family has a dog, and also has a cat.
小狗xiǎogǒu很hěn大dà，小猫xiǎomāo很hěn小xiǎo。
The dog is big, the cat is small.
小狗xiǎogǒu喜欢xǐhuan吃chī东西dōngxi，小猫xiǎomāo喜欢xǐhuan喝hē水shuǐ。
The dog likes to eat, the cat likes to drink water.
小狗xiǎogǒu和hé小猫xiǎomāo都dōu很hěn漂亮piàoliang。
Both the dog and the cat are pretty.
我wǒ很hěn喜欢xǐhuan小狗xiǎogǒu，也yě很hěn喜欢xǐhuan小猫xiǎomāo。
I like the dog a lot, and I also like the cat a lot.

Grammar note 语法点

很 + adjective — adjective predicate

To describe something in Chinese you often just use 很 plus an adjective, with no separate word for "is".

小狗很大，小猫很小。 — The dog is big, the cat is small.
也 — also

也 goes right before the verb to add another true statement about the same subject.

我家有一个小狗，也有一个小猫。 — My family has a dog, and also has a cat.
都 — both / all

都 comes after the two things it describes and before 很 or the verb.

小狗和小猫都很漂亮。 — Both the dog and the cat are pretty.

Check yourself 小测验

1. How big is the dog?
2. What does the cat like to drink?
3. According to the text, are the dog and cat pretty?
