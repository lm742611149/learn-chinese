茶文化Chá wénhuàTea Culture

HSK 3 reading practice · 163 words · ~3 min
星期天xīngqītiān，朋友péngyou小张Xiǎo Zhāng带dài我wǒ去qù了le一家yì jiā很hěn老lǎo的de茶馆cháguǎn。
On Sunday, my friend Xiao Zhang took me to a very old teahouse.
他tā的de爷爷yéye常常chángcháng在zài那里nàlǐ喝hē茶chá，爷爷yéye说shuō：“在zài中国Zhōngguó，喝hē茶chá不bù只zhǐ是shì喝hē水shuǐ，也yě是shì一种yì zhǒng文化wénhuà。”
His grandpa often drinks tea there. Grandpa said, "In China, drinking tea is not just drinking water — it is also a culture."
中国Zhōngguó茶chá有yǒu很hěn多duō种zhǒng，绿茶lǜchá、红茶hóngchá都dōu很hěn有名yǒumíng。
There are many kinds of Chinese tea; green tea and black tea are both very famous.
爷爷yéye教jiāo我wǒ倒dào茶chá：给gěi客人kèrén倒dào茶chá的de时候shíhou，不要búyào倒dào得de太tài满mǎn。
Grandpa taught me how to pour tea: when pouring tea for a guest, don't fill the cup too full.
他tā笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“杯子bēizi太tài满mǎn，客人kèrén就jiù不bù好hǎo拿ná了le。”
He said with a smile, "If the cup is too full, it becomes hard for the guest to pick up."
我们wǒmen一边yìbiān喝hē茶chá，一边yìbiān听tīng爷爷yéye讲jiǎng过去guòqù的de事shì，一个yí ge下午xiàwǔ很hěn快kuài就jiù过去guòqù了le。
We drank tea while listening to Grandpa's stories about the past, and the whole afternoon slipped by quickly.
第一dì yī杯bēi茶chá有点儿yǒudiǎnr热rè，我wǒ喝hē得de很hěn慢màn，但是dànshì越yuè喝hē越yuè觉得juéde好喝hǎohē。
The first cup of tea was a little hot, so I drank it slowly — but the more I drank, the better it tasted.
回家huíjiā的de时候shíhou，爷爷yéye送sòng了le我wǒ一点儿yìdiǎnr茶chá，还hái说shuō：“茶馆cháguǎn的de门mén天天tiāntiān开kāi着zhe，欢迎huānyíng你nǐ常cháng来lái！”
When I was leaving, Grandpa gave me a little tea and said, "The teahouse door is open every day — you're welcome to come often!"

Grammar note 语法点

越 A 越 B — the more A, the more B

Shows one thing growing along with another: 我越喝越觉得好喝。

雨越下越大。 — The rain is getting heavier and heavier.
Verb + 着 — a state that continues

着 after a verb shows a lasting state, not an action: 茶馆的门天天开着。

商店的灯还开着。 — The shop's lights are still on.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Who took the writer to the teahouse?
2. When pouring tea for a guest, what should you NOT do?
3. What did Grandpa give the writer to take home?
