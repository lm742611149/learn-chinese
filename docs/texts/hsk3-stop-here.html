停一下Tíng yíxiàStop Here in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 129 words · ~2 min
我wǒ要yào去qù朋友péngyou的de公司gōngsī，可是kěshì不bù知道zhīdào怎么zěnme走zǒu，就jiù打车dǎchē去qù了le。
I needed to go to my friend's company, but I didn't know the way, so I took a taxi.
出租车chūzūchē开kāi了le二十èrshí分钟fēnzhōng，前面qiánmian的de车chē突然tūrán多duō了le，车chē走zǒu得de很hěn慢màn。
The taxi had been driving for twenty minutes when the traffic ahead suddenly got heavy, and the car moved very slowly.
我wǒ看kàn了le看kàn时间shíjiān，怕pà迟到chídào，就jiù跟gēn司机sījī说shuō：“师傅shīfu，前面qiánmian帮bāng我wǒ停tíng一下yíxià。”
I checked the time, and worried I'd be late, I said to the driver: "Excuse me, please pull over up ahead."
司机sījī说shuō：“好hǎo，是shì这儿zhèr吗ma？”
The driver said: "OK, is it here?"
我wǒ说shuō：“再zài往wǎng前qián一点儿yìdiǎnr，就jiù停tíng这儿zhèr吧ba，我wǒ自己zìjǐ走zǒu过去guòqù。”
I said: "A little further, just stop here — I'll walk the rest of the way myself."
车chē停tíng下xià以后yǐhòu，我wǒ给gěi了le司机sījī钱qián，说shuō了le谢谢xièxie，就jiù下车xiàchē了le。
After the car stopped, I gave the driver money, said thank you, and got out.
我wǒ走zǒu了le两liǎng分钟fēnzhōng就jiù到dào了le公司gōngsī，那nà辆liàng出租车chūzūchē还hái在zài后面hòumian呢ne。
I walked for two minutes and reached the company — that taxi was still behind me.

Key words 生词

打车dǎchēto take a taxi
师傅shīfumaster (polite address for a driver)
停tíngto stop
前面qiánmianahead; in front
突然tūránsuddenly
怕pàto be afraid; to worry

Grammar note 语法点

师傅，前面帮我停一下（Excuse me, please stop up ahead）

师傅 is how you address a taxi or bus driver, and 停（一下）asks them to pull over — the key phrase for telling a driver where to stop.

师傅，前面帮我停一下。— Excuse me, please pull over up ahead.
就停这儿吧（just stop right here）

就 right before 停 pins down the exact spot you want the driver to stop, and 吧 softens it into a suggestion.

再往前一点儿，就停这儿吧，我自己走过去。— A little further, just stop here — I'll walk the rest of the way myself.
V + 得 + adjective（describing how an action happens）

得 connects a verb to a word describing the manner of the action, such as how slowly traffic is moving.

车走得很慢。— The car was moving very slowly.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Why does the narrator decide to take a taxi?
2. What does the narrator say to the driver?
3. What happens after the narrator gets out and walks?
