宠物经济

Chǒngwù jīngjì
The Pet Economy · 205 words · ~3 min
我wǒ朋友péngyou小雨Xiǎoyǔ没有méiyǒu孩子háizi，却què养yǎng了le一yì只zhī猫māo，每měi个ge月yuè花huā在zài猫māo身上shēnshang的de钱qián比bǐ她tā自己zìjǐ吃饭chīfàn还hái多duō。
My friend Xiaoyu has no children, but she keeps a cat — the money she spends on the cat each month is even more than what she spends on herself for food.
猫粮māoliáng要yào买mǎi进口jìnkǒu的de，玩具wánjù每měi个ge月yuè换huàn新xīn的de，连lián猫砂māoshā都dōu要yào选xuǎn进口jìnkǒu品牌pǐnpái。
The cat food has to be imported, the toys get replaced every month, and even the cat litter has to be an imported brand.
上个月shàng ge yuè猫māo感冒gǎnmào，去qù宠物chǒngwù医院yīyuàn看病kànbìng，一yí次cì就jiù花huā了le两千liǎngqiān多duō块kuài钱qián。
Last month the cat caught a cold, and just one visit to the pet hospital cost more than two thousand yuan.
小雨Xiǎoyǔ说shuō：“人rén生病shēngbìng去qù医院yīyuàn不一定bù yídìng花huā这么zhème多duō钱qián，可是kěshì猫māo生病shēngbìng，我wǒ不能bùnéng省shěng。”
Xiaoyu said: "When a person gets sick and goes to the hospital, it doesn't necessarily cost this much money, but when the cat gets sick, I can't cut corners."
像xiàng小雨Xiǎoyǔ这样zhèyàng的de人rén越来越yuèláiyuè多duō，这zhè几jǐ年nián“宠物经济chǒngwù jīngjì”这个zhège词cí也yě越来越yuèláiyuè常见chángjiàn。
There are more and more people like Xiaoyu, and in recent years the term "pet economy" has also become more and more common.
数据shùjù显示xiǎnshì，中国Zhōngguó有yǒu超过chāoguò一yí亿yì只zhī宠物chǒngwù猫狗māo gǒu，相关xiāngguān的de市场shìchǎng一yì年nián能néng达到dádào几jǐ千亿qiānyì元yuán。
Data shows that China has more than one hundred million pet cats and dogs, and the related market can reach hundreds of billions of yuan a year.
从cóng宠物chǒngwù食品shípǐn、宠物chǒngwù医院yīyuàn，到dào宠物chǒngwù摄影shèyǐng、宠物chǒngwù保险bǎoxiǎn，几乎jīhū每měi个ge行业hángyè都dōu在zài为wèi宠物chǒngwù服务fúwù。
From pet food and pet hospitals to pet photography and pet insurance, almost every industry is serving pets.
有人yǒurén觉得juéde这zhè是shì浪费làngfèi钱qián，可是kěshì对duì小雨Xiǎoyǔ来说láishuō，猫māo早就zǎojiù是shì家人jiārén，不bú是shì宠物chǒngwù那么nàme简单jiǎndān。
Some people feel it's a waste of money, but to Xiaoyu, the cat has long been family — not as simple as just being a pet.
她tā说shuō：“它tā不会búhuì说话shuōhuà，但是dànshì它tā陪着péizhe我wǒ，这种zhèzhǒng陪伴péibàn，多少duōshao钱qián都dōu买不到mǎibudào。”
She said: "It can't talk, but it keeps me company — no amount of money can buy this kind of companionship."

Grammar note 语法点

越来越……

'More and more...' — describes a gradually increasing trend.

像小雨这样的人越来越多。
连……都……

'Even...' — emphasizes an extreme example.

连猫砂都要选进口品牌。
对……来说

'As for...; to...' — introduces a personal perspective.

对小雨来说，猫早就是家人。

Check yourself 小测验

1. 小雨的猫感冒后去宠物医院，一次花了多少钱？
2. 根据数据，中国大约有多少只宠物猫狗？
3. 对小雨来说，猫是什么？
