电子邮件Diànzǐ yóujiànWriting an Email in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 107 words · ~2 min
今天jīntiān早上zǎoshang，我wǒ收到shōudào一个yí ge电子邮件diànzǐ yóujiàn，是shì经理jīnglǐ写xiě的de。
This morning, I received an email; it was written by my manager.
电子邮件diànzǐ yóujiàn里lǐ说shuō，今天jīntiān下午xiàwǔ五点wǔ diǎn以前yǐqián要yào把bǎ计划jìhuà发fā给gěi他tā。
The email said that I needed to send him the plan before five o'clock this afternoon.
我wǒ看kàn了le看kàn时间shíjiān，只有zhǐyǒu三个sān ge小时xiǎoshí了le，有点儿yǒudiǎnr着急zháojí。
I checked the time; there were only three hours left, and I felt a little anxious.
我wǒ马上mǎshàng开始kāishǐ写xiě计划jìhuà，一边yìbiān写xiě一边yìbiān检查jiǎnchá。
I immediately started writing the plan, checking it as I went.
写xiě完wán以后yǐhòu，我wǒ又yòu看kàn了le一遍yí biàn，没有méiyǒu发现fāxiàn错误cuòwù。
After I finished writing, I read it over once more and found no mistakes.
四点半sì diǎn bàn，我wǒ把bǎ计划jìhuà发fā给gěi了le经理jīnglǐ。
At four thirty, I sent the plan to my manager.
五分钟wǔ fēnzhōng以后yǐhòu，经理jīnglǐ给gěi我wǒ发fā来lái一条yì tiáo消息xiāoxi：“谢谢xièxie你nǐ，写xiě得de很hěn好hǎo！”
Five minutes later, my manager sent me a message: 'Thank you, this is written very well!'
我wǒ终于zhōngyú放心fàngxīn了le，心里xīnli很hěn开心kāixīn。
I finally felt relieved, and I was very happy inside.

Grammar note 语法点

一边……一边……(doing two things at the same time)

一边 before each of two verbs shows both actions happen together.

我一边写一边检查。
把 + object + verb + 给 + person

The 把 structure moves the object before the verb; 给 then introduces the person who receives the result.

我把计划发给了经理。
V + 了 + 一遍 (to do something through once)

一遍 after a verb counts one complete pass through an action.

我又看了一遍，没有发现错误。

Check yourself 小测验

1. 经理要求几点以前发计划？
2. 收到邮件的时候，我的心情怎么样？
3. 经理看了计划以后说什么？
