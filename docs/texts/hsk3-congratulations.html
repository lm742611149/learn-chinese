恭喜GōngxǐCongratulations in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 99 words · ~2 min
我wǒ的de朋友péngyou小李Xiǎo Lǐ找zhǎo工作gōngzuò找zhǎo了le很久hěnjiǔ，一直yìzhí没méi找到zhǎodào。
My friend Xiao Li had been job-hunting for a long time, and hadn't found one.
上个shàng ge星期xīngqī，他tā去qù了le一家yì jiā公司gōngsī面试miànshì。
Last week, he went to interview at a company.
昨天zuótiān，公司gōngsī打电话dǎ diànhuà告诉gàosu他tā，可以kěyǐ去qù上班shàngbān了le。
Yesterday, the company called and told him he could start work.
小李Xiǎo Lǐ很hěn开心kāixīn，马上mǎshàng给gěi我wǒ打电话dǎ diànhuà，告诉gàosu我wǒ这个zhège好hǎo消息xiāoxi。
Xiao Li was so happy, he called me right away and told me the good news.
我wǒ说shuō：“恭喜gōngxǐ你nǐ，终于zhōngyú找到zhǎodào工作gōngzuò了le！”
I said: "Congratulations, you finally found a job!"
晚上wǎnshang，几个jǐ ge同学tóngxué一起yìqǐ请qǐng他tā吃饭chīfàn，大家dàjiā都dōu对duì他tā说shuō：“恭喜gōngxǐ！”
In the evening, a few classmates treated him to a meal together, and everyone said to him: "Congratulations!"
小李Xiǎo Lǐ拿ná起qǐ杯子bēizi，说shuō：“谢谢xièxie大家dàjiā，下次xià cì我wǒ请客qǐngkè。”
Xiao Li raised his glass: "Thank you all — next one's on me."

Key words 生词

恭喜gōngxǐcongratulations
找到zhǎodàoto find
面试miànshìinterview
终于zhōngyúfinally
消息xiāoxinews; message
请客qǐngkèto treat (someone to something)

Grammar note 语法点

恭喜 + person (Congratulations, ...)

恭喜 is used to congratulate someone on good news — finding a job, passing an exam, getting married, or having a baby. Add 你 to speak directly to the person, or just say 恭喜恭喜 to a group.

恭喜你，终于找到工作了！— Congratulations, you finally found a job!
找到 (to successfully find)

找 means 'to look for', but 到 after it shows the search succeeded — 找到 means 'found it', while 没找到 means 'didn't find it.'

他找了很久，一直没找到。— He looked for a long time, and hadn't found one.
verb + 了 + duration (how long you did something)

Put 了 right after the verb, then add how long — 找了很久 means 'looked for a long time.'

他找工作找了很久。— He had been job-hunting for a long time.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Why had Xiao Li been unhappy before the good news?
2. What does the narrator say when Xiao Li shares his news?
3. What does Xiao Li promise at the end?
