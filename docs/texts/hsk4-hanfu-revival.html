HSK 4 · Upper Int.

汉服复兴

Hànfú fùxīng
The Hanfu Revival · 169 words · ~3 min
最近zuìjìn几jǐ年nián，穿着chuānzhuó汉服hànfú在zài街上jiē shang散步sànbù、拍照pāizhào的de年轻人niánqīngrén越来越yuè lái yuè多duō。
In recent years, more and more young people have been strolling and taking photos on the streets while wearing hanfu.
长长chángcháng的de衣袖yīxiù、精致jīngzhì的de花纹huāwén，让ràng人rén一yì看kàn就jiù想到xiǎngdào古代gǔdài的de诗词shīcí和hé故事gùshi。
The long sleeves and exquisite patterns make people think of ancient poetry and stories at first glance.
对duì不少bùshǎo年轻人niánqīngrén来说láishuō，穿chuān汉服hànfú不仅bùjǐn是shì好看hǎokàn，更gèng是shì一yì种zhǒng表达biǎodá对duì传统chuántǒng文化wénhuà的de热爱rè'ài。
For quite a few young people, wearing hanfu is not only about looking good, but also a way of expressing love for traditional culture.
不过búguò，也yě有yǒu人rén质疑zhìyí：这些zhèxiē人rén是shì真的zhēn de了解liǎojiě汉服hànfú背后bèihòu的de历史lìshǐ，还是háishi只是zhǐshì为了wèile拍出pāichū好看hǎokàn的de照片zhàopiàn？
However, some people question: do these people really understand the history behind hanfu, or are they only doing it to take good-looking photos?
事实上shìshí shang，一yí件jiàn真正zhēnzhèng的de汉服hànfú，从cóng布料bùliào到dào剪裁jiǎncái都dōu有yǒu讲究jiǎngjiu，并不是bìng bú shì随便suíbiàn穿穿chuānchuan就jiù行xíng的de。
In fact, a genuine hanfu takes great care from the fabric to the tailoring — it is definitely not something you can just casually put on.
于是yúshì，一些yìxiē爱好者àihàozhě开始kāishǐ认真rènzhēn研究yánjiū古代gǔdài服饰fúshì，甚至shènzhì自己zìjǐ动手dòngshǒu做zuò衣服yīfu。
So, some enthusiasts have started seriously studying ancient clothing, and even make the clothes themselves.
既然jìrán年轻人niánqīngrén愿意yuànyì花huā时间shíjiān和hé金钱jīnqián去qù了解liǎojiě传统chuántǒng文化wénhuà，这zhè本身běnshēn就是jiùshì一yí件jiàn值得zhídé鼓励gǔlì的de事shì。
Since young people are willing to spend time and money to understand traditional culture, this in itself is something worth encouraging.
我wǒ觉得juéde，无论wúlùn出于chūyú喜欢xǐhuan历史lìshǐ还是háishi喜欢xǐhuan拍照pāizhào，汉服hànfú热rè都dōu让ràng更多gèng duō人rén重新chóngxīn看到kàndào了le传统chuántǒng文化wénhuà的de美měi。
I think, whether out of love for history or love for photography, the hanfu craze has let more people rediscover the beauty of traditional culture.

Grammar note 语法点

对……来说

"As for.../from the perspective of..." introduces whose viewpoint follows: 对不少年轻人来说，穿汉服不仅是好看，更是一种表达对传统文化的热爱。

对我来说，健康比钱更重要。— To me, health matters more than money.
并不是……的

An emphatic negation meaning "is definitely not...": 并不是随便穿穿就行的。

这件事并不是他一个人的错。— This matter is definitely not his fault alone.
