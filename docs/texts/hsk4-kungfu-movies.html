HSK 4 · Upper Int.

功夫电影

Gōngfu diànyǐng
Kung Fu Movies · 144 words · ~2 min
说起shuōqǐ中国Zhōngguó电影diànyǐng，很多hěn duō外国人wàiguórén第一dì-yī个ge想到xiǎngdào的de就是jiù shì功夫gōngfu电影diànyǐng。
Speaking of Chinese movies, the first thing many foreigners think of is kung fu movies.
从cóng李小龙Lǐ Xiǎolóng到dào成龙Chéng Lóng，这些zhèxiē演员yǎnyuán让ràng世界shìjiè认识rènshi了le中国Zhōngguó功夫gōngfu。
From Bruce Lee to Jackie Chan, these actors made the world know about Chinese kung fu.
功夫gōngfu电影diànyǐng里li，主人公zhǔréngōng常常chángcháng从cóng一yí个ge普通人pǔtōngrén，通过tōngguò努力nǔlì，变成biànchéng一yí个ge很hěn厉害lìhai的de人rén。
In kung fu movies, the protagonist often goes from an ordinary person, through hard work, to become someone truly formidable.
这样zhèyàng的de故事gùshi让ràng人rén觉得juéde，只要zhǐyào坚持jiānchí练习liànxí，谁shéi都dōu可以kěyǐ变得biàn de更gèng强qiáng。
Stories like this make people feel that as long as you keep practicing, anyone can become stronger.
除了chúle打架dǎjià，功夫gōngfu电影diànyǐng也yě经常jīngcháng讲jiǎng友情yǒuqíng、家庭jiātíng和hé责任zérèn。
Besides fighting, kung fu movies also often talk about friendship, family, and responsibility.
现在xiànzài的de年轻人niánqīngrén虽然suīrán更gèng喜欢xǐhuan看kàn别的biéde电影diànyǐng，但是dànshì老lǎo功夫片gōngfu piàn还是háishi有yǒu很多hěn duō人rén愿意yuànyì再zài看kàn一yí遍biàn。
Although young people today prefer to watch other movies, there are still many people willing to watch old kung fu films again.
对duì我wǒ来说lái shuō，功夫gōngfu电影diànyǐng最zuì有意思yǒu yìsi的de地方dìfang，是shì那种nàzhǒng“努力nǔlì就jiù能néng变强biàn qiáng”的de简单jiǎndān道理dàolǐ。
For me, the most interesting thing about kung fu movies is the simple truth that "if you work hard, you can become strong."
也许yěxǔ这zhè就是jiù shì为什么wèishénme，几十jǐ shí年nián过去guòqù了le，功夫gōngfu电影diànyǐng还是háishi那么nàme受欢迎shòu huānyíng。
Maybe that's why, even decades later, kung fu movies are still so popular.

Grammar note 语法点

从……到…… — From... to...

Marks a range or progression between a starting point and an ending point.

从李小龙到成龙，这些演员让世界认识了中国功夫。— From Bruce Lee to Jackie Chan, these actors made the world know Chinese kung fu.
只要……都…… — As long as..., everyone/anyone...

States a condition that, once met, applies to every possible subject (谁, 什么, etc.).

只要坚持练习，谁都可以变得更强。— As long as you keep practicing, anyone can become stronger.
虽然……但是…… — Although... but...

Introduces a concession, followed by a contrasting main point.

年轻人虽然更喜欢看别的电影，但是还是有很多人愿意再看老功夫片。— Although young people prefer other movies, many are still willing to watch old kung fu films again.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Which two actors does the text mention as having made the world know Chinese kung fu?
2. Besides fighting, what else do kung fu movies often talk about?
3. What does the author find most interesting about kung fu movies?
