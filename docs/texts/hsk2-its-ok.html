HSK 2 · Elementary

没关系Méi guānxiIt Is OK in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 91 words · ~2 min
下午xiàwǔ，我wǒ要yào去qù电影院diànyǐngyuàn和hé朋友péngyou看kàn电影diànyǐng。
In the afternoon, I'm going to the cinema to watch a movie with my friend.
路上lùshang车chē很hěn慢màn，我wǒ很hěn着急zháojí，跑pǎo到dào电影院diànyǐngyuàn的时候de shíhou已经yǐjīng晚wǎn了le。
Traffic on the way was slow, I got anxious, and by the time I ran to the cinema I was already late.
我wǒ对duì朋友péngyou说shuō：“对不起duìbuqǐ，我wǒ来lái晚wǎn了le。”
I say to my friend: "Sorry, I'm late."
朋友péngyou笑xiào着zhe说shuō：“没关系méi guānxi，电影diànyǐng还hái没méi开始kāishǐ。”
My friend smiles and says: "It's okay, the movie hasn't started yet."
后来hòulái，我wǒ在zài教室jiàoshì忘wàng了le带dài同学tóngxué的de书shū。
Afterward, at school I forgot to bring my classmate's book.
同学tóngxué说shuō：“没事méishì，你nǐ明天míngtiān带dài来lái就jiù可以kěyǐ了le。”我wǒ说shuō：“谢谢xièxie你nǐ。”
My classmate said, "It's nothing — just bring it tomorrow." I said, "Thank you."

Grammar note 语法点

没关系（it's okay）

没关系 is the standard reply when someone apologizes to you.

朋友说：“没关系，电影还没开始。”— Friend says: "It's okay, the movie hasn't started yet."
没事（it's fine）

没事 is a more casual reply to an apology, common between friends and classmates.

同学说：“没事，你明天带来就可以了。”— Classmate says: "It's fine, you can just bring it tomorrow."
跑到 + place + 的时候（by the time you arrive）

到...的时候 marks the moment of arriving somewhere, showing what had already happened by then.

跑到电影院的时候已经晚了。— By the time I ran to the cinema, I was already late.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does 没关系 mean?
2. Why is the narrator late to the cinema?
3. What did the narrator forget to bring to school?
