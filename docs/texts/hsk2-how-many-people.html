HSK 2 · Elementary

几位Jǐ wèiHow Many People in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 70 words · ~1 min
中午zhōngwǔ，我wǒ和hé两liǎng个gè朋友péngyou去qù饭馆fànguǎn吃饭chīfàn。
At noon, my two friends and I went to a restaurant to eat.
服务员fúwùyuán在zài门口ménkǒu问wèn我们wǒmen：“你们nǐmen几jǐ位wèi？”
The waiter asked us at the door: "How many of you?"
我wǒ说shuō：“我们wǒmen三sān位wèi。”
I said: "Three of us."
朋友péngyou问wèn我wǒ：“为什么wèishénme不bù说shuō三sān个gè人rén？”
My friend asked me: "Why not say 'three people'?"
我wǒ告诉gàosu他tā：“问wèn人rén的时候de shíhou，位wèi比bǐ个gè更gèng客气kèqi。”
I told him: "When asking about people, wèi is more polite than gè."
服务员fúwùyuán带dài我们wǒmen坐下zuòxià，给gěi我们wǒmen菜单càidān。
The waiter led us to sit down and gave us menus.

Key words 生词

位wèi(polite measure word for people)
客气kèqipolite
服务员fúwùyuánwaiter; waitress
门口ménkǒudoorway; entrance
菜单càidānmenu
坐下zuòxiàto sit down

Grammar note 语法点

几位？（How many people?）

Waiters and staff use 几位 to politely ask how many people are in a group.

你们几位？— How many of you?
位（polite measure word for people）

位 is used to count people politely, especially by service staff, while 个 is the plain, everyday measure word.

我们三位。— Three of us.
比 + 更（more ... than ...）

比 compares two things, and 更 adds emphasis to the difference.

位比个更客气。— Wèi is more polite than gè.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the waiter ask when the group arrives?
2. Why does the narrator's friend ask a question?
3. According to the narrator, which word is more polite when asking about people?
