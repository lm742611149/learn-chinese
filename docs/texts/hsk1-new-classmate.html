HSK 1 · Newbie

新同学

Xīn tóngxué
The New Classmate · 51 words · ~1 min
今天jīntiān，我们wǒmen班bān有yǒu一yī个gè新xīn同学tóngxué。
Today, our class has a new classmate.
她tā叫jiào小美Xiǎoměi，是shì中国Zhōngguó人rén。
Her name is Xiaomei, she is Chinese.
我wǒ问wèn她tā：“你nǐ今年jīnnián多duō大dà？”
I ask her, "How old are you this year?"
她tā说shuō：“我wǒ十shí岁suì。”
She says, "I am ten years old."
我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ坐zuò，一起yìqǐ学习xuéxí。
We sit together, and study together.
我wǒ很hěn高兴gāoxìng认识rènshi她tā。
I am very happy to meet her.

Key words 生词

同学tóngxuéclassmate
今年jīnniánthis year
岁suìyears old

一起yìqǐtogether
中国ZhōngguóChina

Grammar note 语法点

多大 — asking someone's age

多大 asks about age (literally 'how big'): 你今年多大？= How old are you this year?

你今年多大？ — How old are you this year?
很高兴 + verb — happy to do something

很高兴 followed directly by a verb means 'happy to (do something)': 我很高兴认识她 = I am happy to meet her.

我很高兴认识她。 — I am very happy to meet her.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What is the new classmate's name?
2. How old is she?
3. How does the narrator feel about meeting her?
