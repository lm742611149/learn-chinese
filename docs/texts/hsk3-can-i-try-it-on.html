我试一下Wǒ shì yíxiàCan I Try It On in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 110 words · ~2 min
我wǒ和hé朋友péngyou去qù商店shāngdiàn买mǎi衣服yīfu，我wǒ看见kànjiàn一件yí jiàn衬衫chènshān，颜色yánsè很hěn好看hǎokàn。
My friend and I went to a store to buy clothes, and I saw a shirt whose color looked great.
我wǒ问wèn服务员fúwùyuán：“我wǒ可以kěyǐ试shì一下yíxià吗ma？”
I asked the clerk: "Can I try it on?"
服务员fúwùyuán说shuō：“当然dāngrán可以kěyǐ，镜子jìngzi在zài旁边pángbiān。”
The clerk said: "Of course, the mirror is right beside you."
我wǒ穿上chuānshàng衬衫chènshān，在zài镜子jìngzi前面qiánmiàn看kàn了le看kàn，觉得juéde有点儿yǒudiǎnr大dà。
I put on the shirt, looked at myself in the mirror, and felt it was a bit too big.
我wǒ问wèn服务员fúwùyuán：“有yǒu没有méiyǒu小xiǎo一点yìdiǎn的de号hào？”
I asked the clerk: "Do you have a smaller size?"
服务员fúwùyuán又yòu拿ná来lái一件yí jiàn，我wǒ穿上chuānshàng试shì了le试shì。
The clerk brought me another one, and I put it on and tried it out.
这次zhè cì不bú大dà也yě不bù小xiǎo，很hěn合适héshì。
This time it wasn't too big or too small — it fit just right.
我wǒ付fù了le钱qián，拿ná着zhe衬衫chènshān走zǒu出chū商店shāngdiàn，朋友péngyou还hái在zài旁边pángbiān试shì裙子qúnzi。
I paid, carried the shirt out of the store, and my friend was still nearby trying on a skirt.

Key words 生词

合适héshìfitting; suitable
试shìto try
穿上chuānshàngto put on
号hàosize (number)
衬衫chènshānshirt
有点儿yǒudiǎnra little

Grammar note 语法点

可以...吗？(May I...?)

可以 + verb + 吗 is the everyday way to ask for permission to do something.

我可以试一下吗？— Can I try it on?
有点儿 + adjective (a bit...)

有点儿 goes right before an adjective to soften a complaint, showing something is a little more than you'd like.

觉得有点儿大。— Felt it was a bit too big.
不 A 也不 B (neither A nor B)

Repeating 不 before two opposite adjectives says something lands right in the middle — not too much of either.

不大也不小，很合适。— Not too big or too small — it fit just right.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What did the narrator ask the clerk?
2. How did the shirt feel the first time?
3. What is the narrator's friend doing at the end of the story?
