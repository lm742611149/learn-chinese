HSK 3 · Intermediate

我同意Wǒ tóngyìI Agree in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 98 words · ~2 min
周末zhōumò我们wǒmen几个jǐge朋友péngyou在zài一起yìqǐ吃饭chīfàn，讨论tǎolùn周六zhōuliù去qù哪儿nǎr玩wán。
Over the weekend a few of us friends were eating together, discussing where to go on Saturday.
小美Xiǎoměi说shuō：“我们wǒmen去qù爬山páshān吧ba，天气tiānqì这么zhème好hǎo。”
Xiaomei said: "Let's go hiking, the weather's so nice."
小张Xiǎozhāng说shuō：“我wǒ同意tóngyì，爬山páshān可以kěyǐ运动yùndòng一下yíxià。”
Xiaozhang said: "I agree, hiking's good exercise too."
我wǒ说shuō：“我wǒ也yě这么zhème想xiǎng，我们wǒmen好久hǎojiǔ没有méiyǒu一起yìqǐ出去chūqù了le。”
I said: "I think so too, we haven't gone out together in ages."
于是yúshì我们wǒmen决定juédìng周六zhōuliù早上zǎoshang八点bādiǎn在zài公园gōngyuán门口ménkǒu见面jiànmiàn。
So we decided to meet at the park entrance at eight on Saturday morning.
周六zhōuliù早上zǎoshang，我们wǒmen都dōu到dào了le公园gōngyuán门口ménkǒu，一起yìqǐ去qù爬山páshān。
Saturday morning, we all arrived at the park entrance and went hiking together.
到dào了le山顶shāndǐng，小美Xiǎoměi拿ná出chū手机shǒujī，给gěi我们wǒmen拍pāi了le照片zhàopiàn。
When we got to the top, Xiaomei took out her phone and took a photo of all of us.

Grammar note 语法点

我同意 (I agree)

我同意 is a simple, direct way to agree with what someone just said — literally "I agree."

我同意，爬山可以运动一下。— I agree, hiking is good exercise too.
我也这么想 (I think so too)

也 (also) + 这么想 (think this way) is a natural way to say you feel the same as the person before you.

我也这么想，我们好久没有一起出去了。— I think so too, we haven't gone out together in ages.
于是 (so; as a result)

于是 links two clauses to show the second happened as a natural result of the first.

于是我们决定周六见面。— So we decided to meet on Saturday.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What are the friends deciding at dinner?
2. How does 小张 respond to 小美's suggestion?
3. What do the friends do once they reach the top of the mountain?
