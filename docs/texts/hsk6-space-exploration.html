太空探索值得吗

Tàikōng tànsuǒ zhídé ma
Is Space Exploration Worth It? · 222 words · ~4 min
每当měi dāng有yǒu火箭huǒjiàn升空shēngkōng，社交shèjiāo媒体méitǐ上shàng总会zǒng huì出现chūxiàn同tóng一yí个ge质疑zhìyí：地球dìqiú上shàng还hái有yǒu那么nàme多duō问题wèntí没méi解决jiějué，为什么wèishénme要yào花钱huāqián去qù太空tàikōng？
Whenever a rocket lifts off, the same challenge shows up on social media: there are still so many unsolved problems on Earth, so why spend money going into space?
这个zhège问题wèntí听起来tīng qǐlái很hěn有道理yǒu dàolǐ，却què忽略hūlüè了le一yí个ge事实shìshí：航天hángtiān投入tóurù并没有bìng méiyǒu被bèi“扔rēng进jìn太空tàikōng”，它tā最终zuìzhōng花huā在zài了le地面上dìmiàn shang的de工程师gōngchéngshī和hé工厂gōngchǎng身上shēnshang。
The question sounds reasonable, but it overlooks one fact: money put into spaceflight is not actually "thrown into space" — it ends up being spent on engineers and factories here on the ground.
更何况gèng hékuàng，许多xǔduō今天jīntiān离不开lí bu kāi的de东西dōngxi，都dōu来自láizì航天hángtiān技术jìshù的de意外yìwài收获shōuhuò，比如bǐrú卫星wèixīng导航dǎoháng、气象qìxiàng预报yùbào，以及yǐjí医院yīyuàn里lǐ的de一yí部分bùfen成像chéngxiàng设备shèbèi。
What is more, many things we cannot do without today came out of the unexpected spin-offs of aerospace technology: satellite navigation, weather forecasting, and some of the imaging equipment in hospitals.
不过búguò，怀疑者huáiyízhě的de担忧dānyōu也yě不是bú shì没有méiyǒu道理dàolǐ。
That said, the skeptics' worries are not without foundation either.
当dāng亿万富翁yìwàn fùwēng把bǎ飞船fēichuán当成dàngchéng个人gèrén玩具wánjù的de时候shíhou，普通人pǔtōngrén很难hěn nán感到gǎndào这zhè是shì“人类rénlèi的de探索tànsuǒ”。
When billionaires treat spacecraft as personal toys, ordinary people find it hard to feel that this is "humanity's exploration."
近地jìndì轨道guǐdào上shàng越来越yuèláiyuè拥挤yōngjǐ的de卫星wèixīng和hé碎片suìpiàn，同样tóngyàng是shì一yì笔bǐ迟早chízǎo要yào还huán的de账zhàng。
The satellites and debris crowding low Earth orbit are likewise a bill that will have to be paid sooner or later.
或许huòxǔ更gèng值得zhídé讨论tǎolùn的de不是bú shì要不要yào bu yào去qù，而是érshì怎么zěnme去qù、由yóu谁shéi决定juédìng去qù。
Perhaps what deserves discussion is not whether to go, but how to go and who gets to decide.
一九六八yī jiǔ liù bā年nián，阿波罗Ābōluó八号bā hào的de宇航员yǔhángyuán从cóng月球yuèqiú轨道guǐdào拍pāi下xià了le那nà张zhāng著名zhùmíng的de“地球升起dìqiú shēngqǐ”，第一次dì-yī cì让ràng人类rénlèi看清kànqīng自己zìjǐ住zhù在zài一yì颗kē多么duōme小xiǎo的de星球xīngqiú上shàng。
In 1968 the astronauts of Apollo 8 took the famous "Earthrise" photograph from lunar orbit, letting humankind see for the first time just how small a planet it lives on.
从cóng这个zhège角度jiǎodù看kàn，探索tànsuǒ太空tàikōng与yǔ照顾zhàogù地球dìqiú，未必wèibì是shì一yí道dào非此即彼fēi cǐ jí bǐ的de选择题xuǎnzétí。
Seen from that angle, exploring space and caring for the Earth are not necessarily an either-or question.

Grammar note 语法点

每当…，总…

'Whenever A, B always happens' — 每当 marks a recurring occasion (more formal than 每次), and 总(会/是) states the regular outcome. 例：每当想起那段日子，他总是笑着摇头。

每当有火箭升空，社交媒体上总会出现同一个质疑。 — Whenever a rocket lifts off, the same challenge always appears on social media.
更何况

'What is more; let alone' — adds a further, stronger reason after an argument has already been made, pushing the conclusion harder. 例：他连简单的句子都听不懂，更何况这样的长篇报告。

更何况，许多今天离不开的东西，都来自航天技术的意外收获。 — What is more, many things we cannot do without today came from the unexpected spin-offs of aerospace technology.
也不是没有…（双重否定）

A double negative that concedes a point mildly: 'it is not that there is no…', i.e. there is some. Softer and more balanced than a flat affirmative. 例：这个计划也不是没有风险，只是风险可以控制。

怀疑者的担忧也不是没有道理。 — The skeptics' worries are not without foundation either.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What fact does the writer say the common objection overlooks?
2. Which examples does the text give of unexpected spin-offs from aerospace technology?
3. What does the writer suggest is the more useful question to debate?
