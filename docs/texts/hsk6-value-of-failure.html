失败的价值

Shībài de jiàzhí
The Value of Failure · 191 words · ~3 min
人们rénmen常cháng说shuō“失败shībài是shì成功chénggōng之母zhī mǔ”，似乎sìhū只要zhǐyào经历jīnglì挫折cuòzhé，成功chénggōng便biàn会huì如期而至rúqī ér zhì。
People often say 'failure is the mother of success,' as if success will arrive right on schedule the moment one goes through setbacks.
然而rán'ér，这句zhè jù口号kǒuhào如果rúguǒ被bèi简单jiǎndān地de照搬zhàobān，反而fǎn'ér会huì掩盖yǎngài失败shībài背后bèihòu真正zhēnzhèng复杂fùzá的de问题wèntí。
However, if this slogan is simply copied and applied mechanically, it will instead conceal the truly complex problems behind failure.
事实上shìshí shàng，失败shībài本身běnshēn并不会bìng bú huì自动zìdòng转化为zhuǎnhuà wéi经验jīngyàn，它tā只是zhǐshì提供了tígōng le反思fǎnsī的de素材sùcái。
In fact, failure itself does not automatically turn into experience; it merely provides material for reflection.
一旦yīdàn缺乏quēfá诚实chéngshí而ér深入shēnrù的de总结zǒngjié，同样tóngyàng的de错误cuòwù便biàn会huì反复fǎnfù重演chóngyǎn，甚至shènzhì令lìng人rén一蹶不振yī jué bù zhèn。
Once honest and thorough summarizing is lacking, the same mistakes will repeat themselves over and over, even leaving a person unable to recover.
以yǐ某位mǒu wèi研究yánjiū疫苗yìmiáo的de科学家kēxuéjiā为例wéi lì，他tā历经lìjīng上百次shàng bǎi cì失败shībài的de实验shíyàn，却què依然yīrán坚持不懈jiānchí búxiè。
Take a certain vaccine researcher as an example: he went through over a hundred failed experiments, yet still persevered without giving up.
他tā在zài每一次měi yī cì失败shībài后hòu都dōu详细xiángxì记录jìlù变量biànliàng、修正xiūzhèng假设jiǎshè，最终zuìzhōng因祸得福yīn huò dé fú，找到了zhǎodào le突破性tūpòxìng的de解决方案jiějué fāng'àn。
After every failure he meticulously recorded variables and revised his hypotheses, and in the end turned misfortune into a blessing, finding a breakthrough solution.
反之fǎnzhī，也yě有yǒu创业者chuàngyèzhě屡次lǚcì亏损kūnsǔn却què不愿bú yuàn检讨jiǎntǎo决策juécè失误shīwù，只zhǐ归咎于guījiù yú运气yùnqi不佳bùjiā，非但fēidàn没有méiyǒu引以为戒yǐn yǐ wéi jiè，反而fǎn'ér一再yīzài重蹈覆辙chóng dǎo fù zhé。
Conversely, there are also entrepreneurs who, despite repeated losses, refuse to examine their own decision-making mistakes, blaming only bad luck — not only failing to learn the lesson, but repeating the same mistake again and again.
由此可见yóu cǐ kě jiàn，两者liǎngzhě的de根本gēnběn区别qūbié不在于bú zàiyú失败shībài的de次数cìshù多寡duōguǎ，而ér在于zàiyú是否shìfǒu建立起jiànlì qǐ系统xìtǒng的de复盘fùpán机制jīzhì。
From this it can be seen that the fundamental difference between the two lies not in how many times one fails, but in whether one has built a systematic habit of review.
归根结底guī gēn jié dǐ，与其yǔqí空谈kōngtán失败shībài的de意义yìyì，不如bùrú养成yǎngchéng对duì失败shībài进行jìnxíng系统xìtǒng反思fǎnsī的de习惯xíguàn，唯有wéiyǒu如此rúcǐ，失败shībài才能cáinéng真正zhēnzhèng孕育出yùnyù chū成功chénggōng。
In the final analysis, rather than talking emptily about the meaning of failure, it is better to cultivate a habit of systematically reflecting on it — only then can failure truly give birth to success.

Key words 生词

挫折cuòzhésetback
反思fǎnsīreflection; to reflect on
归咎guījiùto attribute blame to
复盘fùpánto review; debrief (a process)
一蹶不振yī jué bù zhènto collapse after a setback and never recover
因祸得福yīn huò dé fúto turn misfortune into a blessing

Grammar note 语法点

……不在于……，而在于……

'It does not lie in A, but rather in B' — used to redirect the reader's attention from a surface-level factor to the real, deeper factor that actually matters. 例：学习的效果不在于时间的长短，而在于是否用心。

两者的根本区别不在于失败的次数多寡，而在于是否建立起系统的复盘机制。 — The fundamental difference between the two lies not in how many times one fails, but in whether one has built a systematic habit of review.
与其……，不如……

'Rather than A, it would be better to B' — used to weigh two options and argue that the second is the wiser or more worthwhile course of action. 例：与其抱怨环境，不如努力改变自己。

与其空谈失败的意义，不如养成对失败进行系统反思的习惯。 — Rather than talking emptily about the meaning of failure, it is better to cultivate a habit of systematically reflecting on it.

Check yourself 小测验

1. According to the passage, what is wrong with taking the slogan 'failure is the mother of success' at face value?
2. What did the vaccine researcher do differently after each failed experiment, compared to the struggling entrepreneur?
3. According to the passage, what is the real difference between valuable failure and wasted failure?
