HSK 3 · Intermediate

我请客Wǒ qǐngkèMy Treat in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 125 words · ~2 min
晚上wǎnshang，我wǒ和hé朋友péngyou一起yìqǐ去qù饭馆fànguǎn吃饭chīfàn，点diǎn了le很多hěn duō菜cài。
In the evening, my friend and I went to a restaurant together and ordered a lot of dishes.
吃chī完wán饭fàn，我们wǒmen叫jiào服务员fúwùyuán买单mǎidān。
After we finished eating, we called the waiter over to pay the bill.
朋友péngyou想xiǎng付fù钱qián，我wǒ也yě想xiǎng付fù钱qián，我们wǒmen都dōu想xiǎng请客qǐngkè。
My friend wanted to pay, and I wanted to pay too — we both wanted to treat.
服务员fúwùyuán刚gāng说shuō完wán多少钱duōshao qián，我wǒ就jiù抢qiǎng着zhe说shuō：“这次zhècì我wǒ请客qǐngkè！”
The waiter had just said how much it was when I jumped in: "This one's on me!"
朋友péngyou也yě想xiǎng给gěi钱qián，说shuō：“不bù行xíng，今天jīntiān应该yīnggāi我wǒ请客qǐngkè。”
My friend also wanted to pay and said: "No way — today it should be me treating."
我wǒ拿ná着zhe钱qián，跑pǎo到dào服务员fúwùyuán那儿nàr，先xiān把bǎ钱qián给gěi了le她tā。
I grabbed the money, ran over to the waiter, and handed it to her first.
朋友péngyou还hái没méi走zǒu到dào，我wǒ已经yǐjīng把bǎ钱qián给gěi了le服务员fúwùyuán。
Before my friend could get there, I had already handed the money to the waiter.
出chū了le饭馆fànguǎn，朋友péngyou说shuō：“那nà我wǒ请qǐng你nǐ喝hē一杯yì bēi咖啡kāfēi吧ba！”
Once we were outside the restaurant, my friend said: "Then let me buy you a cup of coffee!"

Key words 生词

请客qǐngkèto treat (someone to a meal)
买单mǎidānto pay the bill (at a restaurant)
付fùto pay
抢qiǎngto rush to do something first
应该yīnggāishould
咖啡kāfēicoffee

Grammar note 语法点

抢着 + verb (to rush to be the one who does it)

抢 means 'to grab/rush', and 抢着 + verb shows someone hurrying to do something before anyone else can.

我就抢着说：“这次我请客！”— I jumped in: "This one's on me!"
还没 A，已经 B (before A happened, B already had)

还没 + verb pairs with 已经 + verb to show one thing beat another to happen.

朋友还没走到，我已经把钱给了服务员。— Before my friend could get there, I had already handed the money to the waiter.
那 + clause (in that case, ...)

那 at the start of a sentence means 'in that case' or 'then', responding to what just happened.

那我请你喝一杯咖啡吧！— Then let me buy you a cup of coffee!

Check yourself 小测验

1. What did both the narrator and the friend want to do when the bill came?
2. How did the narrator manage to pay first?
3. What did the friend do after losing the race to pay?
