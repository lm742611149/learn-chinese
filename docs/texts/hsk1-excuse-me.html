HSK 1 · Newbie

请问QǐngwènExcuse Me in Chinese

HSK 1 reading practice · 45 words · ~1 min
今天jīntiān早上zǎoshang，我wǒ在zài外面wàimiàn，看见kànjiàn一个yí ge老师lǎoshī。
This morning, I'm outside, and I see a teacher.
我wǒ说shuō：“老师lǎoshī，请问qǐngwèn，现在xiànzài几点jǐ diǎn？”
I say, "Teacher, excuse me, what time is it now?"
老师lǎoshī说shuō：“现在xiànzài七点qī diǎn。”
The teacher says, "It's seven o'clock now."
我wǒ说shuō：“谢谢xièxie！”
I say, "Thank you!"
现在xiànzài，我wǒ去qù学校xuéxiào了le。
Now, I go to school.

Key words 生词

请问qǐngwènexcuse me; may I ask
看见kànjiànto see
老师lǎoshīteacher
现在xiànzàinow
几点jǐ diǎnwhat time

Grammar note 语法点

请问 — polite way to open a question

Say 请问 before asking a stranger something; it politely gets their attention first, like "Excuse me, ..."

请问，现在几点？— Excuse me, what time is it now?
几点 — asking what time it is

几点 asks for a clock time; put it right after 现在 to ask "what time is it now?"

现在几点？— What time is it now?
number + 点 — telling time

To say a specific hour, put a number directly before 点.

现在七点。— It's seven o'clock now.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does 请问 mean before asking a question?
2. What time does the teacher say it is?
3. What does the narrator do after thanking the teacher?
