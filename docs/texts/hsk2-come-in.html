HSK 2 · Elementary

请进Qǐng jìnCome In in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 99 words · ~2 min
晚上wǎnshang，我wǒ在zài家jiā写xiě作业zuòyè，门口ménkǒu有yǒu人rén叫jiào我wǒ。
One evening I was at home doing homework when someone called out at my door.
我wǒ问wèn：“谁shéi？”
I ask: "Who is it?"
外面wàimiàn说shuō：“是shì我wǒ，你nǐ的de同学tóngxué。”
From outside, she says: "It's me, your classmate."
我wǒ笑xiào着zhe走zǒu到dào门口ménkǒu，说shuō：“请qǐng进jìn，请qǐng坐zuò！”
I smile, walk to the door, and say: "Please come in, please sit!"
同学tóngxué进jìn来lái，看见kànjiàn我wǒ的de桌子zhuōzi上shàng有yǒu很多hěn duō书shū，说shuō：“你nǐ在zài忙máng吗ma？”
My classmate comes in, sees there are lots of books on my table, and says: "Are you busy?"
我wǒ说shuō：“不太bú tài忙máng，我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ做zuò作业zuòyè吧ba。”
I say: "Not too busy, let's do homework together."
她tā坐下zuòxià，说shuō：“好hǎo，我们wǒmen开始kāishǐ吧ba！”
She sits down and says: "Okay, let's get started!"

Key words 生词

请qǐngplease
进jìnto go in; to enter
门口ménkǒudoorway
谁shéiwho
同学tóngxuéclassmate
坐下zuòxiàto sit down

Grammar note 语法点

请 + verb（a polite invitation）

请 before an action politely invites someone to do it, like English 'please'.

请进，请坐！— Please come in, please sit!
进 vs 进来（to enter / to come in）

进 alone just means 'to enter'; adding 来 (进来) frames the movement as coming toward the speaker.

同学进来，看见我的桌子上有很多书。— My classmate comes in and sees lots of books on my table.
在 + verb（doing something right now）

在 right before a verb shows the action is happening at this moment.

你在忙吗？— Are you busy right now?

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does 请进 mean?
2. Who is at the door?
3. What do they decide to do together?
