我累了Wǒ lèi leI Am Tired in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 75 words · ~1 min
今天jīntiān我wǒ很hěn忙máng，从cóng早上zǎoshang忙máng到dào晚上wǎnshang。
Today I'm very busy, busy from morning until evening.
下班xiàbān以后yǐhòu，我wǒ坐zuò公共汽车gōnggòng qìchē回家huí jiā。
After work, I take the bus home.
到家dào jiā以后yǐhòu，我wǒ坐zuò在zài沙发shāfā上shàng，说shuō：“我wǒ累lèi了le。”
After I get home, I sit on the sofa and say: "I'm tired."
妈妈māma问wèn我wǒ：“你nǐ今天jīntiān很hěn忙máng吗ma？”
Mom asks me: "Were you very busy today?"
我wǒ说shuō：“对duì，太tài累lèi了le，我wǒ想xiǎng休息xiūxi一下yíxià。”
I say: "Yes, I'm so tired, I want to rest a bit."
说shuō完wán，我wǒ在zài沙发shāfā上shàng睡shuì了le一会儿yíhuìr。
After I finish talking, I sleep on the sofa for a little while.

Key words 生词

累lèitired
忙mángbusy
下班xiàbānto get off work

沙发shāfāsofa

Grammar note 语法点

从...到...（from...to...）

从 marks the starting point and 到 the ending point, for time or place.

我从早上忙到晚上。— I was busy from morning until evening.
太 + adj + 了（too...!）

太累了 says something is too much — here, too tired.

太累了，我想休息一下。— I'm so tired, I want to rest a bit.
V + 完（to finish doing something）

完 right after a verb shows that action is complete.

说完，我睡了一会儿。— After I finished talking, I slept for a while.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does 我累了 mean?
2. How does the narrator get home from work?
3. What does the narrator do after talking with mom?
