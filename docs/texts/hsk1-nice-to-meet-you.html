认识你很高兴Rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìngNice to Meet You in Chinese

HSK 1 reading practice · 88 words · ~1 min
今天jīntiān，我wǒ在zài学校xuéxiào，我wǒ看见kànjiàn一yí个gè同学tóngxué，她tā叫jiào小美Xiǎoměi。
Today at school, I see a classmate; her name is Xiaomei.
她tā说shuō：“你nǐ叫jiào什么shénme名字míngzi？”
She says, "What's your name?"
我wǒ说shuō：“我wǒ叫jiào王小明Wáng Xiǎomíng，认识rènshi你nǐ很hěn高兴gāoxìng！”
I say, "My name is Wang Xiaoming. Nice to meet you!"
她tā说shuō：“认识rènshi你nǐ，我wǒ也yě很hěn高兴gāoxìng！你nǐ是shì哪国人nǎ guó rén？”
She says, "Nice to meet you too! What's your nationality?"
我wǒ说shuō：“我wǒ是shì中国Zhōngguó人rén，你nǐ呢ne？”
I say, "I'm Chinese. What about you?"
她tā说shuō：“我wǒ也yě是shì中国人Zhōngguórén，我们wǒmen是shì同学tóngxué，也yě是shì朋友péngyou。”
She says, "I'm Chinese too — we're classmates, and friends as well."

Grammar note 语法点

你叫什么名字? — asking someone's name

To ask someone's name, say 你叫什么名字? The answer follows the pattern 我叫 + name.

你叫什么名字？我叫王小明。 — What's your name? My name is Wang Xiaoming.
认识你很高兴! — nice to meet you

When you meet someone for the first time, say 认识你很高兴! (Nice to meet you!) 认识 means "to know" or "to meet" someone.

认识你很高兴！ — Nice to meet you!
你是哪国人? — asking someone's nationality

To ask where someone is from, say 你是哪国人? Answer with 我是 + country + 人.

你是哪国人？我是中国人。 — What's your nationality? I'm Chinese.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What is the classmate's name?
2. What does the narrator say his name is?
3. What nationality are both of them?
