HSK 3 · Intermediate

借我用一下Jiè wǒ yòng yíxiàCan I Borrow It in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 92 words · ~2 min
我wǒ的de手机shǒujī快kuài没méi电diàn了le，可是kěshì找不到zhǎobudào充电器chōngdiànqì。
My phone is almost out of battery, but I can't find my charger.
我wǒ问wèn同事tóngshì：“你nǐ的de充电器chōngdiànqì能néng借jiè我wǒ用yòng一下yíxià吗ma？”
I asked my coworker: "Can I borrow your charger for a bit?"
她tā说shuō：“当然dāngrán可以kěyǐ，给gěi你nǐ。”
She said: "Of course, here you go."
我wǒ把bǎ手机shǒujī放fàng在zài桌子zhuōzi上shàng，坐zuò在zài她tā旁边pángbiān等děng。
I put my phone on the table and sat next to her to wait.
半个小时bàn ge xiǎoshí后hòu，手机shǒujī的de电diàn够gòu用yòng了le。
Half an hour later, the phone had enough battery.
我wǒ把bǎ充电器chōngdiànqì还给huángěi她tā，说shuō：“谢谢xièxie，你nǐ帮bāng了le我wǒ。”
I returned the charger to her and said: "Thanks, you really helped me."
她tā把bǎ充电器chōngdiànqì放fàng进jìn包bāo里li，然后ránhòu继续jìxù工作gōngzuò。
She put the charger back in her bag, then went on with her work.

Key words 生词

充电器chōngdiànqìcharger
借jièto lend; to borrow
够gòuenough
还给huángěito return (something) to
然后ránhòuthen
继续jìxùto continue

Grammar note 语法点

能 + verb + 一下 + 吗？（polite request）

Adding 一下 after a verb softens the request, and 能...吗 asks politely whether something is possible.

你的充电器能借我用一下吗？— Can I borrow your charger for a bit?
把 + object + verb（the 把-construction）

把 moves the object in front of the verb to stress what happens to it.

我把充电器还给她。— I returned the charger to her.
够 + verb（enough to）

够 before a verb shows there's now enough of something to do that action.

手机的电够用了。— The phone has enough battery now.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Why does the narrator ask to borrow something?
2. What does 能...一下吗？ do in this text?
3. What does the coworker do after getting the charger back?
