网上聊天Wǎngshàng liáotiānChatting Online

HSK 3 reading practice · 93 words · ~2 min
我wǒ有yǒu一个yí ge朋友péngyou住zhù在zài美国Měiguó，我们wǒmen很久hěnjiǔ没méi见面jiànmiàn了le。
I have a friend who lives in America; we haven't seen each other in a long time.
昨天zuótiān晚上wǎnshang，我wǒ开kāi电脑diànnǎo，和hé他tā在zài网上wǎngshàng聊天liáotiān。
Last night, I turned on my computer and chatted with him online.
他tā问wèn我wǒ最近zuìjìn怎么样zěnmeyàng，工作gōngzuò忙不忙máng bu máng。
He asked me how I've been recently, and whether work has been busy.
我wǒ告诉gàosu他tā，我wǒ换huàn了le新xīn工作gōngzuò，也yě搬bān了le新xīn家jiā。
I told him that I've changed to a new job, and also moved to a new home.
他tā说shuō他tā打算dǎsuàn明年míngnián去qù中国Zhōngguó旅游lǚyóu，想xiǎng来lái看kàn我wǒ。
He said he plans to travel to China next year, and wants to come see me.
我wǒ听tīng了le很hěn高兴gāoxìng，马上mǎshàng说shuō好hǎo啊a，欢迎huānyíng你nǐ。
I was happy to hear this, and immediately said, great, welcome!
我们wǒmen聊liáo了le两个多liǎng ge duō小时xiǎoshí，还是háishi觉得juéde时间shíjiān太tài短duǎn了le。
We chatted for more than two hours, but we still felt the time was too short.

Key words 生词

聊天liáotiānto chat
最近zuìjìnrecently
打算dǎsuànto plan
换huànto change
搬bānto move (house)
短duǎnshort

Grammar note 语法点

数字 + 多 + 量词 (more than a number)

多 placed after a number and before the measure word shows the true amount is a bit more than that number.

我们聊了两个多小时。
打算 + verb (to plan to do something)

打算 introduces a plan or intention for the future.

他打算明年去中国旅游。
还是 (still; nevertheless)

还是 shows that despite something happening, a feeling or situation stays the same.

我们聊了两个多小时，还是觉得时间太短了。

Check yourself 小测验

1. 我们在哪儿聊天？
2. 我最近做了什么改变？
3. 我们聊了多长时间？
