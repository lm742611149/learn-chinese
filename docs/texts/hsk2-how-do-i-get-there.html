怎么走Zěnme zǒuHow Do I Get There in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 147 words · ~2 min
星期六xīngqīliù下午xiàwǔ，我wǒ想xiǎng去qù图书馆túshūguǎn看书kànshū，可是kěshì我wǒ不bù知道zhīdào路lù。
On Saturday afternoon, I wanted to go read at the library, but I didn't know the way.
我wǒ在zài路上lùshang看见kànjiàn一个yí ge同学tóngxué，问wèn她tā：“请问qǐngwèn，图书馆túshūguǎn怎么zěnme走zǒu？”
On the road, I saw a classmate, and asked her: "Excuse me, how do I get to the library?"
她tā说shuō：“你nǐ往wǎng前qián走zǒu，过guò了le公园gōngyuán，往wǎng右边yòubiān走zǒu，就jiù到dào了le。”
She said: "Walk straight ahead, pass the park, turn right, and you're there."
我wǒ往wǎng前qián走zǒu，可是kěshì走zǒu错cuò了le，看见kànjiàn的de不bú是shì公园gōngyuán，是shì商店shāngdiàn。
I walked ahead, but I went the wrong way — what I saw wasn't the park, it was a store.
我wǒ给gěi她tā打电话dǎ diànhuà，说shuō：“我wǒ走zǒu错cuò了le，我wǒ在zài商店shāngdiàn这儿zhèr，图书馆túshūguǎn怎么zěnme走zǒu？”
I called her and said: "I went the wrong way, I'm at the store, how do I get to the library?"
她tā说shuō：“商店shāngdiàn旁边pángbiān是shì公园gōngyuán，你nǐ再zài往wǎng前qián走zǒu一点yìdiǎn，往wǎng右边yòubiān走zǒu就jiù到dào了le。”
She said: "Right next to the store is the park — walk ahead a little more, turn right, and you're there."
我wǒ找到zhǎodào了le公园gōngyuán，往wǎng右边yòubiān走zǒu，很快hěn kuài就jiù看见kànjiàn了le图书馆túshūguǎn，找到zhǎodào了le一本yì běn很hěn有意思yǒu yìsi的de书shū，坐下zuòxià看书kànshū。
I found the park, turned right, and quickly saw the library — I found a very interesting book and sat down to read.

Key words 生词

怎么zěnmehow
往wǎngtoward
右边yòubiānright side
错cuòwrong
再zàiagain
有意思yǒu yìsiinteresting

Grammar note 语法点

地方 + 怎么走？(asking how to get somewhere)

Put a place before 怎么走 to ask for the route there.

图书馆怎么走？— How do I get to the library?
走 + 错（to go the wrong way）

错 right after a verb shows the action was done incorrectly.

我走错了，看见的是商店。— I went the wrong way; what I saw was a store.
再 for something not yet done

再 before a verb tells someone to repeat or continue an action that hasn't happened yet.

你再往前走一点。— Walk a little further ahead.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does 图书馆怎么走 ask?
2. What mistake did the writer make on the way?
3. What does the writer do at the end of the story?
