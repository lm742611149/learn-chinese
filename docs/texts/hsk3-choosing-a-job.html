选择工作Xuǎnzé gōngzuòChoosing a Job

HSK 3 reading practice · 106 words · ~2 min
我wǒ要yào找zhǎo新xīn工作gōngzuò，现在xiànzài有yǒu两个liǎng ge机会jīhuì。
I want to find a new job; right now I have two opportunities.
一个yí ge工作gōngzuò离lí家jiā很hěn近jìn，钱qián不bú太tài多duō，但是dànshì很hěn轻松qīngsōng。
One job is close to home; it doesn't pay much, but it's relaxed and easy.
还有hái yǒu一个yí ge工作gōngzuò在zài别的biéde城市chéngshì，钱qián多duō一些yìxiē，可是kěshì要yào常常chángcháng出差chūchāi。
There's also a job in another city that pays a bit more, but I would need to travel for business often.
妈妈māma觉得juéde钱qián多duō的de工作gōngzuò更gèng好hǎo，以后yǐhòu可以kěyǐ买mǎi房子fángzi。
Mom thinks the higher-paying job is better, since later I could buy a house.
可是kěshì我wǒ不想bù xiǎng离开líkāi我的wǒ de家人jiārén和hé朋友péngyou。
But I don't want to leave my family and friends.
我wǒ想xiǎng了le很多hěn duō天tiān，最后zuìhòu选择xuǎnzé了le离lí家jiā近jìn的de工作gōngzuò。
I thought about it for many days, and finally chose the job close to home.
虽然suīrán钱qián不bù多duō，但是dànshì我wǒ每天měitiān都dōu很hěn轻松qīngsōng，也yě很hěn高兴gāoxìng。
Although it doesn't pay much, I feel relaxed every day, and also happy.
我wǒ觉得juéde，这个zhège决定juédìng是shì对duì的de。
I think this decision was the right one.

Grammar note 语法点

虽然……但是……(although ... but ...)

Introduces a fact that contrasts with what follows, showing a concession.

虽然钱不多，但是我每天都很轻松，也很高兴。
更 (comparative: more; -er)

更 is placed before an adjective to compare two things without using 比.

妈妈觉得钱多的工作更好。
了 marking a completed action

了 after a verb shows the action has happened or been completed.

我想了很多天，最后选择了离家近的工作。

Check yourself 小测验

1. 离家近的工作怎么样？
2. 别的城市的工作有什么问题？
3. 最后这个人选择了哪个工作？
