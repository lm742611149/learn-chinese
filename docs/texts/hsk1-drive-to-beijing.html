开车去北京Kāichē qù BěijīngDrive to Beijing

HSK 1 reading practice · 50 words · ~1 min
今天jīntiān天气tiānqì很hěn好hǎo，我们wǒmen开车kāichē去qù北京Běijīng。
Today the weather is very good, and we are driving to Beijing.
现在xiànzài是shì上午shàngwǔ九jiǔ点diǎn，我们wǒmen很hěn高兴gāoxìng。
It is nine in the morning now, and we are very happy.
北京Běijīng有yǒu我们wǒmen的de朋友péngyou，他tā叫jiào王明Wáng Míng。
Beijing has our friend; his name is Wang Ming.
我们wǒmen想xiǎng去qù他tā家jiā喝茶hē chá。
We want to go to his home to drink tea.
王明Wáng Míng说shuō：“你们nǐmen来lái了le，太tài好hǎo了le！”
Wang Ming says, "You've come, that's wonderful!"

Grammar note 语法点

开车 — to drive (a car)

开 (to operate) plus 车 (vehicle) together mean 'to drive'; it works like a normal verb before a destination.

我们开车去北京。 — We are driving to Beijing.
有 — there is / there are

有 says that something exists at a place: place + 有 + person or thing.

北京有我们的朋友。 — Beijing has our friend.
想 + verb — to want to do something

想 sits right before another verb to say you want to do that action.

我们想去他家喝茶。 — We want to go to his home to drink tea.

Check yourself 小测验

1. How is the weather today?
2. What time is it in the morning?
3. What is their friend's name?
