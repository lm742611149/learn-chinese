我不知道Wǒ bù zhīdàoI Don't Know in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 76 words · ~1 min
下午xiàwǔ，一个yí gè学生xuésheng在zài学校xuéxiào门口ménkǒu问wèn我wǒ路lù。
In the afternoon, a student asked me for directions at the school gate.
他tā说shuō：“请问qǐngwèn，图书馆túshūguǎn在zài哪儿nǎr？”
He said, "Excuse me, where is the library?"
我wǒ不bù知道zhīdào图书馆túshūguǎn在zài哪儿nǎr，我wǒ也yě是shì新xīn同学tóngxué。
I don't know where the library is — I'm also a new student.
我wǒ告诉gàosu他tā：“你nǐ可以kěyǐ问wèn李老师Lǐ lǎoshī，她tā知道zhīdào。”
I told him, "You can ask Teacher Li — she knows."
那个nàge学生xuésheng说shuō：“谢谢xièxie你nǐ，我wǒ去qù问wèn她tā。”
The student said, "Thank you — I'll go ask her."
后来hòulái他tā找到zhǎodào了le李老师Lǐ lǎoshī，李老师Lǐ lǎoshī告诉gàosu他tā图书馆túshūguǎn在zài哪儿nǎr。
Afterward, he found Teacher Li, and she told him where the library was.

Key words 生词

知道zhīdàoto know
问wènto ask
路lùroad; way
告诉gàosuto tell
找到zhǎodàoto find
新xīnnew

Grammar note 语法点

不知道 + 疑问句

不知道 is followed directly by a question phrase like 在哪儿 or 是谁 — no 吗 needed.

我不知道图书馆在哪儿。 — I don't know where the library is.
你可以 + V

可以 here softly suggests an action — like saying 'you could' rather than asking permission.

你可以问李老师。 — You could ask Teacher Li.
也 + 是/V

也 goes right before 是 or a verb to mean 'also' or 'too'.

我也是新同学。 — I'm also a new student.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Who asks for directions at the school gate?
2. Why can't the narrator help?
3. Who finally tells the student where the library is?
