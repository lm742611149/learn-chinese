HSK 4 · Upper Int.

假期计划

Jiàqī jìhuà
Holiday Plans · 113 words · ~2 min
暑假shǔjià快kuài到了dàole，我wǒ和hé朋友péngyou一直yìzhí在zài讨论tǎolùn假期jiàqī计划jìhuà。
Summer vacation is coming soon, and my friend and I have been discussing our holiday plans.
我wǒ想xiǎng去qù南方nánfāng看kàn海hǎi，因为yīnwèi北方běifāng的de夏天xiàtiān太tài热rè了le。
I want to go south to see the ocean, because summer in the north is too hot.
朋友péngyou觉得juéde旅行lǚxíng太tài累lèi，他tā更gèng喜欢xǐhuan在家zài jiā看书kàn shū、睡懒觉shuì lǎnjiào。
My friend thinks traveling is too tiring — he prefers staying home to read and sleep in.
我们wǒmen两个人liǎng ge rén的de想法xiǎngfǎ完全wánquán不一样bù yíyàng，谁shéi也yě说服shuōfú不了bùliǎo谁shéi。
Our ideas are completely different, and neither of us can persuade the other.
后来hòulái我们wǒmen决定juédìng各自gèzì安排ānpái自己zìjǐ的de假期jiàqī，互hù不bù打扰dǎrǎo。
In the end, we decided to each arrange our own vacation and not bother each other.
我wǒ订好了dìnghǎole机票jīpiào和hé酒店jiǔdiàn，准备zhǔnbèi去qù海边hǎibiān玩儿wánr一个星期yí ge xīngqī。
I booked my flight and hotel, planning to spend a week at the seaside.
出发chūfā前qián，我wǒ把bǎ工作gōngzuò都dōu安排好了ānpái hǎo le，这样zhèyàng才cái能néng放心fàngxīn地de休息xiūxi。
Before leaving, I arranged all my work so that I could rest with peace of mind.
我wǒ觉得juéde，无论wúlùn假期jiàqī怎么zěnme过guò，重要zhòngyào的de是shì让ràng自己zìjǐ真正zhēnzhèng放松fàngsōng下来xiàlái。
I think that no matter how you spend a vacation, what matters most is truly letting yourself relax.

Key words 生词

讨论tǎolùnto discuss
说服shuōfúto persuade
各自gèzìeach on one's own
放松fàngsōngto relax
无论wúlùnno matter (what/how)
睡懒觉shuì lǎnjiàoto sleep in

Grammar note 语法点

谁也……不了……

"No one can..." 谁 here means "whoever/anyone," and 也 emphasizes that not even one person can do it: 谁也说服不了谁。

这道题太难了，谁也做不出来。— This problem is too hard; no one can solve it.
无论……，……都/就……

"No matter..." 无论 introduces a question word or choice, and the following clause states a result that holds regardless: 无论假期怎么过，重要的是让自己真正放松下来。

无论多忙，他每天都锻炼身体。— No matter how busy he is, he exercises every day.
才

才 indicates that something happens only under a certain condition, often translated as "only then": 我把工作都安排好了，这样才能放心地休息。

你多练习，才能说得流利。— Only by practicing more can you speak fluently.

Check yourself 小测验

1. Why does the author want to go south for vacation?
2. What does the friend prefer to do during vacation, according to the text?
3. According to the last sentence, what does the author think matters most about a vacation?
