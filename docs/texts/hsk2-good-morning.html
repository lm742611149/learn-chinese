HSK 2 · Elementary

早上好Zǎoshang hǎoGood Morning in Chinese

HSK 2 reading practice · 82 words · ~1 min
早上zǎoshang，我wǒ出门chūmén，看见kànjiàn爷爷yéye在zài门口ménkǒu。
In the morning, I go out and see grandpa at the doorway.
我wǒ说shuō：“爷爷yéye，早上zǎoshang好hǎo！”
I say, "Grandpa, good morning!"
爷爷yéye说shuō：“早zǎo！你nǐ也yě早zǎo！”
Grandpa says, "Morning! Morning to you too!"
我wǒ去qù上学shàngxué，在zài路上lùshang看见kànjiàn朋友péngyou小美Xiǎoměi。
I go to school, and on the way I see my friend Xiaomei.
小美Xiǎoměi说shuō：“早上zǎoshang好hǎo！中午zhōngwǔ一起yìqǐ吃饭chī fàn，好hǎo吗ma？”
Xiaomei says, "Good morning! Let's eat lunch together at noon, OK?"
我wǒ说shuō：“好hǎo！中午zhōngwǔ见jiàn！”
I say, "Sure! See you at noon!"
中午zhōngwǔ，我wǒ和hé小美Xiǎoměi一起yìqǐ吃饭chī fàn，我们wǒmen说shuō：“晚上wǎnshang见jiàn！”
At noon, Xiaomei and I eat lunch together, and we say, "See you tonight!"

Key words 生词

早zǎomorning (short greeting)
早上zǎoshangmorning
门口ménkǒudoorway; entrance
上学shàngxuéto go to school
中午zhōngwǔnoon
见jiànto see; to meet

Grammar note 语法点

早上好 vs 早 — two ways to say good morning

早上好 is the full morning greeting; 早 alone is a shorter, casual way to say the same thing.

早！你也早！— Morning! Morning to you too!
time word + 见 — arranging to meet again

Put a time word like 中午 or 晚上 before 见 to say when you'll see someone again.

中午见！— See you at noon!
上午/中午/晚上 — parts of the day

These words mark different times of day and pair with different greetings or plans.

中午一起吃饭，好吗？— Let's eat lunch together at noon, OK?

Check yourself 小测验

1. What is a short, casual way to say "good morning"?
2. What do the narrator and Xiaomei plan to do at noon?
3. What do they say when parting after lunch?
