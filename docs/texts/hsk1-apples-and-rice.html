苹果和米饭Píngguǒ hé mǐfànApples and Rice

HSK 1 reading practice · 41 words · ~1 min
我wǒ喜欢xǐhuan苹果píngguǒ，也yě喜欢xǐhuan米饭mǐfàn。
I like apples, and I also like rice.
苹果píngguǒ是shì水果shuǐguǒ，米饭mǐfàn不bú是shì水果shuǐguǒ。
Apples are fruit, rice is not fruit.
中午zhōngwǔ我wǒ吃chī米饭mǐfàn，不bù吃chī苹果píngguǒ。
At noon I eat rice, not apples.
苹果píngguǒ和hé米饭mǐfàn都dōu很hěn好hǎo。
Both apples and rice are good.

Grammar note 语法点

也 — also

也 sits right before the verb to add another true statement.

我喜欢苹果，也喜欢米饭。 — I like apples, and I also like rice.
不是 — to negate 是

不 goes right before 是 to say something is not the case.

米饭不是水果。 — Rice is not fruit.
都 — both / all

都 comes after the things it covers and before the verb or adjective.

苹果和米饭都很好。 — Both apples and rice are good.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the narrator like?
2. According to the text, is rice a fruit?
3. What did mom buy?
