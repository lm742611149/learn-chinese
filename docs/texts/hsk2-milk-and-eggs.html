喝牛奶吃鸡蛋Hē niúnǎi chī jīdànDrinking Milk and Eating Eggs

HSK 2 reading practice · 71 words · ~1 min
每天měitiān早上zǎoshang，我wǒ都dōu喝hē一杯yì bēi牛奶niúnǎi，吃chī一个yí ge鸡蛋jīdàn。
Every morning, I drink a cup of milk and eat an egg.
妈妈māma说shuō，牛奶niúnǎi和hé鸡蛋jīdàn对duì身体shēntǐ很hěn好hǎo。
Mom says milk and eggs are very good for your health.
以前yǐqián我wǒ不bù喜欢xǐhuan喝hē牛奶niúnǎi，觉得juéde牛奶niúnǎi不bù好喝hǎohē。
Before, I didn't like drinking milk — I thought it didn't taste good.
现在xiànzài我wǒ每天měitiān都dōu喝hē，已经yǐjīng习惯xíguàn了le。
Now I drink it every day — I'm already used to it.
鸡蛋jīdàn可以kěyǐ煮zhǔ着zhe吃chī，也yě可以kěyǐ做zuò鸡蛋汤jīdàn tāng。
Eggs can be eaten boiled, or made into egg soup.
喝hē了le牛奶niúnǎi，吃chī了le鸡蛋jīdàn，我wǒ觉得juéde很hěn有yǒu力气lìqi。
After drinking milk and eating eggs, I feel full of energy.

好喝hǎohētasty (for drinks)

Grammar note 语法点

对 + noun + 好/不好 — 'good/bad for'

对 marks the target that something affects: X 对 Y 好 means 'X is good for Y.'

牛奶和鸡蛋对身体很好。— Milk and eggs are good for your health.
V + 着 + V — doing one action by means of another

着 after a verb can describe the manner of doing something else: 煮着吃 = 'eat (it) by boiling it.'

鸡蛋可以煮着吃。— Eggs can be eaten boiled.
已经...了 — 'already'

已经 + verb (+ 了) shows something has already happened or become true: 已经习惯了 = 'already used to it.'

现在我每天都喝，已经习惯了。— Now I drink it every day; I'm already used to it.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does the writer drink and eat every morning?
2. Did the writer like milk before?
3. Besides boiling, what else can eggs be made into?
