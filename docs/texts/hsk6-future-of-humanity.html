人类的未来

Rénlèi de wèilái
The Future of Humanity · 203 words · ~3 min
把bǎ地球dìqiú四十六亿sìshíliù yì年nián的de历史lìshǐ压缩yāsuō成chéng一yì天tiān，人类rénlèi文明wénmíng出现chūxiàn的de时间shíjiān，还hái不到bú dào最后zuìhòu一yì秒miǎo。
Compress Earth's 4.6-billion-year history into a single day, and the appearance of human civilization takes up less than the final second.
这zhè一yí比喻bǐyù常cháng被bèi用来yònglái提醒tíxǐng人们rénmen：从cóng宇宙yǔzhòu尺度chǐdù看kàn，我们wǒmen的de存在cúnzài何其héqí短暂duǎnzàn。
This metaphor is often used to remind people: on a cosmic scale, our existence is how fleeting.
然而rán'ér，正zhèng是shì这zhè短暂duǎnzàn的de一yí瞬shùn，人类rénlèi发展fāzhǎn出chū了le改变gǎibiàn自身zìshēn命运mìngyùn的de能力nénglì：从cóng基因jīyīn编辑biānjí到dào人工智能réngōng zhìnéng。
Yet it is precisely within this fleeting instant that humanity developed the ability to alter its own fate — from gene editing to artificial intelligence.
技术jìshù乐观lèguān者zhě认为rènwéi，只要zhǐyào科学kēxué不断búduàn进步jìnbù，疾病jíbìng、衰老shuāilǎo甚至shènzhì资源zīyuán匮乏kuìfá，终zhōng将jiāng被bèi一一yīyī攻克gōngkè。
Techno-optimists believe that as long as science keeps advancing, disease, aging, and even resource scarcity will eventually be conquered one by one.
但dàn悲观bēiguān者zhě提醒tíxǐng我们wǒmen，每měi一yí项xiàng强大qiángdà的de技术jìshù，既jì能néng造福zàofú人类rénlèi，也yě能néng被bèi用yòng于yú毁灭huǐmiè。
But pessimists remind us that every powerful technology can both benefit humanity and be used toward destruction.
核hé能néng既jì能néng点亮diǎnliàng万家灯火wàn jiā dēnghuǒ，也yě曾céng带来dàilái毁灭性huǐmièxìng的de灾难zāinàn，这zhè正zhèng是shì技术jìshù双刃剑shuāngrènjiàn性质xìngzhì最zuì直观zhíguān的de例证lìzhèng。
Nuclear energy can both light up the homes of ten thousand families and has once brought devastating disaster — this is the most direct illustration of technology's double-edged nature.
与其yǔqí纠结jiūjié于yú人类rénlèi究竟jiūjìng会huì走向zǒuxiàng繁荣fánróng还是háishi衰亡shuāiwáng，不如bùrú把bǎ精力jīnglì放fàng在zài如何rúhé为wèi技术jìshù设置shèzhì约束yuēshù上shàng。
Rather than agonizing over whether humanity will ultimately head toward prosperity or decline, it is better to put our energy into how to set constraints on technology.
一yí个ge能够nénggòu自我zìwǒ约束yuēshù的de文明wénmíng，才cái有yǒu资格zīgé谈论tánlùn长远chángyuǎn的de未来wèilái。
Only a civilization capable of restraining itself has earned the right to speak of a long-term future.
毕竟bìjìng，决定juédìng人类rénlèi能否néngfǒu延续yánxù下去xiàqù的de，从来cónglái不bù只是zhǐshì技术jìshù本身běnshēn，而是érshì我们wǒmen运用yùnyòng技术jìshù的de智慧zhìhuì。
After all, what determines whether humanity can carry on has never been technology itself alone, but the wisdom with which we wield it.

Grammar note 语法点

既…，也…

Presents two things that hold true at once, often two opposing sides of one thing: 'both... and also...'. 例：这份工作既能锻炼能力，也能积累人脉。

核能既能点亮万家灯火，也曾带来毁灭性的灾难。 — Nuclear energy can both light up the homes of ten thousand families and has once brought devastating disaster.
与其…，不如…

Weighs two courses of action and recommends the latter: 'rather than A, it is better to B'. Often used to redirect a debate toward a more productive question. 例：与其猜测结果，不如动手去验证。

与其纠结于人类究竟会走向繁荣还是衰亡，不如把精力放在如何为技术设置约束上。 — Rather than agonizing over whether humanity will head toward prosperity or decline, it is better to focus on how to constrain technology.
…从来不只是…，而是…

Rejects a narrow, single-cause explanation in favor of a broader one: 'has never been merely A, but rather B'. Common in essays making a deeper, reflective point. 例：真正的成功从来不只是运气，而是长期积累的结果。

决定人类能否延续下去的，从来不只是技术本身，而是我们运用技术的智慧。 — What determines whether humanity can carry on has never been technology itself alone, but the wisdom with which we wield it.

Check yourself 小测验

1. In the compressed-day metaphor, how much of the day does human civilization occupy?
2. What example does the text give to illustrate technology's double-edged nature?
3. According to the text, what ultimately determines whether humanity can carry on?
