你好吗Nǐ hǎo maHow Are You in Chinese

HSK 1 reading practice · 72 words · ~1 min
今天jīntiān早上zǎoshang，我wǒ在zài学校xuéxiào看见kànjiàn朋友péngyou小美Xiǎoměi。
This morning, I see my friend Xiaomei at school.
我wǒ问wèn她tā：“你好nǐ hǎo吗ma？”
I ask her, "How are you?"
小美Xiǎoměi看了看kànlekàn我wǒ，说shuō：“我wǒ很hěn好hǎo，你nǐ呢ne？”
Xiaomei looks at me and says, "I'm very good, and you?"
我wǒ说shuō：“我wǒ也yě很hěn好hǎo，谢谢xièxie。”
I say, "I'm very good too, thank you."
小美Xiǎoměi说shuō：“我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ去qù喝茶hē chá，好hǎo吗ma？”
Xiaomei says, "Let's go drink tea together, OK?"
我wǒ说shuō：“好hǎo！”我们wǒmen一起yìqǐ去qù喝茶hē chá了le。
I say, "Sure!" We go drink tea together.

Key words 生词

你好nǐ hǎohello
好hǎogood
吗ma(question particle)
呢ne(question particle)
谢谢xièxiethank you
一起yìqǐtogether

Grammar note 语法点

你好吗？— asking how someone is

Add 吗 after 你好 to ask how the other person is doing.

你好吗？— How are you?
呢 — asking the same question back

After answering, add 呢 to a pronoun to ask "what about ...?" without repeating the whole question.

我很好，你呢？— I'm good, and you?
…，好吗？— a yes/no tag question

Adding 好吗 to the end of a suggestion turns it into a question asking if the other person agrees.

我们一起去喝茶，好吗？— Let's go get tea together, OK?

Check yourself 小测验

1. What does 呢 mean in "我很好，你呢？"?
2. What do the narrator and Xiaomei do at the end of the story?
3. What does 好吗 mean at the end of a suggestion?
