HSK 3 · Intermediate

我只看看Wǒ zhǐ kànkanJust Looking in Chinese

HSK 3 reading practice · 116 words · ~2 min
我wǒ走zǒu进jìn一家yì jiā商店shāngdiàn，只zhǐ想xiǎng看看kànkan，不bù想xiǎng买mǎi东西dōngxi。
I walked into a store, just wanting to look around — I didn't want to buy anything.
服务员fúwùyuán看见kànjiàn我wǒ，很hěn热情rèqíng地de说shuō：“欢迎huānyíng！需要xūyào帮助bāngzhù吗ma？”
The clerk saw me and said warmly: "Welcome! Do you need any help?"
我wǒ说shuō：“不用búyòng，谢谢xièxiè，我wǒ只zhǐ看看kànkan。”
I said: "No need, thanks — I'm just looking."
服务员fúwùyuán一直yìzhí在zài我wǒ旁边pángbiān，介绍jièshào完wán这个zhège又yòu介绍jièshào那个nàge。
The clerk stayed at my elbow the whole time, moving from one item to the next.
我wǒ看kàn了le很久hěnjiǔ，还是háishi不bù知道zhīdào要yào买mǎi什么shénme。
I looked for a long time, but still didn't know what I wanted to buy.
突然tūrán，我wǒ看见kànjiàn一个yí ge杯子bēizi，跟gēn我wǒ家里jiā lǐ那个nàge坏huài了le的de杯子bēizi很hěn像xiàng。
Suddenly, I saw a cup that looked just like the broken one at my house.
我wǒ拿ná起qǐ杯子bēizi，问wèn服务员fúwùyuán多少钱duōshao qián。
I picked up the cup and asked the clerk how much it cost.
我wǒ付fù了le钱qián，回家huíjiā以后yǐhòu，把bǎ新xīn杯子bēizi放fàng在zài坏huài了le的de那个nàge旁边pángbiān。
I paid, and once I got home, I put the new cup right next to the broken one.

Key words 生词

只zhǐonly
看看kànkanto take a look at
欢迎huānyíngwelcome
不用búyòngno need
热情rèqíngwarm; friendly
突然tūránsuddenly

Grammar note 语法点

只 + verb (only...)

只 goes right before a verb to say that's the only thing you're doing — nothing more.

我只想看看。— I just want to look around.
不用 (no need to...)

不用 politely turns down an offer of help, saying it isn't necessary.

不用，谢谢。— No need, thanks.
跟...很像 (similar to...)

跟 + noun + 很像 compares two things, saying one looks a lot like the other.

跟我家里那个杯子很像。— It looked a lot like the cup at my house.

Check yourself 小测验

1. What did the customer say when the clerk offered help?
2. Why did the customer end up buying a cup?
3. How did the customer pay for the cup?
