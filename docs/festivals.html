Chinese festivals: greetings and words 节日

The main Chinese festivals, when they fall, what people say and the words you will hear, with graded readings and videos where we have them. Most traditional festivals follow the lunar calendar, so their Western dates move from year to year.

春节元宵节清明节端午节七夕中秋节国庆节重阳节生日和婚礼

春节 Spring Festival (Chinese New Year) Chūnjié

When: 1st day of the 1st lunar month; falls between 21 January and 20 February

The biggest holiday of the year. Families travel home for the reunion dinner on New Year's Eve, children get red envelopes, doors get red couplets, and the celebrations run for about two weeks, up to the Lantern Festival.

What to say

新年快乐xīnnián kuàilèHappy New Year
春节快乐Chūnjié kuàilèHappy Spring Festival
过年好guònián hǎoHappy New Year (casual, northern)
恭喜发财gōngxǐ fācáiWishing you prosperity

Words

年夜饭niányèfànNew Year's Eve reunion dinner
红包hóngbāored envelope with money
饺子jiǎozidumplings
春联chūnliánred couplets on the door
春运chūnyùnthe Spring Festival travel rush

元宵节 Lantern Festival Yuánxiāojié

When: 15th day of the 1st lunar month; the first full moon of the year, usually in February

The last day of the New Year celebrations. People eat sweet rice balls, hang and look at lanterns, and guess the riddles written on them.

What to say

元宵节快乐Yuánxiāojié kuàilèHappy Lantern Festival

Words

汤圆tāngyuánsweet glutinous rice balls
元宵yuánxiāorice balls (northern name)
灯笼dēnglonglantern
猜灯谜cāi dēngmíto guess lantern riddles

清明节 Qingming Festival (Tomb-Sweeping Day) Qīngmíngjié

When: one of the 24 solar terms, around 4 or 5 April

A day to remember ancestors: families visit and tidy graves and offer flowers. It is also a spring outing day. Because it is a day of remembrance, people do not wish each other a happy Qingming.

Words

扫墓sǎomùto sweep and tend graves
祖先zǔxiānancestors
踏青tàqīnga spring outing
节气jiéqisolar term

Read about it: 二十四节气 The Twenty-Four Solar Terms (HSK 5)

端午节 Dragon Boat Festival Duānwǔjié

When: 5th day of the 5th lunar month; late May to late June

Remembered through the story of the poet Qu Yuan. People race dragon boats and eat zongzi, sticky rice wrapped in leaves. The usual greeting wishes health rather than happiness.

What to say

端午节安康Duānwǔjié ānkāngWishing you good health this Dragon Boat Festival

Words

粽子zòngzisticky rice dumplings wrapped in leaves
龙舟lóngzhōudragon boat
赛龙舟sài lóngzhōudragon boat racing
屈原Qū YuánQu Yuan, the poet the festival remembers

七夕 Qixi Festival (Chinese Valentine's Day) Qīxī

When: 7th day of the 7th lunar month; usually in August

Based on the legend of the cowherd and the weaver girl, two lovers who can meet only once a year on a bridge of magpies. Today it is a day for couples.

What to say

七夕快乐Qīxī kuàilèHappy Qixi

Words

牛郎Niúlángthe cowherd
织女Zhīnǚthe weaver girl
情人节qíngrénjiéValentine's Day

中秋节 Mid-Autumn Festival Zhōngqiūjié

When: 15th day of the 8th lunar month; mid-September to early October

The night of the fullest moon of the year and a festival of family reunion. People look at the moon, eat and give mooncakes, and tell the story of Chang'e, who lives on the moon.

What to say

中秋节快乐Zhōngqiūjié kuàilèHappy Mid-Autumn Festival
中秋快乐Zhōngqiū kuàilèHappy Mid-Autumn (shorter)

Words

月饼yuèbingmooncake
团圆tuányuánfamily reunion
赏月shǎngyuèto admire the moon
嫦娥Cháng'éChang'e, the goddess of the moon

国庆节 National Day Guóqìngjié

When: 1 October, followed by a week-long holiday

Marks the founding of the People's Republic of China in 1949. The holiday week, the Golden Week, is one of the busiest travel times of the year.

What to say

国庆节快乐Guóqìngjié kuàilèHappy National Day

Words

黄金周huángjīnzhōuGolden Week
放假fàngjiàto have a holiday
旅游lǚyóuto travel

重阳节 Double Ninth Festival Chóngyángjié

When: 9th day of the 9th lunar month; usually in October

Traditionally a day to climb to a high place and look at chrysanthemums; today it is also a day to show respect and care for older people.

What to say

重阳节快乐Chóngyángjié kuàilèHappy Double Ninth Festival

Words

登高dēnggāoto climb to a height
菊花júhuāchrysanthemum
敬老jìnglǎoto respect the elderly

生日和婚礼 Birthdays and weddings shēngrì hé hūnlǐ

When: any day of the year

Not festivals, but the celebrations learners are most often invited to. Noodles for a long life on a birthday, red envelopes and a lot of toasting at a wedding.

What to say

生日快乐shēngrì kuàilèHappy birthday
新婚快乐xīnhūn kuàilèCongratulations on your wedding
恭喜恭喜gōngxǐ gōngxǐCongratulations
干杯gānbēiCheers

Words

长寿面chángshòumiànlongevity noodles
礼物lǐwùgift
婚礼hūnlǐwedding
红包hóngbāored envelope with money
