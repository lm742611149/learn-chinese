Chinese tone changes (tone sandhi) 变调

A few tones change depending on the syllable that follows. There are three rules worth learning: the third tone before another third tone, 一 yī, and 不 bù. Pinyin in dictionaries shows the original tones for the third-tone rule, but on this site (and in most textbooks) 一 and 不 are written with the tone you actually say.

1. Third tone + third tone → second tone + third tone

When two third tones come together, the first one is said as a second tone. 你好 is written nǐ hǎo but said ní hǎo. Pinyin keeps the written ǐ, so you have to apply this one yourself.

Words from the readings where it applies:

可以kěyǐ所以suǒyǐ往往wǎngwǎng水果shuǐguǒ也许yěxǔ只有zhǐyǒu小狗xiǎogǒu小雨xiǎoyǔ很久hěnjiǔ了解liǎojiě
  • 我说：“都可以，我不挑食。”I said: "Either is fine, I'm not picky about food."HSK 2 · 和同事吃午饭
  • 所以昨天下午我去商店看新手机。So yesterday afternoon I went to the shop to look at new phones.HSK 2 · 买手机
  • 今天，中国孩子上学后背的第一首诗，往往还是唐诗。Today, the first poem Chinese children memorize after starting school is still usually a Tang poem.HSK 5 · 唐诗之美

In a run of three or more third tones, group them by meaning and change all but the last in each group: 我很好 wǒ hěn hǎo is usually said wó hén hǎo.

3. 不 bù becomes bú before a fourth tone

WhenSayExamples from the readings
Before a fourth tonebú (second)
不是bú shì不用bú yòng不会bú huì不过búguò不断búduàn不要bú yào不再bú zài不太bú tài
Everywhere elsebù (fourth)
不如bùrú不仅bùjǐn不同bùtóng不少bù shǎo不能bù néng不好bù hǎo不妨bùfáng不该bù gāi

In the middle of a verb phrase 不 is often light and toneless: 听不懂 tīng bu dǒng, 对不起 duì bu qǐ.

Also good to know

  • Half third tone. Before a first, second or fourth tone, a third tone usually just dips low and does not rise again: 很忙 hěn máng, 很高 hěn gāo, 我去 wǒ qù.
  • Neutral tone. Particles such as 吗, 呢, 吧, 的, 了 and the second syllable of doubled family words (妈妈, 爸爸, 哥哥) are short and light, with no tone mark.
