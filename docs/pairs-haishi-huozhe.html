还是 vs 或者: or, in questions and in statements

还是 when you are asking someone to choose, 或者 when you are not.

What is the difference between 还是 and 或者?

Both translate as "or". Use 还是 in a question that asks someone to pick one option: 你喝茶还是咖啡？ Use 或者 in a statement, when either option is fine: 喝茶或者咖啡都可以. 还是 also means "still" and "had better" (还是早点睡吧), which 或者 never does.

还是or in a choice question: 你今天去还是明天去？
或者or in a statement: 周末我看书或者去公园
