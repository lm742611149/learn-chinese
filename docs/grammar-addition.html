How to say both, also and not only in Chinese

Adding a second thing usually needs a marker before the verb — 也, 又, 都 — not just a comma.

都 — both / allHSK 1

How do you say "all" or "both" in Chinese?

都 comes after the things it covers and before the verb or adjective.

苹果和米饭都很好。 — Both apples and rice are good.
Seen in 苹果和米饭小狗和小猫三口人吃饭回家 +5

也 — alsoHSK 1

Where does 也 go in a Chinese sentence?

也 sits right before the verb to add another true statement.

我喜欢苹果，也喜欢米饭。 — I like apples, and I also like rice.
Seen in 苹果和米饭大商店小商店一起吃早饭小狗和小猫 +4

一边…，一边… — doing two things at onceHSK 2

一边 + V1，一边 + V2 describes two actions happening at the same time.

他一边喝茶，一边看报纸。 — He drinks tea while reading the newspaper.
Seen in 看报纸加个微信小心刷牙洗脸 +15

又…又… — both...and...HSK 2

又 + adjective + 又 + adjective describes two qualities happening at the same time.

我觉得又累又饿。 — I feel both tired and hungry.
Seen in 打扫房间着急的一天难忘的生日锻炼身体 +8

又 + verb（to do something again）HSK 2

又 goes before a verb to show an action happening again, already in the past or present.

我又看见杯子。— I see a cup again.
Seen in 怎么说你说什么这个字怎么读

连……都……HSK 4

"Even..." — 连 picks out an extreme example and 都 (or 也) stresses it, as in 人们连机器都没有 (people didn't even have machines).

几百年前人们连机器都没有。 — Hundreds of years ago, people didn't even have machines.
Seen in 长城游记二维码生活微信改变生活极简生活 +2

不仅……而且……HSK 4

"Not only A, but also B" — the 而且 part adds something further and often more important, as in 不仅是美丽的风景，而且是那种让人慢下来的生活.

丽江迷人的地方，不仅是美丽的风景，而且是那种让人慢下来的生活。 — What makes Lijiang charming is not only the beautiful scenery, but also the kind of life that makes people slow down.
Seen in 迷人的丽江中国的高铁火锅与朋友面试技巧 +1

甚至HSK 4

甚至 means "even" and introduces the most extreme or surprising case in a list or statement, as in 我很累，甚至有点儿后悔养了它.

有一段时间我真的很累，甚至有点儿后悔养了它。 — For a while I was really exhausted; I even slightly regretted getting it.
Seen in 养宠物的责任地方口音消费主义文化冲击

不但……而且……HSK 4

"Not only A, but also B" adds a second, often stronger point: 广场舞不但能锻炼身体，而且给退休以后的生活增加了不少乐趣。

这个办法不但简单，而且很有效。— This method is not only simple, but also very effective.
Seen in 广场舞快递小哥独自旅行

既……又……HSK 4

"Both... and..." links two simultaneous benefits or qualities: 买二手的东西既省钱，又不用等厂家生产新的。

这份工作既有意思，又能锻炼人。— This job is both interesting and good practice.
Seen in 二手市场汉语的模糊之美

不仅…，更…HSK 5

'Not only..., but even more...' — the second clause outweighs the first in importance.

方言不仅是一种交流工具，更是一种身份的象征。 — Dialect is not only a tool for communication, but even more a symbol of identity.
Seen in 方言与身份认同春运语言与思维《三体》现象

不仅…还… (not only… but also…)HSK 5

Adds a second, often more important layer: 传统手艺不仅是一种技术，还是一代又一代人生活的记忆。

他不仅会说汉语，还会写漂亮的汉字。 — He not only speaks Chinese, but also writes beautiful characters.
Seen in 传统手艺的消失谦虚的文化根源

一方面…，另一方面…HSK 5

Presents two sides or aspects of the same topic, like 'on the one hand... on the other hand...', e.g. 学汉语一方面有趣，另一方面也很难.

一方面，电动汽车不污染空气，对环保有很大的好处，另一方面，它的使用成本也比传统汽车低。 — On the one hand, electric cars do not pollute the air, which greatly benefits the environment; on the other hand, they also cost less to run than traditional cars.
Seen in 新能源汽车城市化

与此同时 (at the same time)HSK 5

A formal connector adding a simultaneous event: 与此同时，葡萄、西方的好马等也传入了中国。

他在学习汉语，与此同时，他也在教英语。 — He is studying Chinese; at the same time, he is also teaching English.
Seen in 丝绸之路匠人精神

不仅…，也…HSK 5

'Not only..., but also...' links two related facts to show a broader trend.

不仅中国人对中医重新产生兴趣，许多外国人也开始学习针灸。 — Not only have Chinese people rediscovered interest in Chinese medicine, many foreigners have also started learning acupuncture.
Seen in 中医的现代处境生物多样性

既……，也…… — both … andHSK 6

Joins two qualities or requirements that hold at the same time. Unlike 又……又……, it is more formal and often summarizes an argument.

这份工作既需要耐心，也需要判断力。 — This job calls for both patience and judgment.
Seen in 气候变化与责任集体与个人人类的未来

How to use this page

These 16 patterns all do the same job in Chinese, so they are worth seeing side by side — the differences between them are easier to feel when they are next to each other than when they are spread across six HSK levels. They range from HSK 1–6.

Grammar sticks through reading, not through lists. Each entry links to the reading it was taken from. Skim the page, then go read one of them and come back when a sentence stops making sense.
